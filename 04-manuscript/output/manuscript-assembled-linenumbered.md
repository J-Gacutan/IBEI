1	# Ocean accounts as compilation infrastructure for a blue economy index
2	
3	## Abstract
4	
5	Indonesia is statutorily obliged to score every province annually on a 44-indicator blue economy
6	composite compiled, on its own registration, as sectoral statistics with no primary collection.
7	Indonesia's ocean accounts are assessed here as compilation infrastructure for that index rather
8	than as an alternative indicator source.
9	Here, the analysis demonstrates that what accounting offers such an index is a production
10	process, being a standing classification, a balancing identity, a re-aggregable spatial basis
11	and a documented revision rule, rather than a stock of substitutable values.
12	Reading the register's verbatim constituent data items rather than its indicator names corrects
13	prior assessments: three condition indicators ask for the share of provincial area within a stated
14	cover band, a tabulation of extent by class rather than the index against reference condition they
15	found missing. Supporting that claim, the account types
16	Indonesia has compiled, at one pilot site, carry about 7.6% of the composite's
17	published weight, whilst those bearing on roughly 19% remain methodology under development, both
18	shares contingent on an undocumented ambiguity in how the weights are expressed. What an accounting
19	system can carry for a statutory composite is now a general question.
20	
21	
22	# 1. Introduction
23	
24	Composite indices have become a standard instrument of ocean governance, converting heterogeneous
25	marine statistics into a single number that ministries can target, budget against and report. Their
26	credibility rests less on indicator design than on the production system beneath them, since an index
27	can only be as traceable as the statistics it consumes. That dependency is rarely examined, in part
28	because indices are usually assessed on what they measure rather than on how their inputs are made.
29	
30	Composite indices of ocean condition are themselves an established form, the Ocean Health Index scoring
31	every coastal country against ten goals for a coupled human–ocean system (Halpern et al., 2012), and the
32	methodological difficulties they meet are documented rather than novel, the standard reference treating
33	imputation, weighting and aggregation as the three choices that most move a published ranking
34	(Nardo et al., 2008). The Indonesian case therefore belongs to a recognised class of measurement problem
35	and not to an idiosyncratic one.
36	
37	Ocean accounting has developed as the candidate infrastructure for that problem. The System of
38	Environmental-Economic Accounting Ecosystem Accounting framework, adopted by the UN Statistical
39	Commission in 2021 as an international statistical standard, supplies the extent, condition, service
40	and asset structures on which such accounts are built (United Nations et al., 2024), and a complete
41	national sequence has been specified in detail (IDEEA Group, 2021). Ocean accounts have been argued
42	to belong in the national statistical apparatus rather than in analysis, on the grounds that recurrent
43	policy reporting requires a production system and not a study (Fenichel et al., 2020), and
44	sub-national compilation across extent, service flows and ocean-economy production has been
45	demonstrated operationally (Gacutan et al., 2022). In Indonesia, the spatial basis such accounts
46	require has been tested empirically, with grid size and designation rule shown to change the extent
47	estimate materially (Rahayu et al., 2024). What none of that work establishes is whether an account
48	compiled to those specifications can carry the reporting load of a statutory index, which is a
49	question about production capacity rather than about accounting method.
50	
51	Indonesia is where the policy demand is most explicit. Its ocean policy lineage runs from the 2017
52	Indonesian Ocean Policy through a development framework (BAPPENAS, 2021) to a blue economy roadmap
53	(BAPPENAS, 2024), and a recent review of that lineage names limited data support among the obstacles to
54	implementation (Wuwung et al., 2024). Quantitative work on Indonesian blue economy indicators remains
55	thin, and Rinaldi et al. (2025) construct their own sub-national index for Sumatera on a human
56	development index template and regress it on regional product, which is the closest existing treatment
57	and is not an analysis of the official index. What has not been examined is the production system
58	beneath that index itself.
59	
60	The Indonesia Blue Economy Index (IBEI) is an official national development indicator under
61	presidential regulation Perpres 12/2025, with provincial targets for 2045 assigned by joint circular
62	of the Ministry of Home Affairs and the Ministry of National Development Planning (BAPPENAS) for
63	carriage in long-term development plans. A province cannot decline to be scored, and BAPPENAS cannot
64	decline to score it. Against that recurring obligation, the compilation activity is registered in
65	Indonesia's statistical activity system (SIRUSA kegiatan 94936) as sectoral statistics gathered by
66	secondary collection from the publications of the Ministry of Marine Affairs and Fisheries (MMAF,
67	Indonesian KKP) and of Statistics Indonesia (BPS), so the index conducts no primary collection of its
68	own and inherits whatever those agencies publish, at whatever resolution they publish it. The
69	traceability defects that follow from compiling a 44-indicator provincial composite entirely from
70	secondary publications are legible in the index's own documentation and are documented in Section 2.
71	
72	Here, this study assesses Indonesia's ocean accounts as compilation infrastructure for an index the
73	state is already obliged to produce, rather than as an alternative source of indicator values. The
74	assessment is built on the IBEI's own published documentation, being the 2025 technical handbook
75	(Juknis) and the December 2025 revision of its report, on the primary reports of the Indonesian
76	account pilots, and on the international accounting standard, with every claim tagged for maturity.
77	It contributes a documented account of what makes provincial compilation hard, a three-tier indicator
78	mapping scored against the register's verbatim constituent data items rather than its indicator names,
79	a quantification of where in the index the account-relevant weight sits, and a register of the
80	conditions precedent for continued supply. Two boundaries hold throughout the assessment. Account
81	compilation has its own statutory and analytical purposes under MMAF's mandate, and supplying an index
82	is one downstream use among several, so nothing here proposes replacing the IBEI or subordinating
83	either framework, the relationship examined being statistical infrastructure feeding a policy
84	instrument. The object of study is bounded to the 44-indicator edition of the index, and every
85	Indonesian ocean account output discussed carries maturity tag (b), being experimental or pilot
86	compilation, with no statistical work-programme entry and no revision policy.
87	
88	Three findings follow. Read against verbatim constituent data items rather than indicator names, the
89	three ecosystem condition indicators are definitionally matched to account output rather than
90	category-mismatched, which corrects a judgement in earlier work, including an earlier provisional
91	mapping by the present authors that scored account-derivable shares at figures moving from 61% to 39%
92	to 2.8% as the assumed indicator set changed, the last over a 36-indicator denominator the register
93	does not have (Section 5.2). The account types Indonesia has compiled at pilot scale bear on a small
94	share of the composite's published weight, whilst the types bearing on a share several times larger,
95	being social classification and governance, are the least developed anywhere and are compiled in
96	Indonesia at no maturity above methodology under development (Section 7.3). Neither share describes
97	achievable score improvement, as both describe the share of the index whose production these account
98	types bear on, and what the mature account types offer at those indicators is a production process
99	rather than a stock of substitutable values (Section 9.1).

102	# 2. Why provincial compilation is hard
103	
104	A recurring statutory obligation to score every province annually sits on top of a compilation
105	activity that conducts no primary collection of its own. The obligation is assigned rather than
106	volunteered: Perpres 12/2025 carries the IBEI as an official national development indicator, and
107	joint circular SEB Mendagri/Bappenas No. 2/2024 distributes a 2045 target to each province for
108	carriage in its long-term development plan, with Papua Pegunungan carried at no baseline, achievement
109	or target (Juknis, Tabel 2, p. 69 [pdf 79]). That the compilation beneath it collects nothing
110	directly is a design decision rather than a lapse, it is recorded in Indonesia's own statistical
111	metadata system, and its consequences are legible as specific strains in the published output rather
112	than as commentary. This section establishes both from the IBEI's own documentation, and closes by
113	declaring the pillar structure the rest of this article computes on, since the handbook prints two.
114	
115	## 2.1 Compilation without collection
116	
117	SIRUSA registers the IBEI compilation as *Kompilasi Produk Administrasi* under *Statistik Sektoral*,
118	implemented by BAPPENAS's Directorate of Marine Affairs and Fisheries, with an empty *Identitas
119	Rekomendasi* (kegiatan 94936, retrieved 2026-08-13). The activity design is secondary compilation
120	throughout. Collection mode is *Pengumpulan data sekunder*, the instrument is *Lainnya: Publikasi KKP
121	dan BPS*, the unit is the province, and the register records no pilot survey, no non-response
122	adjustment, no staff training, zero supervisors and zero enumerators. The index's data supply is
123	therefore fixed to whatever KKP and BPS have already published, at the resolution and vintage they
124	published it. The collection capability the index draws on is real and sits with those two producers,
125	which is the statutory division this article's framing holds to: BAPPENAS plans, whilst MMAF and BPS
126	produce statistics. What the register establishes is not an absence of capability in the system but
127	the absence of any collection instrument inside the compilation itself, so the index can acquire
128	nothing that its two suppliers have not already published.
129	
130	The window is narrow and is published from two directions. SIRUSA schedules collection from 1 April to
131	1 May 2025 and dissemination from 1 August to 1 September 2025, whilst the Juknis puts the first phase
132	of its monitoring and evaluation cycle, covering the circular, the timeline, the standard reporting
133	format and contact points, in October to December of year *n* (p. 71 [pdf 81]). Read together these
134	imply that an upstream figure not public by roughly April of year *n+1* cannot enter that cycle's
135	index. They are stated conditionally, as neither document reconciles them and only the first phase was
136	extracted into the corpus `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Reference periods compound the problem in two independent
137	ways. Variables 12 to 14 carry T-2 where the register otherwise carries T-1, so a two-year lag sits
138	inside a single index vintage. Separately, the same reference periods appear twice in the source with
139	different renderings, as T-1 in the numbered variable table and as 2024 in the summary table, so a
140	prospective supplier cannot infer which vintage will be used.

142	## 2.2 What the published output records
143	
144	Two defects carry the argument, and both are failures of provenance rather than of arithmetic.
145	
146	Gaps are filled by prediction, and the prediction is not recorded. Annex 1 of the Report specifies a
147	machine-learning framework predicting missing values from socio-economic proxies under two scenarios,
148	with SUSENAS named as the proxy source (pp. 64–66 [pdf 76–78]). The framework itself is
149	specified in detail, and what is missing is downstream of it: the annex names no imputed indicator, no count of imputed
150	cells, no selected model, no achieved error, and no proxy beyond SUSENAS. Under the first scenario an
151	indicator absent in every province is predicted for every province. No reader of any published table
152	can therefore distinguish a measured provincial value from a predicted one, and which indicators were
153	imputed is unrecoverable from the published record, the annex naming none of them `[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
154	It must not be inferred from which indicators look hard to collect.
155	
156	Three provinces record no value across three sub-pillars. Papua Barat Daya, Papua Selatan and Papua
157	Tengah, three of the four provinces created in Papua in 2022, each record 0.00 for marine-based
158	industry, technology and governance, and Papua Tengah's headline value of 8.51 is the lowest in the
159	annex (Report p. 70 [pdf 82]). Juknis Tabel 2, reproducing the SEB values, carries the same three
160	provinces at 2023 baselines of 49.25, 33.50 and 21.48 against 2025 achievements of 13.16, 12.25 and
161	8.51 (p. 69 [pdf 79]), the two documents heading those columns differently. Whether the zeros reflect
162	data unavailability or a measured zero is not stated in either document, so they are reported as
163	printed and read as consistent with a compilation discontinuity following the 2022 provincial split
164	rather than as proof of one `[ASSUMPTION]`. This is where a new administrative unit needs a spatially
165	re-aggregable source rather than a republished provincial total (Section 3).
166	
167	Three further symptoms are recorded in Table 1 rather than argued here, being an underived rescaling
168	factor printed beneath all four regional tables, sub-pillar values far above the nominal scale on
169	tables headed *Normalized*, and provincial 2045 targets above the national target. Each is
170	uninterrogable from the published record, and none can be resolved without documents this study could
171	not obtain.
172	
173	The denominator of the obligation is itself unstable inside the guidance that creates it. Indonesia
174	has 38 provinces, whilst the Report's Annex 2 tabulates 37, Tabel 2 of the Juknis lists 38 rows plus a
175	national row with Papua Pegunungan carrying a dash in all three columns, and the monitoring chapter
176	asserts comparability over *37 provinsi* (p. 71 [pdf 81]). The number used in any sentence here is the
177	number that sentence is about.

219	# 3. What makes accounts infrastructure
220	
221	Compilation infrastructure, as the term is used here, means a production system that turns primary
222	observation into a statistical output on a repeating cycle, carrying its own spatial unit, provenance
223	chain and revision rule. What makes ocean accounts compilation infrastructure is therefore not the
224	data they contain but four properties of the production process: a standing unit and classification
225	definition, fixed by the account types themselves, a balancing identity that renders an unexplained
226	change a visible residual, a spatial basis re-aggregable to any reporting boundary from one
227	compilation, and documented vintage and revision. The four-property scheme is this study's synthesis
228	rather than a construct the standard names as such, assembled from provisions the standard does state
229	separately, being the account structures and their balancing identity (United Nations et al., 2024,
230	paras 4.14–4.17, pp. 86–88), the treatment of basic spatial units and accounting areas (App. A1.1,
231	para. A1.3), and the consistency and revision requirements (para. 1.60, p. 18). Each is a property of
232	accounting rather than of any particular dataset. Those account types also divide against the register along a line that organises
233	the rest of this article (Table 2), as extent and condition produce quantities the 44 indicators
234	already demand whilst ecosystem services and monetary asset accounts produce quantities appearing
235	nowhere among them.
236	
237	The balancing identity is where the production argument lives. Closing stock equals opening stock plus
238	additions less reductions, which makes an unexplained movement fail to balance rather than pass
239	unnoticed. The extent account adds a further discipline, separating additions into managed and
240	unmanaged expansion and reductions into managed and unmanaged reduction, so that the nature of a
241	change and the policy response available to it are recorded rather than inferred (United Nations
242	et al., 2024, paras 4.14–4.15, p. 86), and so that an addition reported without its concurrent
243	reduction states gross planting where a reader would infer net gain. The contrast with the index is one of production
244	rather than arithmetic, as its standardisation and principal-component weighting are specified in
245	detail and what is absent is any step at which an inconsistency would be forced to surface (Section
246	2). The measured quantity differs too. An account reports a stock in physical units against a stated
247	reference, whilst SIRUSA records that a lower index value, including a negative one, indicates
248	performance lagging relative to other provinces (SIRUSA, indikator 105371, Interpretasi, retrieved
249	2026-08-13). A hectare figure is comparable across years without recomputing every other province, and
250	a weighted standardised score is not.
251	
252	The spatial property needs stating more carefully than it usually is. Basic spatial units retain their
253	place in SEEA Ecosystem Accounting but are "regarded as a means of implementing the ecosystem
254	accounting approach rather than as elements in a nested hierarchy" (SEEA EA 2021, App. A1.1, para.
255	A1.3), the ecosystem asset being the statistical unit and the reporting geography a compiler-delineated
256	accounting area. Provincial and district figures are accordingly two aggregations of one production
257	run rather than two data requests, but only where the grid, the minimum mapping unit, the landward and
258	seaward boundaries and the vintage are documented and held constant. That is a conditional property of
259	a well-specified account rather than an inherent property of accounts, and Rahayu et al. (2024)
260	measure what getting it wrong costs (Section 5.2). Whether cell boundaries nest cleanly within
261	provincial, fisheries-management and marine-spatial-planning frames is asserted rather than evidenced
262	`[ASSUMPTION]`, and no adopted national grid standard has been located `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Both are
263	conditions precedent (Section 6).
264	
265	Provenance is the property that speaks to traceability. An account is compiled to be consistent over
266	time and with respect to concepts and classifications (United Nations et al., 2024, para. 1.60,
267	p. 18), which requires the mapping
268	inputs, their vintage and their spatial resolution to be documented and held constant, so that patchy
269	coverage becomes reportable rather than concealable and a measured value stays distinguishable from a
270	modelled one after aggregation, where the index publishes no microdata and no per-indicator imputation
271	record (Section 2). Revision is likewise a property of the account form, the standard requiring
272	previous estimates to be revised where updated imagery, methods or classifications permit a
273	reassessment, with no distinct revision entry recorded and the size of the revision available as the
274	difference between the historical and the revised account for the same period (para. 4.17, p. 88).
275	
276	A reviewer may object that a well-documented statistical register could supply all four properties
277	without any account being compiled, and on two of the four the objection holds outright. A standing
278	unit and classification definition is an act of codification, requiring a published definition of the
279	mapping unit, the ecosystem classes and the landward and seaward limits, and nothing about compiling
280	an account is needed to write one down. Documented vintage and provenance is likewise a metadata
281	requirement that any well-run statistical register discharges, and the spatial property is
282	intermediate, since a register can mandate a grid and a minimum mapping unit but cannot by itself
283	produce the re-aggregable cell-level tabulation the mandate presupposes. What does not reduce to
284	codification is the residual: the balancing identity, which forces an unexplained movement to appear
285	as a failure to balance rather than as a revised level, and the revision rule that alters the opening
286	and closing entries themselves rather than adding a separate revision line, so that the size of a
287	revision is recoverable by difference. Those two are properties of a compiled account because they are
288	properties of double-entry over a stock, not statements about a stock. The residual is deliberately
289	narrow, and the article's claim is correspondingly narrow, that the near-term contribution of accounts
290	at the indicators they touch is that residual and the production cycle carrying it, rather than a
291	volume of otherwise unavailable values (Section 9.1). Nothing here asserts an Indonesian capability.

307	# 4. What Indonesia has already compiled
308	
309	Indonesian ocean accounting has produced a compiled record small enough to inventory item by item,
310	carrying a maturity tag on every claim, where the tag describes the datum rather than the document
311	reporting it. Two findings follow, and they pull against each other. That accounting has produced the
312	measurement basis IBEI indicators 19, 20 and 21 are defined over, being ecosystem area on a spatial
313	grid with cover measured against documented reference levels, whilst it has not produced the form the
314	index specifies, which is the share of area falling in a cover class rather than a mean cover value.
315	It has produced that basis once, in one marine protected area, in one province, and the properties an
316	annually reported official index requires of a supplier are not yet evidenced in the obtained record.
317	
318	## 4.1 The compiled record
319	
320	The compiled record comprises one pilot in a marine protected area, being Gili Matra Marine
321	Recreational Park off north Lombok, together with one peer-reviewed grid-optimisation study at Saleh
322	Bay, Sumbawa (Rahayu et al., 2024). Both sit in West Nusa Tenggara, so the single province carrying a
323	documented published compilation located by this study is NTB. A national Ocean Accounts Dashboard was
324	launched in July 2025 and is described by GOAP as publicly available and interactive on a harmonised
325	spatial frame (GOAP country page, retrieved 2026-08-13), a programme self-statement about a platform
326	rather than a statistical release, and one that evidences no compilation beyond the pilot record
327	below.
328	
329	The pilot's footprint belongs in its own units, as the smallness is part of the finding: 2,980.84 ha
330	on a 25 m by 25 m marine basic spatial unit (MBSU) grid of 48,211 cells, opening in 2015 and closing
331	in 2021, those years chosen on data availability (MMAF, 2022, pp. 30, 39 [pdf 44, 53]; Wulandari and
332	Jannah, 2023, slide 11). The two figures do not reconcile arithmetically, as 48,211 cells at 625 m2
333	each is 3,013.19 ha against the 2,980.84 ha printed, a shortfall of 32.35 ha or 1.1% that would be
334	consistent with cells clipped at the park boundary but is nowhere explained in the report, and is
335	recorded here as observed rather than resolved `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Against a statutory obligation to score every province annually (Section 2),
336	that is an area smaller than many single districts, compiled once.
337	
338	Maturity is the programme's own self-description rather than an external verdict. GOAP labels the
339	three 2022 MMAF outputs 'preliminary consultation drafts', and BPS lists scaling up to the total
340	Indonesian marine area among its outstanding challenges (Tasriah et al., 2022, slide 9). Every
341	Indonesian ocean account output discussed here therefore carries maturity tag (b), experimental or
342	pilot compilation. There is no BPS ocean account release, no entry in the national statistical work
343	programme, no published revision policy, and no published compilation cost per site or per unit, which
344	constrains what may responsibly be recommended (Sections 6 and 10). The ecosystem scope is confined to
345	mangrove, seagrass and coral reef.
346	
347	## 4.2 One maturity, three completenesses
348	
349	The claim that Indonesia has ocean accounts decomposes into three claims of unequal strength. All
350	three account types carry the same maturity tag (b), and what separates them is completeness of the
351	account form rather than the standing of the compilation producing it.
352	
353	Extent is the only account type with a verified opening, change and closing series. The pilot
354	publishes a complete asset account (MMAF, 2022, tab. 5, p. 52 [pdf 66]) in which coral falls from
355	259.50 ha to 247.50 ha, seagrass rises from 76.75 ha to 102.50 ha and mangrove falls from 21.50 ha to
356	10.69 ha, with additions and reductions itemised as natural expansion, natural regression and managed
357	regression, and with an ecosystem-conversion matrix at tab. 6 (p. 53 [pdf 67]). This is the stock and
358	flow identity in Indonesian practice rather than asserted from the SEEA Ecosystem Accounting standard.
359	
360	Extent is also where two official series diverge. A BPS presentation reports the same pilot,
361	ecosystems and years throughout with coral falling from 183.34 ha to 165.08 ha, seagrass rising from
362	56.50 ha to 64.93 ha and mangrove falling from 13.53 ha to 6.15 ha (Tasriah et al., 2022, slide 3).
363	Both series trace to one layer, being the Geospatial Information Agency's 2020 mapping validated by
364	2021 field survey, which MMAF overlaid on the MBSU grid with 194 verification points (pp. 23, 30 [pdf
365	37, 44]) and which BPS cites as BIG (2020), validated 2021. Neither document states its relationship
366	to the other and no reconciliation is published, so the discrepancy stands as an open conflict between
367	two official documents, neither averaged nor adjudicated here `[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
368	
369	The divergence is not a constant offset, which is the property that matters for an account. Taking the
370	ratio of the BPS figure to the MMAF figure ecosystem by ecosystem and year by year gives coral 0.707
371	in 2015 against 0.667 in 2021, seagrass 0.736 against 0.633 and mangrove 0.629 against 0.575, so the
372	ratio moves both across ecosystems and across the accounting window and cannot be explained as a
373	single boundary clip, a units convention or a fixed classification threshold. The consequence is that
374	the two series disagree on the change and not merely on the level, MMAF recording a coral loss of
375	12.00 ha over the window against a BPS loss of 18.26 ha. Change is the quantity a balancing identity
376	exists to report, so the one compiled Indonesian account has produced two closing stocks and two
377	deltas for the same site, and that fact is carried into the limitations and interrogated against this
378	article's own provenance claim (Sections 8.5 and 9.1).
379	
380	Condition is compiled in a limited form, being cover-based measurement at Tier 1 to Tier 2 read
381	against documented reference bands rather than a normalised multi-indicator composite. Coral was
382	measured by underwater photo transect with CPCe analysis following Muttaqin et al. (2020), seagrass by
383	the Seagrass Watch protocol and mangrove by plot sampling (MMAF, 2022, pp. 31–33 [pdf 45–47]). Values
384	are classified against published reference levels, with 16.99% live hard coral cover in 2021 read
385	against the bands of Giyanto et al. (2017), and mangrove classified as good at a density of at least
386	1,500 individuals per hectare under Ministry of Environment Decree 201/2004 (pp. 40, 47 [pdf 54, 61]).
387	That the pilot measures cover as a value against a documented reference level, rather than scoring it
388	as a composite, is both a limit of the account and what leaves the register's class-tabulation form
389	within reach (Section 5.2).
390	
391	Two facts are recorded here without adjudication. The pilot reports mean cover by zone rather than
392	share of area by class, so producing indicators 19, 20 and 21 from it would require re-tabulating the
393	underlying transect and grid data to a different class boundary `[ASSUMPTION]`. And 16.99% cover is
394	reported, then classified as moderate against a band running from 19% to 35%, a tension recorded as
395	observed and unexplained.
396	
397	Valuation is the least developed of the three and may not be described as an ecosystem services
398	account in physical units. What exists is
399	benefit-transferred monetary valuation of ecosystem functions at pilot scale rather than supply and
400	use of ecosystem services in physical units. Coral values are transferred from Bohol (Samonte-Tan et
401	al., 2007) and the Mariana Islands (van Beukering et al., 2006), seagrass from Mexico, and mangrove
402	from Gazi Bay, Kenya (Hoberg, 2011) and Bintuni Bay, Indonesia (Ruitenbeek, 1992), giving pilot totals
403	rising from IDR 45.24 bn per year in 2015 to IDR 64.17 bn per year in 2021 (pp. 36, 60 [pdf 50, 74]).
404	Benefit transfer is not the exchange-value basis the SEEA Ecosystem Accounting standard requires, so
405	no exchange-value or depreciation claim here can rest on it, a limit BPS states itself in listing the
406	absence of implemented standard valuation methods among its challenges (Tasriah et al., 2022, slide
407	9). On this evidence, no ecosystem services account in physical units has been compiled in Indonesia.
408	
409	## 4.3 Institutional and spatial standing
410	
411	A national index would require of an account supplier a standing custodian carrying a periodicity and
412	a revision policy, together with an adopted spatial standard. Both are unsettled on the primary
413	record, and both on the programme's own instruments rather than by inference from silence.
414	
415	Kepmen KP 44/2023, which constituted the Tim Penyusun Neraca Sumber Daya Laut on 16 February 2023,
416	cuts both ways. It establishes five working groups and names five accounts, covering ecosystem extent
417	and condition with monetary value, flows to the economy, waste flows, the ocean economy and
418	governance, and it places the BPS Director of Production Accounts in the chair of environmental
419	accounts implementation (Pokja C), with BAPPENAS a single member of the governance working group
420	(Pokja D) rather than a co-lead. Its own diktum KEEMPAT set the team's term to expire on 31 December
421	2023, and no renewal instrument was located. The July 2025 dashboard launch indicates continued
422	activity under some instrument not obtained, so the defensible statement is that no renewal instrument
423	was located rather than that the mandate lapsed `[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
424	
425	Rahayu et al. (2024), the only peer-reviewed Indonesian account source, state that a standardised grid
426	has yet to be established. Grid choice changes the extent tabulation itself, which makes an adopted
427	grid a substantive prerequisite for supply rather than a technical detail (Section 6).
428	
429	One bounded search result is reported on what this study read rather than on an exhaustive survey. The
430	searched set was assembled between 11 and 16 August 2026 and comprises the MMAF 2022 series, the BPS
431	materials prepared for the SEEA workshops, Rahayu et al. (2024), Kepmen KP 44/2023, the GOAP country
432	page (retrieved 2026-08-13) and the SIRUSA metadata for the IBEI compilation activity (kegiatan 94936
433	and indikator 105371, both retrieved 2026-08-13), searched bilingually on the Indonesian terms *neraca
434	laut*, *akun laut*, *neraca ekosistem*, *unit spasial dasar laut*, *peta jalan ekonomi biru* and *Satu
435	Data Indonesia* together with their English equivalents, with no date restriction applied. Within that
436	set, no Indonesian statistical register supplying provenance, spatial unit, vintage and revision
437	policy for marine ecosystem extent and condition products, separately from an account structure, was
438	encountered `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. No dedicated search of the BPS statistical catalogue or of the Satu Data
439	Indonesia portal was undertaken, and no engagement with any Indonesian agency was sought, so the
440	statement is bounded to the named set and is not a claim about the Indonesian statistical system.
441	
442	Three properties remain unestablished by this inventory: national coverage, an annual cycle capable of
443	meeting the index's T-1 reporting window, and a documented custodial chain. What the record supports
444	is narrower than a claim that supply exists, being a method demonstrated in one province at pilot
445	scale, on one vintage, with cover-based condition and benefit-transferred money.

450	# 5. Mapping: the compiled accounts
451	
452	Of the register's 44 rows, five are the points at which an ocean account touches the IBEI at all.
453	Three of them, indicators 19, 20 and 21, name quantities that an account's output structure already
454	contains, whilst indicators 24 and 27 sit adjacent to account output and are disposed of here rather
455	than claimed. Which rows qualify is established by a membership test applied to the verbatim
456	constituent data items of the register, not to indicator names, indicator names having proved an
457	unreliable guide in earlier assessments.
458	
459	## 5.1 What Tier A means
460	
461	The unit of analysis is the register row, of which there are 44, and the object tested is not the row's
462	title but the *Data Pembentuk Indikator* printed against it, the constituent data items that specify
463	what must be measured. Across the 44 rows those items decompose into 62 distinct constituent data
464	items, some rows naming one and others several (Juknis, Tabel 1, pp. 11–13 [pdf 21–23], this study's
465	count over the register as printed). The test is applied to all 62 in Indonesian, and grades each row
466	on one of three grounds. A row is *matched* where a constituent data item names a quantity that a
467	defined SEEA Ecosystem Accounting account output contains, which yields indicators 19, 20 and 21,
468	n = 3. A row is *partial* where account output supplies a component of the quantity but not the
469	quantity itself, which yields indicator 24, n = 1. A row is a *provenance question* where account
470	output bears on the row without supplying it, which yields indicator 27, n = 1. Tier A is the union of
471	the three grades and has five members out of 44. Every other row is scored none, on the ground that no
472	constituent data item in it names a quantity any account output contains.
473	
474	Membership is therefore a claim about correspondence between two published specifications, being the
475	index's register and the SEEA Ecosystem Accounting output structure, and it carries no claim about
476	compilation state. The distinction matters because a label such as 'can serve now' collapses the two
477	axes, asserting a production capability from a definitional property.
478	
479	On the second axis the concession is complete. Every Indonesian ocean account output carries maturity
480	tag (b) (Section 4.1), and none has published any of the five indicator values, for any province or
481	any site `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Every capability claim here is accordingly a claim about definitional
482	correspondence and a demonstrated input layer, never about an existing output. The tier is named on
483	the first axis and conceded on the second at each use: Tier A, definitionally matched, compilation
484	demonstrated at pilot scale. All five rows sit in the environment pillar under both pillar loci, so
485	the unresolved conflict does not reach this section's arithmetic.
486	
487	What the test yields is a correction of the literature rather than a capability claim. Earlier
488	assessments judged that Indonesia lacks the condition account indicators 19, 20 and 21 require. Those
489	indicators do not require one, so the judgement answers a question the index does not pose, and the
490	barrier at these rows is compilation and geographic coverage rather than measurement target.
491	
492	## 5.2 The cover-class match, and its frays
493	
494	The three condition indicators share one grammatical form. Indicator 19 asks for the *Persentase
495	kawasan terumbu karang dengan tutupan karang hidup antara 50-100% per provinsi*, indicator 20 for the
496	*Persentase kawasan lamun dengan tutupan lamun hidup antara 50-100% per provinsi*, and indicator 21
497	for the *Persentase kawasan mangrove dengan kerapatan tutupan antara 50-100% per provinsi* (Juknis,
498	Tabel 1, p. 12 [pdf 22]). Each names a percentage of area falling within a stated cover or density
499	band, per province, which is a tabulation of extent by class rather than a normalised index against a
500	reference condition.
501	
502	What the pilot sets beside that specification is the input layer and not the tabulation. The MBSU
503	extent surface exists (Section 4.1), and cover is measured at survey stations, giving live coral cover
504	of 18.11% in 2016 and live hard coral cover of 16.99% in 2021, which the report does not state to be
505	the same measure and which are therefore not read here as a series, and seagrass cover averaging
506	32.28% with a station maximum of 82.01% (MMAF, 2022, pp. 40, 45 [pdf 54, 59]). The 2021 coral figure is
507	unreconciled in the source, where the narrative reports 16.99% whilst the report's own tab. 3 on the same page prints
508	an average of 18.36%. The cross-tabulation of extent by cover class is not published, and the step
509	from the input layer to indicator 19 is one tabulation that has not been performed.
510	
511	The match frays at four edges, and the first two are definitional. The 50% threshold is the index's
512	own and matches no Indonesian condition class boundary in use, as the pilot classifies reef condition
513	on the bands of Giyanto et al. (2017), where moderate runs from 19% to below 35% live hard coral.
514	Reclassifying a continuous per-cell cover surface at 50% would be arithmetically trivial, but that
515	holds only where condition is mapped wall to wall per grid cell rather than surveyed at stations, and
516	no obtained document evidences a wall-to-wall condition surface `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Where national
517	condition data exist only as classes pre-binned on the Giyanto boundaries, the 50% cut cannot be
518	recovered, and the coral row becomes a definition gap rather than a coverage gap `[ASSUMPTION]`. The
519	mangrove unit differs outright: indicator 21 asks for canopy density as a percentage, whilst the pilot
520	reports mangrove canopy cover in m² per ha and stem density in individuals per ha, with no documented
521	conversion between them (p. 47 [pdf 61]) `[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
522	
523	The second two edges are unfixed choices. Neither the minimum mapping unit nor the denominator is
524	fixed by the register or by the SIRUSA definitions, so whether the percentage is taken over mapped
525	ecosystem extent or over total provincial marine area remains a definitional residue `[ASSUMPTION]`.
526	And the extent estimate is itself a function of the grid rule, as Rahayu et al. (2024) report mangrove
527	error of 44.06% under a 25 m hierarchy scheme against 1.59% under 25 m dominance. Until a grid rule is
528	adopted, the account's figure is a family of figures, and the index would be importing a
529	methodological choice.
530	
531	A vintage gap sits alongside the definitional ones, as the only verified account series closes in 2021
532	against the index's T-1 requirement and its unsettled rendering (Section 2.1).
533	
534	Restated after those concessions, the correction narrows and holds. The measurement task reduces from
535	constructing reference-condition norms for three ecosystems in every province to classifying cover per
536	cell against a single threshold, which is a smaller object to build, and the wall-to-wall condition
537	surface it needs remains unbuilt. Where these three values are currently predicted under the
538	imputation framework the Report publishes (pp. 64–66 [pdf 76–78]), supplying them from an account
539	would replace a prediction with a measurement, though which indicators were imputed, for which
540	provinces, is not recoverable `[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
541	
542	## 5.3 An addition term and a designation
543	
544	Indicator 24 asks for the *Total luas kawasan mangrove, rawa, dan lahan gambut yang direstorasi per
545	provinsi*, glossed in SIRUSA as degraded area subsequently replanted, which is an activity input
546	measured in hectares planted. The pilot's asset account carries a managed-expansion line in its
547	structure that is unpopulated at Gili Matra, where every addition is natural expansion and the only
548	managed entry is 11.19 ha of managed mangrove regression (MMAF, 2022, tab. 5, p. 52 [pdf 66]). The
549	correspondence at this row is therefore structural rather than demonstrated: an account of this form
550	would record realised managed change, whilst the indicator counts hectares planted. The scope
551	mismatch compounds it, as the indicator's three land covers are mangrove, swamp and peatland against
552	the marine account's mangrove, seagrass and coral reef, so even complete national coverage would leave
553	indicator 24 partly unserved.
554	
555	The pilot's seagrass row illustrates what the distinction buys. Of the 51.50 ha gained, 8.25 ha came
556	from former reef and 43.25 ha from other substrate, against 25.75 ha lost to coral and to other
557	substrate. A hectares-planted count cannot see either side of that ledger, whilst the account's change
558	matrix records which is which.
559	
560	Indicator 27 asks for the *Total luas kawasan konservasi perairan yang ditetapkan oleh KKP, KLHK, dan
561	Pemerintah Daerah per provinsi*, and is in the tier for a different reason again. At 22.0% it is the
562	largest single variable in the resource-quality sub-pillar, about 4.06% of the composite, and it needs
563	no account to be produced: designated area is an administrative datum already held by its custodians
564	and broken out in SIRUSA as nationally managed, regionally managed and KLHK-managed areas. The
565	account's near-term contribution here is reconciliation, being the attachment of mapped ecosystem
566	extent to the designated polygon as the pilot does by zone, and not supply.
567	
568	## 5.4 How the gaps sort
569	
570	Sorted by the kind of gap that remains, the tier splits three ways (Table 3). Indicators 19 and 20 are
571	coverage and vintage rows on sound definitions, implying a compilation programme beyond the pilot
572	sites and inside the index's reporting window (Section 6). Indicators 21 and 24 are
573	definition rows no amount of coverage will close, the first on units and the second on the difference
574	between an activity and an outcome, implying threshold, unit and denominator rules agreed with the
575	index's custodian. Indicator 27 is not a gap but a provenance question. A fourth gap kind, the spatial
576	standard, is cross-cutting rather than attaching to any row, and implies an adopted national grid and
577	clipping rule. That sort, and not the tier's total weight, is what tells a compiler what to do next.
578	
579	The weight arithmetic is shown openly as a multiplication, carried to two decimal places from the
580	one-decimal percentages the handbook prints and reported in prose to one decimal with 'about', a
581	convention held throughout the article. The environment pillar carries 36.9% of the composite and the
582	resource-quality sub-pillar 50.0% of the pillar (Juknis, Tabel 4 and Tabel 5, p. 94 [pdf 104]), giving
583	18.45% of the composite, of which the five Tier A variables sum to about 7.64% (Table 3). That total
584	is tagged `[CONTESTED DENOMINATOR]` throughout this article, and the tag names one specific objection
585	rather than the pillar conflict, which does not reach this arithmetic: 7.64% is a defensible measure of
586	the share of the index these account types bear on, and an indefensible measure of the share an
587	account would supply, because most of it sits at rows the mapping has itself disqualified from supply.
588	The decomposition is therefore reported in three parts, as the tier's own sort requires: about 2.33%
589	at the three matched condition rows, about 1.25% at indicator 24 as a partial, and about 4.06% at
590	indicator 27, which the account does not supply. The share at which an account is the prospective
591	supply route is accordingly about 2.33%, rising to about 3.58% only if indicator 24's structural
592	correspondence is credited in full, which the activity-against-outcome mismatch and the swamp and
593	peatland scope gap indicate it should not be (Section 5.3). The lower figure is the operative one and
594	the upper an outer bound. Whether the printed percentages are raw first principal component loadings
595	or normalised contributions is not stated, so these products are indicative rather than exact
596	`[ASSUMPTION]`, and the Tinggi, Sedang and Rendah bands are the handbook's own rank-based
597	simplification. None of these figures travels alone, as the account types bearing on the larger shares
598	of the index, being social classification and governance, are the least developed anywhere, and are
599	priced in Section 7.3.
600	
601	Under the published weights that share is small, so substituting account-derived values would be
602	unlikely to move the composite materially, though weights derived by principal components are
603	re-estimated from the data, so a substitution is not weight-invariant.
604	
605	Neither reading is the finding. What changes at these rows is how the numbers are made. A cover-class
606	percentage compiled from a gridded extent surface and a classified cover layer carries its own spatial
607	unit, its own opening and closing stocks, and a provenance chain back to the imagery and field survey
608	behind it, and it is reproducible in the next cycle by repeating the compilation. A value taken from a
609	secondary publication, or predicted where no publication exists, carries none of those properties, and
610	the published record does not distinguish the two (Section 2). That is a production-process claim
611	about five rows, not a claim about the index's score. Nothing here proposes that accounts be compiled
612	in order to serve the index, and nothing moves an indicator out of its custodian's hands, on which the
613	register is silent in a way worth recording: no producing agency is stated for any of the 44 rows
614	`[EVIDENCE GAP]`.

632	# 6. Conditions precedent for supply
633	
634	A definitional match is not a supply arrangement. Five conditions must hold before pilot account output
635	could serve the IBEI as a repeating input rather than a single demonstration, none is satisfied in the
636	obtained record, and they are not equally binding (Table 4). Three are one-off acts of codification
637	that a single decision by MMAF, BPS or BAPPENAS could discharge. Two are discharged only by sustaining
638	a production cycle, and those two are what decide whether the mapping in Section 5 is a recommendation
639	or a wish. The register would be refuted by producing an adopted national marine spatial standard, a
640	BPS statistical work-programme entry for a marine ecosystem account, or an extent compilation covering
641	more than the pilot sites and closing after 2021. Each condition is read against the accounting
642	standard where the standard speaks to it, and is otherwise this study's own specification, which is
643	stated as such at each row.
644	
645	## 6.1 The test: a dated window
646	
647	The obligation is recurring and annual, set by Perpres 12/2025 and SEB 2/2024 and registered at annual
648	frequency in SIRUSA, and the monitoring architecture fixes its first phase at October to December of
649	year *n* (Section 2.1). It can receive only what an upstream producer has already released, at that
650	producer's resolution and vintage. The operative test for a prospective supplier is therefore repeatability into a dated window rather than
651	demonstrated capability. Two of the system's own principles are supply-side conditions in disguise,
652	being temporal continuity and a standardisation guaranteeing that provinces aggregate on comparable
653	terms, and a standardised reporting format cannot in any case be issued without stating which of the
654	handbook's two pillar structures it follows (Section 2.3).
655	
656	## 6.2 Spatial conditions
657	
658	Provincial account output is not comparable across provinces or across vintages until one minimum
659	mapping unit and one basic spatial unit are adopted nationally. Both spatial conditions are acts of
660	codification, discharged once and requiring no new compilation. The standard requires an account to be
661	consistent over time and with respect to concepts and classifications (United Nations et al., 2024,
662	para. 1.60), and requires previous estimates to be revised for continuity of the time series wherever
663	updated data, methods or classifications permit a reassessment of area (para. 4.17). Grid-based
664	spatial units are the mechanism by which an account aggregates to any administrative boundary
665	(Section 3), conditional on the grid existing and being national. No adopted national marine grid or minimum mapping unit was found in any
666	obtained Indonesian document, having searched the set stated at Table 4 `[EVIDENCE GAP]`, whilst the
667	pilot literature records different
668	resolutions at different sites, the case the rule exists to prevent.
669	
670	The second spatial condition is a codified clipping rule. Provincial marine authority extends to 12
671	nautical miles under Law 23/2014, cited here at second hand and not read at source `[EVIDENCE GAP]`,
672	so an account reaching further must be clipped before it can yield a provincial number. The standard
673	leaves the accounting area to the compiler (para. 2.12) and supplies no sub-national allocation rule
674	for an asset spanning a boundary, so the convention this study takes as necessary, being allocation by
675	physical extent within each side with condition compiled per spatial unit rather than inherited whole,
676	is a specification rather than a citation `[ASSUMPTION]`, and neither it nor the statutory line is
677	codified in any account obtained by this study `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. One consequence follows from the
678	standard directly. Condition is not additive across areas, its variable account being an area-weighted
679	arithmetic mean over the assets of an ecosystem type (para. 5.54, p. 105), so because indicators 19, 20
680	and 21 ask for a ratio, a provincial value cannot be recovered by averaging district values, and a
681	supplier would need to publish the denominator alongside the percentage for a provincial value to be
682	recoverable.
683	Clean nesting of cell boundaries across administrative, fisheries-management and marine-spatial-planning
684	frames is asserted rather than evidenced `[ASSUMPTION]`, and forms part of this condition rather than a
685	property that may be assumed.
686	
687	## 6.3 Temporal conditions
688	
689	The reference period is the binding constraint on the mapped indicators, and it binds on repetition
690	rather than on speed. SIRUSA specifies T-1 for all five, being variables 19, 20, 21, 24 and 27, the
691	same five matched definitionally in Section 5. The single verified Indonesian compilation closes at
692	2021 and was published the following year, a lag the T-1 requirement can accommodate. What
693	is absent is the second vintage. One compilation, however timely, cannot meet a T-1 requirement in any subsequent
694	cycle, and no repeat compilation of the pilot sites was found in any obtained document
695	`[EVIDENCE GAP]`. This condition is therefore recurring rather than one-off, discharged by running the
696	cycle again and not by a decision, and it survives the index's own inconsistent rendering of its
697	reference periods (Section 2.1) `[ASSUMPTION]`. Sustaining a cycle is a resourcing question, and no
698	compilation cost was located in the public record, so this study cannot price any condition in the
699	register.
700	
701	A revision policy is the second recurring condition. The standard already supplies its substance,
702	requiring revision of previous estimates for continuity of the series, recording no distinct revision
703	entry but altering the opening, closing, addition and reduction entries themselves, and allowing the
704	size of a revision to be shown as the difference between the historical and the revised account for
705	the same period (paras 4.17–4.18, p. 88). What is unwritten is the Indonesian instrument that would
706	designate a baseline year and disclose where the latest extent data refer to an earlier period, and
707	the index's own principle of controlled adaptability presupposes that discipline on the supply side.
708	No revision policy was found for any Indonesian ocean account output in the searched set
709	`[EVIDENCE GAP]`. The consequence is specific: an account silently improved between vintages would
710	move a province's index value with no ecosystem change behind it, and nothing in the published record
711	would flag that. The instrument requires no new compilation, being a statement of when a series is
712	restated and how a provisional estimate is marked.
713	
714	## 6.4 The institutional condition
715	
716	Account output cannot become a durable input to an official index whilst it remains an unregistered
717	ministry product feeding an activity itself registered only as sectoral statistics. The IBEI is
718	recorded in SIRUSA as *Kompilasi Produk Administrasi* under *Statistik Sektoral* with an empty
719	*Identitas Rekomendasi* (kegiatan 94936), whilst the Juknis names BAPPENAS as *pengampu* of the
720	calculation (p. 68 [pdf 78]). Indonesian ocean account output is an MMAF product constituted under
721	Kepmen KP 44/2023 (Section 4.3), and no BPS statistical work-programme entry appears in any document
722	obtained by this study, no dedicated search of the BPS statistical catalogue having been undertaken
723	`[EVIDENCE GAP]`. The condition is unmet against an instrument whose own term has run, that Kepmen
724	having set the team's expiry at 31 December 2023 with no renewal instrument located (Section 4.3), so
725	a supply arrangement would be settled by whichever body now holds the coordinating function rather
726	than by the standing arrangement the record documents. Two sectoral products feeding one another does not by itself
727	constitute an accredited statistical input. The condition is a documented supply arrangement
728	between MMAF as producer, BPS as statistical authority and BAPPENAS as compiler, whose minimum content
729	is stated here as this study's own specification rather than taken from any standard, being scope,
730	frequency, quality standards, confidentiality, format and dispute resolution `[ASSUMPTION]`. Written
731	that way it is a short document rather than a programme. That
732	accreditation would be the route is inferred from the index's own registration rather than stated
733	anywhere, so it is put as something BPS and MMAF would have to settle `[ASSUMPTION]`. The gap runs in both directions, since no
734	microdata are released (Section 3), so even a compliant supplier could not verify that its value
735	survived into the published provincial score. This condition, like the two spatial ones, is discharged
736	once, and all three are conditions the index would need met rather than obligations the accounts
737	programme owes it.
738	
739	## 6.5 Why coverage must lead insertion
740	
741	One design recommendation follows from the conditions. Where a province has no account, an account-derived indicator would itself be predicted from
742	socio-economic proxies under the framework the Report publishes (pp. 64–66 [pdf 76–78]), so an
743	environmental value would become partly a function of economic and social covariates. The framework's
744	first scenario trains on the previous cycle's historical data, which a newly introduced indicator does

832

1000	# 8. What account supply does not fix
1001	
1002	## 8.1 Extending published limitations
1003	
1004	The index's custodian has printed its own methodological limits, and account supply reaches none of
1005	them. Four are conceded in the index's own methods statement (Report Section 3.1.1, p. 34 [pdf 46]),
1006	being an assumption of linear and monotonic relationships, whose worked example is that the benefit of
1007	fishing intensity holds below a threshold whilst damage rises exponentially above it, sensitivity to
1008	outliers, weights set on 2024 data that *mungkin saja menjadi tidak relevan untuk tahun-tahun
1009	mendatang* should the structure or coverage of the blue economy change fundamentally, and a single
1010	weight structure applied across a geographically and socio-economically heterogeneous archipelago. Every one is a property of the
1011	estimator, whilst an account changes the input. The first is worth dwelling on, since the threshold
1012	relationship BAPPENAS describes is exactly what an ecosystem account measures well, and better inputs
1013	cannot reach a relationship the estimator represents linearly by design, which is the limitation
1014	already printed. The remedies BAPPENAS proposes are compatible with account supply, and the first of
1015	them, complementary sectoral analysis reported alongside the index, is compatible in turn with the
1016	combined presentations the accounting standard describes, which summarise data from several accounts
1017	beside related economic and social data without requiring that ecosystem services be valued in
1018	monetary terms (United Nations et al., 2024, paras 14.56–14.57, pp. 356–357). What follows is therefore the limits register
1019	any supplier of inputs must accept, rather than a critique of the index.
1020	
1021	## 8.2 What aggregation discards
1022	
1023	The index consumes a scalar. The constituent data items for indicators 19, 20 and 21 each end *per
1024	provinsi* (Juknis, Tabel 1, p. 12 [pdf 22]), so an account compiled on a fine spatial grid discards its
1025	within-province distribution at the moment it becomes an indicator. The loss is asymmetric in value, because the account can tell a provincial
1026	planner which reef complex is driving a change whilst the index can report only that a percentage
1027	moved. That loss is not a necessary property of accounts but a consequence of publishing the indicator
1028	without the grid `[ASSUMPTION]`, so the remedy is an architecture in which both are released, which is
1029	a publication recommendation rather than a supply one (Section 9).
1030	
1031	The provincial boundary is an administrative line and not an ecological one (Section 6.2), and the
1032	standard is explicit that an accounting area may be a country, a subnational administrative area, a
1033	catchment or a protected area (United Nations et al., 2024, para. 2.12), which leaves the choice of
1034	boundary to the compiler and its ecological consequences to the account. A reef complex straddling
1035	that line
1036	is divided between two accounting areas by legal artefact. Accounts make the division explicit and
1037	auditable, which is worth having, and they do not make it ecologically meaningful, which is why the
1038	clipping rule is a condition precedent rather than a solved problem (Section 6.2). One further limit is
1039	inherited rather than created, since in a province without a compilation an account-derived indicator
1040	would itself be predicted from socio-economic proxies (Section 6.5).
1041	
1042	## 8.3 Why net extent change cannot enter
1043	
1044	The index states its own weighting rule: an indicator with greater variability and strong correlation
1045	to others in its domain *akan secara alami mendapatkan bobot yang lebih tinggi* (p. 29 [pdf 41]). A
1046	signed annual difference of a large, slowly changing stock is low-variance by construction and can
1047	change sign between cycles, so it would attract a small and unstable weight and might meet the rule
1048	printed in the sub-pillar analysis loop as *Reversi nilai indikator (atau drop indikator) jika
1049	ditemukan relasi negatif* (p. 30 [pdf 42]), where the negative relation is to the other indicators in
1050	the same sub-pillar. Whether a sign-changing series would trigger it is not specified in the Report and
1051	is read here as an inference. The mechanism is not peculiar to this index, as the standard reference on
1052	composite construction holds that under principal components weighting corrects for overlapping
1053	information rather than measuring importance, and that where indicators are not correlated weights
1054	cannot be estimated by the method at all (Nardo et al., 2008, §6.1) — a variable that is both
1055	low-variance and liable to correlate negatively with its neighbours is therefore poorly served by
1056	loading-derived weights on the method's own terms. Net extent change belongs
1057	beside the composite as a reported companion series in a combined presentation, not inside it as a
1058	forty-fifth indicator (Table 5).
1059	
1060	## 8.4 What no account should supply
1061	
1062	Half the environment pillar measures something no ocean account should be built to measure. The
1063	renewable-energy sub-pillar carries 50.0% of the pillar (Juknis, Tabel 5, p. 94 [pdf 104]) and contains
1064	no marine variable, being solar and hydropower capacity and generation throughout (Section 7.2).
1065	Offshore energy accounting is a forward recommendation, and the point here is narrower: no existing
1066	account can supply what that sub-pillar currently asks for, and building one to do so would be an
1067	instrument error. The article's coverage claim stops at that boundary, and stopping is what makes it
1068	credible. Everything the mapping and the forward register recommend rests on tag (b) pilot compilation,
1069	and the case for it is a production-process case rather than a weight case.
1070	
1071	## 8.5 Limitations of this study
1072	
1073	Eight limitations follow. The first bears on the argument itself and the rest on specific figures, and
1074	they are ordered accordingly rather than by the sequence in which they arose.
1075	
1076	The first is the counter-instance this article's own evidence supplies to its own positive claim. The
1077	only compiled Indonesian account has produced two official extent series for the same pilot, the same
1078	three ecosystems and the same two years, and they disagree not by a constant offset but by a ratio
1079	that moves across ecosystems and across the accounting window, so the two documents disagree on the
1080	change as well as on the level, MMAF recording a coral loss of 12.00 ha against a BPS loss of 18.26 ha
1081	(Section 4.2). The pilot's own reporting compounds it, the narrative giving live hard coral cover of
1082	16.99% for 2021 against 18.36% in the report's table on the same page (Section 5.2). Change is the
1083	quantity a balancing identity exists to report, and provenance is what this article claims accounts
1084	buy, so a divergence of this kind is the strongest available evidence against that claim and is
1085	interrogated rather than set aside (Section 9.1). No reconciliation is published and none is attempted
1086	here `[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
1087	
1088	Second, the printed aggregation formulae are illegible in the PDF text layer at Juknis pp. 64–65 and
1089	Report pp. 31–32. They are recorded here from their variable definitions, which are unambiguous about
1090	Z-score inputs and first-principal-component loading weights, so what is reconstructed is the notation,
1091	and the exact summation form at medium-high confidence rather than the method. Third, whether Tabel 4
1092	to Tabel 6's percentages are raw loadings or normalised contributions is not stated anywhere, and every
1093	derived figure in this article inherits that, including 7.64% `[CONTESTED DENOMINATOR]`, 13.7%, 5.15%
1094	and 2.33% `[ASSUMPTION]`. The ambiguity is bounded rather than open, because the test is to renormalise
1095	each sub-pillar's printed variable weights to sum to 100 and recompute. In the resource-quality
1096	sub-pillar the nine printed weights already sum to exactly 100.0, so renormalisation leaves 18.45%,
1097	7.64%, 3.58% and 2.33% unchanged to the precision the handbook prints, and the residual uncertainty is
1098	the half-unit rounding of one-decimal inputs, which moves 7.64% by less than 0.1 percentage points. In
1099	the economy pillar the five printed sub-pillar weights sum to 96.0 rather than 100, the residual being
1100	the technology sub-pillar, which is not printed and is recovered here as 4.11%. Renormalising over the
1101	five printed weights alone would instead give about 27.2% for the four Tier B sub-pillars and about
1102	5.37% for governance, and Gambar 11's independently printed integers of 26 and 5 favour the reading
1103	adopted here, so the alternative is reported as an upper bound of about one percentage point rather
1104	than carried. Fourth, the per-indicator imputation record is
1105	unpublished, so which indicators were predicted, for which provinces, by which model and at what error
1106	are all unknown, which is the largest single unknown among the figure-level limitations because it
1107	determines how much of any published provincial value is measurement and how much is prediction.
1108	Fifth, two items sit off the document: Annex 3's per-province analysis is behind a short link, and the
1109	normalised factor of 0.6819 is printed under four provincial tables without formula or derivation
1110	(Table 1).
1111	
1112	Sixth, this study engaged no Indonesian agency, by project constraint, so the limitations above are
1113	conditions to live with rather than future work, and that distinction matters for how a reader should
1114	weigh them. Seventh, the mapping is an assessment against stated criteria rather than an independently
1115	verifiable quantity, which is why the score and the exclusion ground for each of the 44 register rows
1116	are deposited in full rather than summarised, so that a reader may reject a row and recompute. Eighth,
1117	compilation cost is absent from the public record at every site, so the conditions precedent are not
1118	costed and could not be.

1121	# 9. Discussion: gains from compilation
1122	
1123	## 9.1 What Tier A compilation changes
1124	
1125	Three results carry the argument. Examining the production system beneath the index found defects that
1126	are properties of provenance rather than of arithmetic, being an imputation framework whose
1127	per-indicator application is unpublished, a rescaling factor printed without derivation, and two
1128	official extent series for the one compiled Indonesian account that cannot be reconciled (Sections 2
1129	and 4.2). Reading the register's verbatim constituent data items rather than its indicator names
1130	established that three ecosystem condition indicators are definitionally matched to account output
1131	rather than category-mismatched, that five of the 44 rows are the whole of what existing accounts
1132	touch, and that the account types bearing on the larger share of the index are the least developed
1133	(Sections 5 and 7). What follows from those is a production-process case and not a weight case, and it
1134	is narrower than an efficiency claim, which the evidence assembled here would not support.
1135	
1136	The register decomposes into 62 distinct constituent data
1137	items across its 44 indicators (Section 5.1), of which the resource-quality sub-pillar accounts for 9
1138	items across 9 indicators, and the five mapped indicators for 5 items. Three of those five, being indicators 19, 20
1139	and 21, are cross-classifications of one extent-and-condition compilation, whilst indicator 24
1140	corresponds to the account's structure without being produced by it and indicator 27 is a designation
1141	total already held by its custodians rather than measured by an account (Section 5.3). The
1142	consolidation is therefore three items into one production run, out of 62. That is a small share of the
1143	register, and the argument is not that the count is large. It is that those three are among the items
1144	the index presently has no way to show it measured rather than predicted.
1145	
1146	The count is a surrogate for what a reader would want, being the number of separate source publications
1147	a compiler must assemble, which the published record cannot yield, since no register row states a
1148	producing agency and the only producer statement sits at activity level, naming KKP and BPS
1149	collectively (SIRUSA kegiatan 94936).
1150	
1151	What compilation adds is provenance of a specific kind. An account carries an opening stock, additions,
1152	reductions and a closing stock over a defined spatial unit, which allows a provincial figure to be
1153	revised on improved information and defended afterwards, where the published output offers an underived
1154	rescaling factor beneath its regional tables (Table 1).
1155	
1156	That claim has to be tested against the strongest counter-instance in this article's own evidence, and
1157	the counter-instance is the account itself. Two official series for the Gili Matra pilot disagree at
1158	the level and, because the ratio between them moves by ecosystem and by year, on the change as well,
1159	so the one compiled Indonesian account has produced two closing stocks and two deltas for the same
1160	site and the same window (Sections 4.2 and 8.5). Read one way this refutes the provenance claim
1161	outright. Read against what the two documents actually are, it qualifies it in a specific place. The
1162	two series are two parallel releases of the same underlying mapping layer, published in a presentation
1163	and a report respectively, and neither is an account of the other, so what the record demonstrates is
1164	that publishing account-shaped tables is not the same as running an account. The property being
1165	claimed is not that compilation prevents divergence, which nothing in the standard promises, but that
1166	a balancing identity gives divergence somewhere to appear, the opening stock, the additions, the
1167	reductions and the closing stock being four entries that must reconcile and that a second compilation
1168	must restate rather than replace. The Gili Matra case is evidence for that reading in a negative form,
1169	as it is precisely because neither document was compiled under such an identity, and because no
1170	revision rule required either to restate the other, that a 6.26 ha disagreement about coral change
1171	could be published twice and reconciled nowhere. The reading is an argument from the account form
1172	rather than a demonstration, since no Indonesian compilation has yet been revised under such a rule
1173	and the claim therefore remains untested in Indonesia `[ASSUMPTION]`.
1174	
1175	The objection that a well-documented statistical register could supply the same properties without any
1176	account (Section 3) bears directly on that argument, and it is conceded in part. Standing unit and
1177	classification definitions and documented vintage are acts of codification that a register discharges
1178	unaided, and three of the five conditions precedent are codification rather than compilation
1179	(Section 6). What does not reduce to a register is the residual named earlier, being the balancing
1180	identity and the revision rule that alters the entries themselves. The residual is thin, and saying so
1181	is more useful than defending a wider claim: for the three matched indicators, most of what an account
1182	would contribute in the near term could be contributed by a published classification standard, and
1183	what could not is the part that makes a change defensible rather than merely stated. A reader who
1184	concludes that Indonesia should codify definitions before compiling anything has read the evidence
1185	correctly, and that ordering is what Section 6.5 recommends.
1186	
1187	A gain in
1188	imputation is stated conditionally rather than claimed, as no baseline exists against which to measure
1189	one (Section 2), so what can be claimed is that measured values for the three consolidated items would
1190	be distinguishable from predicted ones.
1191	Introducing an account-derived indicator would in any case relocate imputation rather than remove it,
1192	which is why insertion should follow coverage (Section 6.5). That the distinction is worth publishing
1193	is established outside Indonesia. The Ocean Health Index, a composite of comparable construction in the
1194	same domain (Halpern et al., 2012), reports that 18.5% of its global score rests on gapfilled rather
1195	than observed data, ranging from 1% to 43% across its ten goals and exceeding 60% for ten regions that
1196	are predominantly small island territories, and its authors recommend that gapfilling be tracked from
1197	the outset and presented in the same framework as the scores themselves (Frazier et al., 2016). The
1198	IBEI publishes an imputation framework and no per-indicator record, so the comparison identifies a
1199	reporting reform rather than a data programme, and one the index could adopt without any account being
1200	compiled.
1201	
1202	Everything downstream of the input is untouched. Account supply changes the value entering a slot, not
1203	the slot, its weight or the aggregation rule, so the three-stage principal component procedure, the
1204	loading-factor weights, the pillar shares and every limitation the index publishes about itself stand
1205	exactly as they are (Section 8.1). What follows for the composite's stability is conditional on a
1206	reading the handbook does not settle. If Tabel 4 to Tabel 6's percentages are loading-normalised
1207	contributions, the mature route bears on about 7.64% of the composite `[CONTESTED DENOMINATOR]`,
1208	against roughly 18.9% resting on account types compiled nowhere, and inserting account-derived values
1209	at those rows would perturb the composite little, which cuts against the fear of destabilisation and
1210	against any claim of a transformed environmental signal in equal measure. If they are raw loadings,
1211	the share is not calculable from the published record and no statement about perturbation is available
1212	in either direction. The renormalisation test bounds the difference between the two readings at under
1213	a percentage point wherever it can be run (Section 8.5), and the conditional is stated rather than
1214	collapsed because the test cannot be run on the pillar weights themselves. The
1215	design findings this study reports are likewise unaffected by accounts and not repaired by them.
1216	
1217	Two literatures bear on that conditional, and neither favours reading the printed percentages as
1218	importance. The handbook that defines the method holds that under principal components weighting
1219	"intervenes only to correct for overlapping information between two or more correlated indicators and
1220	is not a measure of the theoretical importance of the associated indicator", and that under linear
1221	aggregation weights are trade-offs rather than importance coefficients (Nardo et al., 2008, §6.1 and
1222	p. 31 n. 4). Paruolo et al. (2013) then measure what each variable actually contributes to five
1223	composites, the human development index among them, and find that because socio-economic variables are
1224	heteroscedastic and correlated, relative nominal weights hardly ever match relative effective
1225	importance. The IBEI is a composite over correlated provincial socio-economic variables with weights
1226	taken from first-component loadings, which is the configuration in which that divergence was found. The
1227	consequence here is specific and is not evaded, as every share this article reports, the 7.64%, the
1228	18.9% and the 26.1% alike, is a nominal share of printed weight rather than a measure of effective
1229	influence, and the inversion is a comparison of nominal shares. Becker et al. (2017) supply the test
1230	that would settle it, being a correlation-ratio measure of each variable's importance with confidence
1231	intervals, and it cannot be run here because it requires the provincial variable matrix beneath the
1232	index, which is unpublished `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. What survives the objection is the production-process
1233	case, which never rested on the shares, and what does not survive unqualified is any reading of the
1234	inversion as a statement about influence on a provincial score.
1235	
1236	## 9.2 Loose coupling as architecture
1237	
1238	Where substitution is not warranted, and on the published maturity evidence it is not, the arrangement
1239	that delivers the gain is loose coupling. It has three requirements, being (1) shared indicator definitions at the
1240	cover-class thresholds the Juknis already prints, so an account can tabulate to the index's own
1241	definition, with the unit and denominator rules the register leaves open settled with its custodian
1242	(Section 5.4), (2) a shared spatial unit that aggregates to the province, and (3)
1243	parallel publication of the account alongside the index. It is defined as much by what it does not
1244	require, being no institutional transfer, no change to the index's data pipeline, and no dependency of
1245	a statutory index on an experimental compilation.
1246	
1247	Both halves have a route in the standard. Parallel publication is a combined presentation
1248	(United Nations et al., 2024, paras 14.56–14.57) and is the nearer term of the two, being a
1249	presentational choice rather than a data gap, whilst sub-national compilation follows from the
1250	accounting area being definable at a subnational administrative boundary (para. 2.12) and has been
1251	demonstrated at that scale elsewhere (Gacutan et al., 2022), though nowhere in Indonesia beyond the
1252	pilot (Section 4.1). Timing governs adoption either way (Section 6.1), and loose coupling has the
1253	property that a compilation cycle missing the window fails without the index failing with it.
1254	
1255	The institutional division holds at the point of recommendation, where it is most at risk. BAPPENAS is
1256	*pengampu* of the calculation whilst the constituent data are provincial (Juknis, p. 68 [pdf 78]), so
1257	nothing here asks either institution to take on the other's mandate. The arrangement would also have
1258	to be settled by whichever body now holds the coordinating function, the team constituted under Kepmen
1259	KP 44/2023 having had its term expire on 31 December 2023 with no renewal instrument located
1260	(Sections 4.3 and 6.4), so an architecture requiring inter-agency coordination is being proposed
1261	against a documentary record in which the standing coordination instrument has run its term.
1262	Substitution becomes arguable when the conditions precedent are met (Section 6), and not before.
1263	
1264	The evidence ceiling on all of this is one marine protected area, in one province, at one vintage,
1265	carrying maturity tag (b) throughout (Section 4.1), and the architecture above is therefore a
1266	specification of what would have to be true rather than a generalisation from a demonstrated national
1267	capability. Two readings of the same record would lead elsewhere. Rinaldi et al. (2025) respond to the
1268	weakness of Indonesian blue economy measurement by constructing an alternative index on a human
1269	development index template, which treats the problem as one of index design rather than of the
1270	production system beneath it, and on that reading the effort recommended here would be misdirected.
1271	Wuwung et al. (2024) locate the constraint on Indonesian ocean policy in institutional fragmentation
1272	across mandates, which would make an inter-agency supply arrangement the hardest rather than the
1273	cheapest of the conditions precedent, against the ordering proposed here. A third reading questions the
1274	target rather than the method. Ravallion (2012) defines a mashup index as a composite for which
1275	existing theory and practice provides little or no guidance for its design, so that the producer is
1276	essentially free to set an unusually large number of moving parts, and concludes that doubts remain
1277	about the value added of such indices and their policy relevance relative to the dashboard alternative
1278	of monitoring the components separately. On that reading the effort specified here is aimed at the
1279	wrong object, and an account should be published alongside the components rather than routed into an
1280	aggregate. The answer this record supports is statutory rather than methodological, as the IBEI is
1281	scored for every province annually under Perpres 12/2025 (Section 1), so the dashboard alternative is
1282	not in BAPPENAS's gift and the question of what supplies the composite is live whether or not an
1283	aggregate is the instrument a statistician would have chosen. That is a reason to attend to the inputs
1284	of this index, not a defence of aggregation in general. No source located by this study argues that
1285	accounts cannot serve as statistical infrastructure for a composite index, which is the narrower
1286	proposition advanced here.

1290	
1291	## 10.1 The single take-home
1292	
1293	The single take-home is that ocean accounts are worth building for this index because of how the
1294	handful of indicators they touch would be produced, and not because of how many indicators they could
1295	fill, the share an account would itself supply being small on any reading of the published weights and
1296	resting in part on a denominator the source documents do not settle (Section 9.1). The cost argument
1297	below, the two follow-on studies and the statutory boundary are supporting rather than co-equal, and
1298	each is presented as such.
1299	
1300	## 10.2 Compilation cost, unpublished
1301	
1302	No compilation cost appears anywhere in the record obtained for this study. There is no staff-day
1303	figure, no budget line, no imagery cost and no field-survey cost for any Indonesian ocean account, at
1304	any site `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. That absence, rather than any methodological obstacle in the conditions
1305	precedent, is the binding constraint on acting on this study's recommendation, because a
1306	province-by-province annual obligation is a scope decision before it is a method decision. This study
1307	declines to estimate a cost, since an order-of-magnitude range would be read as a figure. The only
1308	signal in the obtained record is computational rather than financial, in the grid-cell counts a single
1309	bay generates at two resolutions (Rahayu et al., 2024), and processing burden for one bay is not a
1310	compilation cost for a province.
1311	
1312	## 10.3 Follow-on studies, and one risk
1313	
1314	The claim that indicators 19, 20 and 21 are definitionally matched to account output, with the barrier
1315	lying in compilation and geographic coverage rather than in measurement target, is empirically testable
1316	today at exactly one site. Re-tabulating the Gili Matra compilation to the index's own 50% to 100% cut
1317	would settle whether an account's extent and cover layers yield the register's class-share form at all,
1318	and would generate the first real compilation-cost observation. Its limits are set by the record
1319	itself, since the coral and seagrass cover behind that compilation was surveyed at stations rather than
1320	mapped per cell (Section 5.2), the mangrove row is blocked on an undocumented unit conversion, and a
1321	footprint of 2,980.84 ha supports no comparison against a published provincial value. Extending the
1322	same tabulation to NTB as a whole is the step that would test provincial resolution, and it requires
1323	the province-wide compilation that does not exist. Neither settles national coverage, the absent
1324	spatial standard or the absent revision policy, and both carry an unknown on the index's side, since
1325	whether the published values were themselves predicted is not recoverable.
1326	
1327	The second is a compilation-guidance output rather than a research one. No published guidance sets out
1328	how a sub-national marine ecosystem account should be compiled to serve a provincial indicator in an
1329	Indonesian setting, and the material in Sections 2, 4 and 5 here is much of what such a document would
1330	need. The sensible division is that the article supplies the analytical basis and the guidance document
1331	the practitioner-facing output, each citing the other rather than duplicating it.
1332	
1333	Both assume continued external technical assistance to the Indonesian programme. That is an assumption
1334	rather than an established condition, no funding or programme-continuity document having been obtained
1335	by this study `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. The cost study is the one item that becomes more valuable if such
1336	assistance lapses, since it is what would let the decision be taken domestically.
1337	
1338	What would falsify the argument is worth stating, because a paper of this shape is otherwise
1339	structurally unfalsifiable. If a single-province compilation reproduces the published provincial value
1340	no better than the current secondary-publication route, or does so at a cost per province that an
1341	annual national cycle cannot bear, then the production-process case fails on its own terms.
1342	
1343	Within the statutory boundary held throughout (Section 1), what this study describes is a capacity gap
1344	under a statutory obligation, in a country that has demonstrated, at one experimental pilot site, one
1345	instance of the production process the obligation would require at scale.

1348	# Methods
1349	
1350	**Design.** The study is a documentary analysis of two published specifications read against each
1351	other: the Indonesia Blue Economy Index register as printed by its custodian, and the account
1352	structures of the SEEA Ecosystem Accounting standard together with the specified sequence of national
1353	ocean accounts. No sampling, no statistical inference and no hypothesis test is involved, and no
1354	result reported here is an estimate with a sampling distribution. The equivalent burden is
1355	reproducibility of a scoring rule and of an arithmetic chain, which the deposited materials discharge.
1356	
1357	**Corpus.** A citation-anchored corpus of 199 chunks was built over the index's own published
1358	documentation and released with this article, so that every quotation and every figure in the analysis
1359	resolves to a numbered record rather than to a page range. Extraction was purposive rather than
1360	exhaustive: the passages carrying the register, the weights, the calculation method, the sensitivity
1361	analysis, the imputation framework, the provincial output and the cascading obligation were sliced from
1362	each source at stated printed-page ranges, and nothing outside those ranges was chunked. The build,
1363	dated 2026-08-14, comprises 106 chunks from the Juknis (BAPPENAS 2025a) over pp. 11–13, 63–66, 67–71,
1364	93–96 and 101–104, 49 from the IBEI Report 2024 in its December 2025 revision (BAPPENAS 2025b) over
1365	pp. 28–35, 64–66 and 67–70, 15 from Rinaldi et al. (2025) and 29 from the SIRUSA metadata for the
1366	compilation activity and its indicator record (kegiatan 94936 and indikator 105371, both retrieved
1367	2026-08-13). Each record carries a chunk identifier, its document and slice, the verbatim Indonesian
1368	text, an English drafting gloss used nowhere as a quotation source, the printed page with the PDF page
1369	in brackets, a content type, a maturity tag and a flag marking whether any part of the record was
1370	reconstructed rather than read directly, which 17 of the 199 records carry. Chunks in conflict with one
1371	another are recorded as conflicts rather than reconciled, and the four-against-three pillar contradiction
1372	is held as one such record.
1373	
1374	Account-side evidence sits outside that corpus and was read directly: the three MMAF 2022 reports
1375	including the Gili Matra pilot study, the BPS materials prepared for the SEEA workshops, Rahayu et al.
1376	(2024), Kepmen KP 44/2023 and the GOAP country page (retrieved 2026-08-13). All source documents carry
1377	clean text layers and no optical character recognition was applied anywhere, so the two passages
1378	recorded as illegible (Section 8.5) are defects of the published files rather than of extraction.
1379	Retrieval and assembly ran from 11 to 16 August 2026. Alongside the corpus, a join table reproduces all
1380	44 register rows with each row's *Data Pembentuk Indikator* constituent data items verbatim,
1381	transcribed from Juknis Tabel 1, pp. 11–13 [pdf 21–23], and totalling 62 constituent data items. That
1382	join table, and not the running text of the handbook, is the object the membership rule was applied to,
1383	which is what makes the scoring reproducible from the deposit alone.
1384	
1385	**Search protocol.** Searching was bilingual and was run over the document set named above rather than
1386	over a statistical catalogue. The Indonesian terms used were *indeks ekonomi biru*, *neraca laut*,
1387	*akun laut*, *neraca ekosistem*, *unit spasial dasar laut*, *peta jalan ekonomi biru* and *Satu Data
1388	Indonesia*, together with their English equivalents, with no date restriction. No dedicated search of
1389	the BPS statistical catalogue or of the Satu Data Indonesia portal was undertaken, and by project
1390	constraint no Indonesian agency was approached for data, interview or correspondence. Every absence
1391	claim in the article is therefore stated as unfound within the named set and is not a claim about the
1392	Indonesian statistical system.
1393	
1394	**Unit of analysis and the membership rule.** The unit is the register row, of which there are 44, and
1395	the object tested is the row's constituent data items rather than its title, on the Indonesian wording.
1396	Each row was graded on one of three grounds: *matched*, where a constituent data item names a quantity
1397	that a defined SEEA Ecosystem Accounting output contains, *partial*, where account output supplies a
1398	component of the quantity but not the quantity itself, and *provenance question*, where account output
1399	bears on the row without supplying it. Tier A is the union of the three grades and has five members,
1400	being indicators 19, 20 and 21 matched, 24 partial and 27 a provenance question. All remaining 39 rows
1401	are graded none. The grade and the ground for every row, with the constituent data items reproduced,
1402	are deposited as Supplementary Table S1 so that a reader may reject a row and recompute. The rule was
1403	applied item by item rather than row by row, so a row carrying several constituent data items takes the
1404	highest grade any one of its items attains, which is why the 62 items and not the 44 rows are the
1405	tested population. Two decisions were fixed before scoring and held throughout: that indicator titles
1406	are not evidence of what a row requires, titles having produced the predecessor figures the analysis
1407	rejects, and that a definitional match carries no claim about whether anything has been compiled, the
1408	two axes being reported separately at every use. Grading was performed by the authors against those
1409	criteria, not blind, with no second coder and no adjudication protocol, which bounds what the exercise
1410	can claim and is recorded among the limitations (Section 8.5). Replication of the scoring therefore
1411	requires the deposited join table, the rule above and the SEEA Ecosystem Accounting output structures,
1412	and requires nothing this study holds privately.
1413	
1414	**The repeatability test.** Membership in Tier A is a definitional property and carries no claim about
1415	compilation. The separate operative test applied to the account side is repeatability into a dated
1416	window rather than demonstrated capability, since the obligation is annual and can receive only what an
1417	upstream producer has already released at the required vintage (Section 6.1). Maturity is tagged on
1418	every claim that something exists, as (a) published official statistics, (b) experimental or pilot
1419	compilation, (c) methodology under development, or (d) announced but not started, and the tag describes
1420	the datum rather than the document reporting it.
1421	
1422	**Weight arithmetic.** Weights are taken from the Juknis as printed: pillar weights from Tabel 4, p. 94
1423	[pdf 104], the environment pillar's sub-pillar split from Tabel 5, p. 94 [pdf 104], variable weights
1424	from Tabel 6, pp. 95–96 [pdf 105–106], and the sub-pillar shares of Gambar 11, p. 93 [pdf 103], used as
1425	an independent integer cross-check. Shares of the composite are products of those published
1426	percentages, carried to two decimal places from one-decimal inputs and reported in prose to one decimal
1427	with 'about'. The technology sub-pillar's weight is not printed and is recovered as the residual of the
1428	economy pillar, at 4.11%. All weight arithmetic follows the three-pillar structure of Tabel 4, Tabel 5
1429	and Gambar 11, on the ground that weights are published only there, whilst register rows keep their
1430	Tabel 1 labels. The two structures disagree over indicators 15 to 18, and figures computed over the
1431	pillars across which those rows move are identified as structure-dependent at each use (Section 2.3).
1432	
1433	**Sensitivity to the weight ambiguity.** The handbook does not state whether the percentages in Tabel 4
1434	to Tabel 6 are raw first-principal-component loadings or normalised contributions. The test applied is
1435	to renormalise each sub-pillar's printed variable weights to sum to 100 and recompute. In the
1436	resource-quality sub-pillar the nine printed weights already sum to exactly 100.0, so the renormalised
1437	and printed readings coincide and 18.45%, 7.64%, 3.58% and 2.33% are unchanged to the precision
1438	printed, with a residual half-unit rounding uncertainty moving 7.64% by under 0.1 percentage points. In
1439	the economy pillar the five printed sub-pillar weights sum to 96.0, and renormalising over those five
1440	alone would give about 27.2% for the four Tier B sub-pillars and about 5.37% for governance against the
1441	26.1% and 5.15% adopted here, an upper bound of roughly one percentage point which Gambar 11's printed
1442	integers of 26 and 5 do not support. The test cannot be run on the pillar weights themselves, which
