# Section 1 brief — Introduction

**Faithful extract only.** This file restates the section-1 plan and the cross-cutting rules that bear
on it. The SOT skeleton and [PRD-journal-article.md](../PRD-journal-article.md) remain authoritative;
where this brief and either of those differ, they govern.

**Allocation:** 900 words ±20% (720–1,080), PRD §5.
**Focus (PRD §5):** statutory obligation (Perpres 12/2025, SEB 2/2024); 44 indicators × 38 provinces
annually; the compilation-capacity gap; the thesis (accounts as compilation infrastructure); the
contribution list; the explicit statement that account compilation has independent purposes.

## Subsections

- **1.1 A statutory index without a compilation system.** The obligation is legal and recurring, not
  analytical: Perpres 12/2025 (RPJMN 2025–2029) makes the IBEI an official national development
  indicator; SEB Mendagri/Bappenas No. 2/2024 assigns provincial 2045 targets carried in the RPJPD.
  Against that, SIRUSA registers the compilation as sectoral statistics, annual, cross-sectional,
  secondary compilation from KKP and BPS publications, no pilot survey, zero supervisors, zero
  enumerators, descriptive analysis, province-level, T-1 throughout.
- **1.2 What the strain looks like in the published output.** Four documented symptoms, all from
  BAPPENAS's own documents: published ML imputation with no per-indicator record; three new Papuan
  provinces at 0.00 across three sub-pillars with Papua Tengah at 8.51; an unexplained 0.6819
  normalisation factor and out-of-scale sub-pillar and target values; a four-versus-three-pillar
  conflict inside one handbook. Diagnosis is capacity, not competence.
- **1.3 Thesis, contributions, and what is not being claimed.** Accounts as compilation infrastructure
  — one compilation serving several indicators, provenance and repeatability, the province's own
  spatial unit. Leverage inversion stated in the introduction: ~7.6% mature / ~30.5% social + ~5%
  governance emerging. Independent-purposes statement and the BAPPENAS-plans / MMAF-and-BPS-produce
  division stated here and held.
