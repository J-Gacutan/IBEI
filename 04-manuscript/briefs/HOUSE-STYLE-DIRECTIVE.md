# House style directive: binding on every section draft and every review agent

**Authority.** [Gacutan_Style_Guide.md](/Users/z5238824/Documents/GitHub/7.%20Report%20converter/Gacutan_Style_Guide.md),
adopted 2026-08-16 and applied throughout the manuscript. Read that file before drafting. This
directive is the enforcement checklist for the loop, not a substitute for it. Where the style guide
and the SOT conflict on *mechanics*, the style guide governs. Where they conflict on *evidence or
claim scope*, the SOT and the standing rules govern.

## Hard prohibitions, testable by string search

| Test | Rule |
|---|---|
| `—` `–` in prose | None. Use a colon, a comma, or parentheses. En-dash permitted only in citation and reference ranges (`pp. 31–33`, `2015–2021` in a reference entry) |
| `;` in prose | None. Split the sentence, or use `, and` / `, whilst` / `, whereby`. Permitted only inside parenthetical citation clusters |
| `**` in prose | No bold or underline for emphasis, and no bold run-in labels mid-paragraph. Bold is for headings and table captions only |
| `§` | Never. Spell out "Section 4.2", "Table 2", "Figure 1" |
| "we", "I", "our" | None. Use "this study", "the analysis", or a passive construction |
| bullets in the body | None. Inline (1), (2), (3) for sequential items in running prose |
| "shows", "proves" | Use demonstrates, suggests, indicates, is consistent with |
| "while", "however" | Prefer "whilst" for substantive contrasts |
| -ize / -ization | British and Australian spelling throughout: -ise, -isation |

## Cross-references: parenthetical only

**A section, figure or table is never the topic of a sentence.** Cross-references are parenthetical
and mid-sentence, and carry information about where a fact is established: `(Table 2)`,
`(see Section 5.2)`. Barred outright:

- Vacuous handoffs. "Section 6 takes this up", "the next section examines", "as discussed above",
  "what Section 4 has not established". A section's own substance is its bridge to the next.
- A closing rhetorical question that hands the reader onward.
- "Figure 1 shows that X." Write the claim, then cite: "X (Figure 1)."

## Structural tells to hunt on revision (style guide Section 8)

Balanced antithesis and the "not merely X but Y" mould · anthropomorphised methods (an account does
not "see" or "know"; it records, omits, has no entry for) · colloquial idiom, including
"double-edged sword", "comes into play", "paints a picture" · dangling demonstratives opening a
paragraph · inversion or cleft for emphasis · the "does so" pro-verb and repeated "[X] side"
scaffolding · adverb-plus-strained-verb · decorative doublets, reflexive tricolons, the
claim-colon-list rhythm repeated · asides wedged between subject and verb · trailing participials
("…, revealing…") · "and"-chains over three clauses · uniform sentence length.

## What the voice keeps

Density carries the argument. The long multi-clause sentence is the default, and short sentences are
deliberate exceptions at a pivot. Hedge to the real strength of the evidence (*may*, *could*,
*might*). Map prior work before claiming novelty. Keep the load-bearing technical vocabulary exact:
ecosystem asset, extent, condition, exchange value, benefit transfer, stock and flow, supply and use,
disaggregation, crosswalk, operationalise, co-development, ocean accounting (OA). Signature
connectors: Further, · In parallel, · In contrast, · Whilst · Here, · As such, · contingent on ·
coherent · framed · underpinning · nexus. Single quotes mark a constructed or contested term.

## Flags in draft text

Three inline flags are carried in square brackets in the drafts and stripped at submission:
`[EVIDENCE GAP]`, `[ASSUMPTION]`, and, added 2026-08-16 by user ruling, **`[CONTESTED DENOMINATOR]`**.

The third attaches to the mature-route share of **7.64%** and to anything derived from it, including
the inversion of 7.64% against 18.9% at roughly 2.5x. The D1 convention stands and 7.64% remains the
operative figure. The flag records the objection rather than resolving it: of those 7.64 points, about
4.06 sit at indicator 27, which this article states needs no account to be produced, and about 1.25 at
indicator 24, conceded partly unserved, leaving about 3.58% as the share at which an account is the
prospective supply route and about 2.33% where the definitional match is clean. Carry the flag at
first use in each section that states the figure, and report the decomposition at least once wherever
the figure carries argumentative weight.

## Allocation

Per-section allocations from the SOT carry **±10%** (user ruling, 2026-08-16), superseding the ±20%
tolerance in standing rules Section 9.0. Reconciliation remains a single global pass. Where a section
cannot carry its obligations inside ±10%, the overage is reported in a drafting note, never absorbed
by cutting a protected concession, a maturity qualification or a flag.
