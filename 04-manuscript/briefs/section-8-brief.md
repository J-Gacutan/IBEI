## 1. The section plan (verbatim from the skeleton)

**Purpose.** Bound the article's own recommendation by naming, before a reviewer does, the four
things account supply does not fix — and to do so as an extension of BAPPENAS's already-published
limitations rather than as an exposure of them.

**Core claim.** Ocean account supply improves the *provenance* of a small, identified set of IBEI
inputs; it does not repair the index's spatial resolution, its statutory boundary, its
variance-driven weighting, or the roughly one third of the environment pillar that measures things
no ocean account should be built to measure — and two of these four (variance weighting, regional
heterogeneity) are limitations BAPPENAS itself printed in Report §3.1.1.

**Subsections.** 8.1 Extending BAPPENAS's own limitations · 8.2 What provincial aggregation and the
12-nm clip discard · 8.3 Why a signed net-extent-change series cannot simply be inserted · 8.4 What
no account should be asked to supply.

**Logical connection.** Follows §7, which has just made the article's most expansive claim (the ~35%
aspirational route). §8 is the brake. It ends by asking what *is* changed, which §9 answers
operationally.

## 8. Framing boundary

Statistical infrastructure feeding a policy instrument. BAPPENAS plans; MMAF and BPS produce
statistics. §8 **extends** BAPPENAS's published caveats; it does not expose them, and it is not a
fault-finding exercise. Where §8 says "no account should be built to measure this", the point is
about instrument choice, not about the index being wrong to measure it. Do not propose replacing the
index, subordinating either framework, or treating index supply as the purpose of account
compilation.
