# Section 9 brief — Discussion: streamlining, not substituting

**Allocation:** 800 words ±20% (640–960). **Draft after §8** (PRD §12: "§8 written before §9").

## PRD focus, verbatim (PRD §5 row 9)

> What changes operationally if Tier A is compiled: five data requests become one production run;
> provincial figures become reproducible and revisable; district figures for the RPJPD cascade come
> free; the imputation load falls for the indicators covered. What does not change: the weights, the
> aggregation, the index's own limits. Loose coupling — shared definitions, shared spatial units,
> parallel publication — as the interim architecture where substitution is not yet warranted.

## The hostile-review correction this section must implement

"Streamline" was asserted without evidence. §9 reclassifies it as a **structural** claim — a claim
about how many separately sourced constituent data items a compiler must assemble and reconcile —
not an empirical claim about cost, staff time or elapsed days, none of which exist in the public
record. The one quantification the record permits is the constituent-data-item count from the join
table, and it must be presented with its own limitation attached (see next heading).

## The quantification, and why it is a surrogate

**Producer is stated for no indicator.** All 44 join-table rows carry `stated_producer: not_stated`;
Juknis Tabel 1 prints no producing-agency column. The only producer statement anywhere in the corpus
is at activity level in SIRUSA — collection instrument *"Lainnya : Publikasi KKP dan BPS"* — naming
two agencies collectively for the whole compilation and attributing none to a specific indicator
(`doc_4_sirusa-metadata_chunk_007`, kegiatan/94936; evidence-pack gap G1).

Therefore **"distinct source publications per sub-pillar" cannot be counted from the published
record.** Say so explicitly, and use the auditable surrogate: the count of distinct
`Data Pembentuk Indikator` items a compiler must separately source and reconcile.

  compilation. The PRD requires the independent-purposes statement in §1 **and §9**.
