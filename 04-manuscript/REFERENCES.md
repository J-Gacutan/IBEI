## Notes on construction

**Four filenames in [00-background/](../00-background/) carry the wrong year and must never be cited
from the filename.** Rinaldi is 2025, not 2024. The Ocean Panel paper is Fenichel et al. 2020, not
2022. IDEEA cites itself as 2021, not 2023. `SEEA-EA-2021-final-2024ed.pdf` is a 2024 imprint of the
standard adopted in 2021: cite the imprint and name the adoption year in text.

**Excluded and why.** `ADB-Indonesia-blue-economy-key-policies.pdf` is an undated Danareksa Research
Institute slide deck carrying no ADB attribution, and its substantive slide is sourced to BAPPENAS
(2021), which is cited instead. `ASEAN-Blue-Wealth-Indonesia-snapshot.md` is an undated web snapshot
with no named author. `MFAT-2024-Indonesia-Green-Economy.pdf` has no personal author and two
conflicting cover dates. `PermenKP-18-2025` is citable by number and enactment date only, its Berita
Negara number rendering as unmapped glyphs in the copy held. `Bogor_Strategic_Action_Plan_2026` prints
as published on 1 April 2026 whilst its own metadata still reads DRAFT.

**Two documents are objects of study rather than literature.** BAPPENAS (2025a) and BAPPENAS (2025b)
are the artefacts the manuscript analyses. They are cited throughout by printed folio with the PDF page
in brackets, following the workspace convention, and are entered above so that a reader can obtain
them.

**One source deliberately not cited.** The GOAP Technical Guidance circular set, from which an earlier
draft drew badge-and-status attributions, is unpublished and carries Draft and Planned statuses. Those
citations have been replaced by the accounting standard and by IDEEA Group (2021). A published first
edition does exist, *Technical Guidance on Ocean Accounting for Sustainable Development* (Global Ocean
Accounts Partnership, 2019), which MMAF (2022a) cites in its own bibliography. It is not held in this
workspace, and nothing in the manuscript currently rests on it. Obtaining it is the way to restore a
guidance-side citation to the forward register in Section 7 if that is wanted.
