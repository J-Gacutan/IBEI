1	# Ocean accounts as compilation infrastructure for a blue economy index
2	
3	## Abstract
4	
5	Composite indices increasingly carry statutory reporting duties. Indonesia must score every province
6	annually on a 44-indicator blue economy index that, on its own registration, collects nothing and
7	compiles entirely from published sectoral statistics. This study asks whether ocean accounts, which
8	record marine ecosystem extent and condition alongside ocean-sector activity, can serve such an index
9	as compilation infrastructure rather than as a rival indicator source. Scoring all 44 register rows
10	against the verbatim data items that define them, not their indicator names, corrects a mapping the
11	present authors published. Three condition indicators ask for the share of provincial area within a
12	stated cover band, a tabulation of extent by class rather than the index against reference condition
13	that earlier work judged missing. Five rows are all an account touches, carrying 7.64%
14	of published weight, of which about 2.33% is what an account would itself supply. Routes compiled at
15	pilot scale bear on 12.79%, which wholly contains that 7.64%, and the one route compiled nowhere on
16	13.70%, yet none of them produces the data item the register asks for. Every share is nominal
17	published weight, which under principal-component weighting need not track effective importance, and
18	the settling test cannot be run whilst the provincial variable matrix is unpublished. The central
19	finding concerns production rather than coverage: what accounting offers such an index is a standing
20	classification, a balancing identity, a re-aggregable spatial basis and a documented revision rule,
21	and these decide the load such infrastructure can carry for a statutory composite.
22	
23	
24	# 1. Introduction
25	
26	Composite indices are a standard instrument of ocean governance. They convert heterogeneous marine
27	statistics into a single number that ministries can target, budget against and report. Their
28	credibility rests less on indicator design than on the production system beneath them, because an
29	index can only be as traceable as the statistics it consumes. That dependency is rarely examined.
30	
31	The form is well established. The Ocean Health Index scores every coastal country against ten goals
32	for a coupled human–ocean system (Halpern et al., 2012). The methodological difficulties are
33	documented rather than novel: the standard reference treats imputation, weighting and aggregation as
34	the three choices that most move a published ranking (Nardo et al., 2008). The Indonesian case
35	therefore belongs to a recognised class of measurement problem.
36	
37	Ocean accounting has developed as the candidate infrastructure for that problem. The System of
38	Environmental-Economic Accounting Ecosystem Accounting framework was adopted by the UN Statistical
39	Commission in 2021 as an international statistical standard. It supplies the extent, condition,
40	service and asset structures on which such accounts are built (United Nations et al., 2024), and a
41	complete national sequence has been specified in detail (IDEEA Group, 2021). Ocean accounts have been
42	argued to belong in the national statistical apparatus rather than in analysis, on the grounds that
43	recurrent policy reporting requires a production system and not a study (Fenichel et al., 2020).
44	Sub-national compilation across extent, service flows and ocean-economy production has been
45	demonstrated operationally (Gacutan et al., 2022). In Indonesia, the spatial basis such accounts
46	require has been tested empirically: grid size and designation rule change the extent estimate
47	materially (Rahayu et al., 2024). None of that work establishes whether an account compiled to those
48	specifications can carry the reporting load of a statutory index. That is a question about production
49	capacity, not about accounting method.
50	
51	Indonesia is where the policy demand is most explicit. Its ocean policy lineage runs from the 2017
52	Indonesian Ocean Policy through a development framework (BAPPENAS, 2021) to a blue economy roadmap
53	(BAPPENAS, 2024). A recent review of that lineage names limited data support among the obstacles to
54	implementation (Wuwung et al., 2024). Quantitative work on Indonesian blue economy indicators remains
55	thin. Rinaldi et al. (2025) construct their own sub-national index for Sumatera on a human development
56	index template and regress it on regional product. That is the closest existing treatment, and it is
57	not an analysis of the official index. The nearest adjacent class of work is the statistical
58	quality-assurance audit, which assesses a national production system against a general quality
59	framework, whereas what is absent is an assessment of a specific composite against the data items its
60	own register asks for. The production system beneath the official index has not been examined.
61	
62	The Indonesia Blue Economy Index (IBEI) is an official national development indicator under
63	presidential regulation Perpres 12/2025. Provincial targets for 2045 are assigned by joint circular of
64	the Ministry of Home Affairs and the Ministry of National Development Planning (BAPPENAS) for carriage
65	in long-term development plans. A province cannot decline to be scored, and BAPPENAS cannot decline to
66	score it. Against that recurring obligation, the compilation activity is registered in Indonesia's
67	statistical activity system as sectoral statistics gathered by secondary collection from the
68	publications of the Ministry of Marine Affairs and Fisheries (MMAF, Indonesian KKP) and of Statistics
69	Indonesia (BPS) (SIRUSA kegiatan 94936). The index conducts no primary collection of its own. It
70	inherits whatever those agencies publish, at whatever resolution they publish it, and the traceability
71	defects that follow are documented below (Section 2).
72	
73	This study assesses Indonesia's ocean accounts as compilation infrastructure for an index the state is
74	already obliged to produce, rather than as an alternative source of indicator values. The assessment
75	is built on the IBEI's own published documentation, on the primary reports of the Indonesian account
76	pilots, and on the international accounting standard, with every claim tagged for maturity. It
77	contributes four things: an account of what makes provincial compilation hard (Section 2), a three-tier
78	indicator mapping scored against the register's verbatim constituent data items rather than its
79	indicator names (Section 4.2), a quantification of where in the index the account-relevant weight sits
80	(Section 4.3), and a register of the conditions precedent for continued supply (Section 4.4).
81	
82	Two boundaries hold throughout. Account compilation has its own statutory purposes under MMAF's
83	mandate, and supplying an index is one downstream use among several, so nothing here proposes replacing
84	the IBEI or subordinating either framework to the other. The object of study is bounded to the
85	44-indicator edition of the index, and every Indonesian account output discussed carries maturity tag
86	(b), experimental or pilot compilation.

88	
89	# 2. The compilation problem
90	
91	The obligation set out above is assigned rather than volunteered, and the instrument that distributes it
92	names the figure each province must reach: joint circular SEB Mendagri/Bappenas No. 2/2024 carries a
93	2045 target per province into its long-term development plan (Juknis, Tabel 2, p. 69 [pdf 79]). That
94	the compilation beneath it collects nothing directly is a design decision recorded in Indonesia's own
95	statistical metadata system, and its consequences are legible as specific strains in the published
96	output. This section establishes both from the IBEI's own documentation, and closes on two unresolved
97	contradictions.
98	
99	## 2.1 Compilation without collection
100	
101	SIRUSA registers the IBEI compilation as *Kompilasi Produk Administrasi* under *Statistik Sektoral*,
102	implemented by BAPPENAS's Directorate of Marine Affairs and Fisheries, with an empty *Identitas
103	Rekomendasi* (kegiatan 94936, retrieved 2026-08-13). The design is secondary compilation throughout.
104	Collection mode is *Pengumpulan data sekunder*, the instrument is *Lainnya: Publikasi KKP dan BPS*,
105	and the unit is the province. The register records no pilot survey, no non-response adjustment, no
106	staff training, zero supervisors and zero enumerators. The index's supply is therefore fixed to
107	whatever KKP and BPS have already published, at the resolution and vintage they published it.
108	
109	The collection capability the index draws on is real, and it sits with those two producers, which is
110	the statutory division this article holds to: BAPPENAS plans, whilst MMAF and BPS produce statistics.
111	What the register establishes is not an absence of capability in the system. It is the absence of any
112	collection instrument inside the compilation itself.
113	
114	The reporting window is narrow and is published from two directions. SIRUSA schedules collection from
115	1 April to 1 May 2025 and dissemination from 1 August to 1 September 2025, whilst the Juknis puts the
116	first phase of its monitoring and evaluation cycle in October to December of year *n* (p. 71 [pdf 81]).
117	Read together, these imply that an upstream figure not public by roughly April of year *n+1* cannot
118	enter that cycle's index. Neither document reconciles them, and only the first phase was extracted into
119	the corpus, so the implication is stated conditionally. Figure 1 draws both calendars against the
120	production chain they feed, and marks the single step at which account-derived data could enter.
121	
122	## 2.2 What the published output records
123	
124	Two defects carry the argument, and both are failures of provenance rather than of arithmetic.
125	
126	Gaps are filled by prediction, and the prediction is not recorded. Annex 1 of the Report specifies a
127	machine-learning framework predicting missing values from socio-economic proxies under two scenarios,
128	with SUSENAS named as the proxy source (pp. 64–66 [pdf 76–78]). The framework itself is specified in
129	detail. What is missing sits downstream of it: the annex names no imputed indicator, no count of
130	imputed cells, no selected model, no achieved error, and no proxy beyond SUSENAS. Under the first
131	scenario an indicator absent in every province is predicted for every province. No reader of any
132	published table can therefore distinguish a measured provincial value from a predicted one, no
133	document obtained recording which rows were imputed. Which indicators were imputed must not be inferred from which look hard to collect.
134	
135	Three provinces record no value across three sub-pillars. Papua Barat Daya, Papua Selatan and Papua
136	Tengah each record 0.00 for marine-based industry, technology and governance, and all three were created
137	in the 2022 provincial split (Report p. 70 [pdf 82]). Juknis Tabel 2 carries the same three provinces
138	at 2023 baselines well above their 2025 achievements (p. 69 [pdf 79]), and the two documents head those
139	columns differently. Whether the zeros reflect unavailability or a measured zero is not stated in either
140	document. They are reported as printed, and read as consistent with a compilation discontinuity rather
141	than as proof of one. This is where a new administrative unit needs a spatially re-aggregable source
142	rather than a republished provincial total.
143	
144	Table 1 records four further symptoms without arguing them here. Each is uninterrogable from the
145	published record.
146	
147	The denominator of the obligation is itself unstable inside the guidance that creates it. Indonesia has
148	38 provinces, whilst the Report's Annex 2 tabulates 37, the monitoring chapter asserts comparability
149	over *37 provinsi* (p. 71 [pdf 81]), and the sensitivity chapter recomputes the index *untuk 38
150	provinsi* (p. 101 [pdf 111]). Two of those are in the same handbook. The number used in any sentence
151	here is the number that sentence is about.

193	# 3. Materials and methods
194	
195	## 3.1 Design
196	
197	The study is a documentary analysis of two published specifications read against each other: the IBEI
198	register as printed by its custodian, and the account structures of the SEEA Ecosystem Accounting
199	standard. No sampling, statistical inference or hypothesis test is involved, and no result reported
200	here is an estimate with a sampling distribution. The equivalent burden is reproducibility of a scoring
201	rule and of an arithmetic chain, which the deposited materials discharge.
202	
203	## 3.2 What an account has to be
204	
205	The membership rule below tests register rows against account output, so the account side needs
206	specifying first. Compilation infrastructure, as used here, means a production system that turns
207	primary observation into a statistical output on a repeating cycle, carrying its own spatial unit,
208	provenance chain and revision rule. Four properties do that work. They are this study's synthesis
209	rather than a construct the standard names as such, assembled from provisions it states separately.
210	
211	*A standing unit and classification definition* is fixed by the account types themselves. *A
212	balancing identity* renders an unexplained change a visible residual, closing stock equalling opening
213	stock plus additions less reductions, each split into managed and unmanaged, so that the nature of a
214	change is recorded rather than inferred (United Nations et al., 2024, paras 4.14–4.15, p. 86). *A
215	re-aggregable spatial basis* follows from basic spatial units being a means of implementing the
216	approach rather than elements of a nested hierarchy (App. A1.1, para. A1.3), the reporting geography
217	being a compiler-delineated accounting area, so provincial and district figures are two aggregations of
218	one production run rather than two data requests. *Documented vintage and revision* follows from the
219	requirements that an account be consistent over time and across classifications (para. 1.60, p. 18) and
220	that previous estimates be revised where updated imagery or methods permit, with the revision
221	recoverable as a difference (para. 4.17, p. 88).
222	
223	Two of the four are properties of a compiled account rather than of a well-run register. Section 5
224	concedes the other two.
225	
226	## 3.3 Corpus
227	
228	A citation-anchored corpus of 199 chunks was built over the index's own published documentation and is
229	released with this article under the Data availability statement, so every quotation resolves to a
230	numbered record rather than a page range. Extraction was purposive. The build, dated 2026-08-14,
231	comprises 106 chunks from the Juknis (BAPPENAS 2025a), 49 from the IBEI Report 2024 in its December 2025 revision
232	(BAPPENAS 2025b), 15 from Rinaldi et al. (2025) and 29 from the SIRUSA metadata (kegiatan 94936
233	together with indikator 105371 and 105709–105718, all retrieved 2026-08-13), at the printed-page
234	ranges listed in the deposit. Three rules governed it. A passage was included where it stated a
235	register row, a weight, a method step, a published provincial value or a metadata field of the
236	compilation. It was excluded where it was narrative framing, policy justification, or repetition of a
237	passage already captured. Extraction of a slice stopped when a further page yielded nothing meeting the
238	inclusion rule. The boundary is the four documents named, so the corpus is a census of what they state
239	on those five topics rather than a sample of a literature. Each record carries its document and slice,
240	the verbatim Indonesian text, an English gloss used nowhere as a quotation source, the printed and PDF
241	page, a maturity tag and a reconstruction flag. That flag, on 17 records, marks content assembled from
242	a table the PDF text layer renders incompletely, so the values are read from the document whilst their
243	layout is reconstructed, and no flagged record is the sole support for any claim made here. Chunks in
244	conflict are recorded as conflicts rather than reconciled, the four-against-three pillar contradiction
245	among them.
246	
247	Account-side evidence sits outside that corpus and was read directly: the three MMAF 2022 reports,
248	of which Gili Matra is the compilation (MMAF, 2022a, 2022b, 2022c); BPS SEEA-workshop decks
249	(Tasriah et al., 2022; Wulandari and Jannah, 2023); Rahayu et al. (2024); Kepmen KP 44/2023; and the
250	GOAP country page (retrieved 2026-08-13). All carry clean text layers, and no optical character recognition
251	was applied. Retrieval and
252	assembly ran from 11 to 16 August 2026. A join table reproduces all 44 register rows with each row's
253	*Data Pembentuk Indikator* constituent data items verbatim, transcribed from Juknis Tabel 1, pp.
254	11–13 [pdf 21–23], totalling 62 items. That join table, not the running text of the handbook, is the
255	object the membership rule was applied to.
256	
257	Searching was bilingual and was run over the document set named above rather than over a statistical
258	catalogue. The Indonesian terms used were *indeks ekonomi biru*, *neraca laut*, *akun laut*, *neraca
259	ekosistem*, *unit spasial dasar laut*, *peta jalan ekonomi biru* and *Satu Data Indonesia*, with
260	their English equivalents and no date restriction. No dedicated search of the BPS statistical
261	catalogue or of the Satu Data Indonesia portal was undertaken, and by project constraint no
262	Indonesian agency was approached. Every absence claim in this article is therefore stated as unfound
263	within the named set, and is not a claim about the Indonesian statistical system. Within that set,
264	no Indonesian statistical register supplying provenance, spatial unit, vintage and revision policy
265	for marine ecosystem extent and condition products, separately from an account structure, was
266	encountered. That negative bears on the register objection weighed in Section 5.2.

268	## 3.4 Unit of analysis and membership
269	
270	The unit is the register row, of which there are 44. The object tested is the row's constituent data
271	items rather than its title, on the Indonesian wording. Each row was graded on one of three grounds.
272	A row is *matched* where a constituent data item names a quantity that a defined SEEA Ecosystem
273	Accounting output contains. It is *partial* where account output supplies a component of the quantity
274	but not the quantity itself. It is a *provenance question* where account output bears on the row
275	without supplying it. Tier A is the union of the three grades and has five members: indicators 19, 20
276	and 21 matched, 24 partial, and 27 a provenance question. The remaining 39 rows are graded none.
277	
278	The rule was applied item by item, so a row carrying several items takes the highest grade any one
279	attains, which makes the 62 items rather than the 44 rows the tested population. Two decisions were
280	fixed before scoring: that indicator titles are not evidence of what a row requires, titles having
281	produced the predecessor figures this analysis rejects, and that a definitional match carries no
282	claim about whether anything has been compiled. Grading was by the authors against those criteria,
283	not blind, with no second coder and no adjudication protocol. Every row's grade and ground is
284	deposited as Supplementary Table S1.
285	
286	Membership in Tier A is a definitional property. The separate operative test applied to the account
287	side is repeatability into a dated window rather than demonstrated capability, since the obligation
288	is annual and can receive only what a producer has already released at the required vintage.
289	Maturity is tagged on every claim that something exists, as (a) published official statistics, (b)
290	experimental or pilot compilation, (c) methodology under development, or (d) announced but not
291	started. The tag describes the datum rather than the document reporting it.
292	
293	## 3.5 Ambiguities in the weight arithmetic
294	
295	Weights are taken from the Juknis as printed: pillar weights from Tabel 4, p. 94 [pdf 104];
296	sub-pillar splits from Tabel 5, p. 94 [pdf 104]; variable weights from Tabel 6, pp. 95–96 [pdf
297	105–106]; and the sub-pillar shares of Gambar 11, p. 93 [pdf 103], used as an independent integer
298	cross-check. Shares of the composite are products of those published percentages, carried to two
299	decimal places from one-decimal inputs and reported in prose to one decimal with 'about'. Every
300	weight used here is printed, and none is reconstructed. Where a total is built from component shares it
301	is the sum of the rounded components rather than the product of the unrounded inputs, which moves
302	three totals by 0.01 and is stated so either route is reproducible. All weight arithmetic follows
303	the three-pillar structure of Tabel 4, Tabel 5 and Gambar 11, weights being published only there,
304	whilst register rows keep their Tabel 1 labels (Section 2.3). The choice is material in one place and
305	immaterial in another. Every Tier A indicator sits in the environment pillar under both structures, so
306	Section 4.2's arithmetic is unaffected on either reading. Section 4.3's figures are computed over
307	precisely the pillars across which indicators 15 to 18 move, and are therefore structure-dependent,
308	with no four-pillar equivalent in the published weights.
309	
310	The handbook does not state whether the percentages in Tabel 4 to Tabel 6 are raw
311	first-principal-component loadings or normalised contributions, noting only that *besaran pembobot
312	dihasilkan secara otomatis melalui proses PCA* (p. 93 [pdf 103]). The distinction matters because
313	raw loadings need not sum to anything in particular, so shares built by multiplying them would have
314	no defined denominator. The test applied is to renormalise each printed group to sum to 100 and
315	recompute, and it changes almost nothing, for a reason that is a property of the tables rather than a
316	stipulation of this study. All fifteen printed weight groups already sum to 100.0 within one-decimal
317	rounding, and the largest effect anywhere is 0.03 percentage points, with every share carried in this
318	article unchanged (S2, renormalisation test). Fifteen independent groups of raw loadings would not sum to 100 by accident,
319	so the percentages are read here as normalised contributions and the derived shares are carried without
320	a denominator caveat. That inference is this study's reading of the tables and is stated nowhere in
321	the source. A second ambiguity is local rather than global and the census does not
322	reach it: indicator 38 is built from a spatial domain in Tabel 1 and a sectoral one in Tabel 6, which
323	moves the social share between 13.7% and about 14.0% (S4.1). The census is deposited as Supplementary
324	Data S2.
325	
326	## 3.6 Reporting conventions
327	
328	Indonesian decimal commas are preserved inside quotation and rendered as points in prose. Quotations
329	are from the Indonesian source text, and the English glosses accompanying them are this study's own,
330	prepared against the Indonesian and not drawn from any published translation. Three evidential
331	distinctions are marked in prose wherever they bear on a claim. A required source that was not found is
332	reported as unfound rather than inferred around. A step resting on something unconfirmed is stated as
333	resting on it. And every share of the composite is qualified as measuring what an account type bears on
334	rather than what it supplies, which is defined at the head of Section 4.2 and is not a qualification of
335	the weights, which are printed. Conflicting sources are
336	reported as conflicts and are not averaged or adjudicated. Compilation, scoring and the weight
337	arithmetic were carried out in Python 3 over the deposited corpus and join table, with the scripts
338	deposited as part of the replication materials.

340	# 4. Results
341	
342	## 4.1 The compiled record
343	
344	Indonesian ocean accounting has produced one pilot compilation, in one marine protected area, in one
345	province, on one vintage, and every output carries maturity tag (b). The compiled record comprises the
346	Gili Matra Marine Recreational Park pilot off north Lombok, together with one peer-reviewed
347	grid-optimisation study at Saleh Bay, Sumbawa (Rahayu et al., 2024). Both sit in West Nusa Tenggara, so
348	NTB is the single province carrying a documented published compilation located by this study. A national Ocean Accounts Dashboard launched in July 2025 is described by GOAP as publicly available
349	and interactive on a harmonised spatial frame (GOAP country page, retrieved 2026-08-13), a programme
350	self-statement about a platform that evidences no compilation beyond the pilot record.
351	
352	The pilot's footprint belongs in its own units, because the smallness is part of the finding: 2,980.84
353	ha on a 25 m by 25 m marine basic spatial unit (MBSU) grid of 48,211 cells, opening in 2015 and closing
354	in 2021 (MMAF, 2022a, pp. 30, 39 [pdf 44, 53]). Footprint and cell count do not reconcile, and the
355	discrepancy is recorded as observed rather than resolved (S5.1). Against a statutory obligation to
356	score every province annually, this is roughly 30 km², compiled once.
357	
358	Maturity is the programme's own self-description rather than an external verdict. GOAP labels the three
359	2022 MMAF outputs 'preliminary consultation drafts', and BPS lists scaling up to the total Indonesian
360	marine area among its outstanding challenges (Tasriah et al., 2022, slide 9). There is no BPS ocean
361	account release, no work-programme entry, no revision policy and no published compilation cost, and the
362	ecosystem scope is confined to mangrove, seagrass and coral reef.
363	
364	The three account types differ in completeness of the account form, not in the standing of the
365	compilation producing them. Extent is the only type with a verified opening, change and closing series,
366	the pilot publishing a complete asset account with an ecosystem-conversion matrix alongside (MMAF,
367	2022a, tab. 5–6, pp. 52–53 [pdf 66–67]). Condition is compiled in a limited form, as cover-based
368	measurement at Tier 1 to Tier 2 against documented reference bands rather than as a normalised
369	multi-indicator composite (pp. 31–33 [pdf 45–47]). Valuation
370	is the least developed and may not be described as an ecosystem services account in physical units.
371	What exists is benefit-transferred monetary valuation (pp. 36, 60 [pdf 50, 74]), which is not the
372	exchange-value basis the standard requires, a limit BPS states itself (Tasriah et al., 2022, slide 9).
373	No ecosystem services account in physical units has been compiled in Indonesia. Supplementary S5
374	reproduces all three with their series.
375	
376	Extent is also where two official series diverge, and the divergence is this article's strongest
377	internal counter-instance. A BPS presentation reports the same pilot, ecosystems and years at
378	substantially lower areas (Tasriah et al., 2022, slide 3), although both series trace to one layer, the
379	Geospatial Information Agency's 2020 mapping validated by 2021 field survey. Neither document states
380	its relationship to the other and no reconciliation is published. The divergence is
381	not a constant offset: the BPS-to-MMAF ratio moves across ecosystems and across the accounting window
382	(S5.2). The two series therefore disagree on the change and not merely on the level, MMAF recording a
383	coral loss of 12.00 ha against a BPS loss of 18.26 ha, a 6.26 ha disagreement. Change is the quantity a balancing identity
384	exists to report, and Section 5.2 interrogates the consequence.
385	
386	## 4.2 What the existing accounts can serve
387	
388	Five of the register's 44 rows are all an ocean account touches. Indicators 19, 20 and 21 name
389	quantities an account's output structure already contains, whilst indicators 24 and 27 sit adjacent to
390	account output and are disposed of rather than claimed (Table 2). Figure 2 gives the assignment for all
391	44 rows with their constituent data items.
392	
393	Four qualifications govern every share in this section and the two that follow. Each share measures
394	what these account types bear on, never what an account would supply and never an achievable score
395	improvement. Each is a share of nominal published weight, which under this estimator need not track
396	effective importance (Section 5.5). Every economy and social figure is structure-dependent on the
397	three-pillar locus of Section 3.5, with no four-pillar equivalent in the published weights. And the
398	social pillar's indicators remain BPS outputs throughout, the account contribution being a
399	classification standard rather than a substitute source, so the wider 30.5% reading may be used only
400	where it is named as the wider reading.
401	
402	On the compilation axis the concession is complete. None of the five indicator values has been
403	published by any Indonesian account, for any province or any site. Every capability
404	claim here is a claim about definitional correspondence and a demonstrated input layer, never about an
405	existing output. All five rows sit in the environment pillar under both pillar structures, so the
406	unresolved conflict does not reach this arithmetic.
407	
408	The three condition indicators share one grammatical form. Indicator 19 asks for the *Persentase
409	kawasan terumbu karang dengan tutupan karang hidup antara 50-100% per provinsi*, indicator 20 for the
410	seagrass equivalent, and indicator 21 for *kerapatan tutupan* in mangrove (Juknis, Tabel 1, p. 12 [pdf
411	22]). Each names a percentage of area falling within a stated cover or density band, per province. That
412	is a tabulation of extent by class, not a normalised index against a reference condition. Earlier
413	assessments judged that Indonesia lacks the condition account these indicators require. They do not
414	require one, so the judgement answers a question the index does not pose.
415	
416	What the pilot sets beside that specification is the input layer, not the tabulation. The MBSU extent
417	surface exists, and cover is measured at survey stations rather than mapped per cell, on figures S5.3
418	reproduces with their locators (MMAF, 2022a, pp. 40, 45 [pdf 54, 59]). The cross-tabulation of extent
419	by cover class is not published. The step from the input layer to indicator 19 is one tabulation that
420	has not been performed.
421	
422	The match frays at four edges, derived in full in S5.3. Two are definitional. The index's 50%
423	threshold matches no Indonesian condition class in use, the pilot classifying reef on the bands of
424	Giyanto et al. (2017), and reclassifying at 50% is trivial on a continuous per-cell surface but
425	impossible where national data exist only as classes pre-binned on other boundaries. That makes the
426	coral row a definition gap rather than a coverage gap, and no obtained document evidences a
427	wall-to-wall condition surface. The mangrove unit differs outright, indicator 21 asking for canopy
428	density as a percentage against the pilot's m² per ha and individuals per ha, with no documented
429	conversion (p. 47 [pdf 61]). The other two edges are unfixed choices. Neither the minimum mapping unit
430	nor the denominator is fixed by the register or by the SIRUSA definitions, and the extent estimate is
431	itself a function of the grid rule (Rahayu et al., 2024), so until a grid rule is adopted the account's
432	figure is a family of figures. A vintage gap sits alongside these, the only verified series closing in
433	2021 against the index's T-1 requirement.
434	
435	Indicator 24 asks for restored area, glossed in SIRUSA as degraded area subsequently replanted, which
436	is an activity input measured in hectares planted (Table 2). The pilot's asset account carries a
437	managed-expansion line, unpopulated at Gili Matra, so the correspondence is structural rather than
438	demonstrated: an account of this form records realised managed change, whilst the indicator counts
439	hectares planted. A scope mismatch compounds it, the indicator's three land covers being mangrove,
440	swamp and peatland against the marine account's mangrove, seagrass and coral reef.
441	
442	Indicator 27 asks for designated conservation area, and is in the tier for a different reason. At 22.0% it is the largest
443	single variable in the resource-quality sub-pillar, about 4.06% of the composite, which exceeds all
444	three ecosystem-condition variables combined at about 2.33%. Tabel 7's shock analysis also makes it the
445	environment pillar's largest sensitivity contributor, at +2.45 against +0.41 for good-condition coral
446	reef, in units the handbook prints and does not define, so the two are read as ordinally comparable
447	(pp. 101–104 [pdf 111–114]). It needs no account to be produced. Its constituent data item measures
448	area *ditetapkan*, designated, an administrative datum already held by its custodians, and the
449	account's near-term contribution here is reconciliation of mapped extent to the designated polygon
450	rather than supply.
451	
452	The weight chain is shown openly as a multiplication. The environment pillar carries 36.9% of the
453	composite and the resource-quality sub-pillar 50.0% of the pillar (Juknis, Tabel 4 and Tabel 5, p. 94
454	[pdf 104]), giving 18.45%, of which the five Tier A variables sum to about 7.64% (Figure 3). Most of
455	that 7.64% sits at rows the mapping has itself disqualified: about 2.33% at the three matched condition

457	not supply. **The share at which an account is the prospective

459	is credited in full, which the activity-against-outcome mismatch and the scope gap indicate it should

584	## 4.4 Conditions precedent for supply
585	
586	A definitional match is not a supply arrangement. Five conditions must hold before pilot account output
587	could serve the IBEI as a repeating input, none is satisfied in the obtained record, and they are not
588	equally binding (Table 4). Three are one-off acts of codification that a single decision by MMAF, BPS or
589	BAPPENAS could discharge. Two are discharged only by sustaining a production cycle, and those two
590	decide whether the mapping in Section 4.2 is a recommendation or a wish.
591	
592	The register would be refuted by an adopted national marine spatial standard, a BPS work-programme
593	entry for a marine ecosystem account, or an extent compilation beyond the pilot sites closing after
594	2021.
595	
596	The two recurring conditions are the binding ones. SIRUSA specifies reference period T-1 for all five
597	mapped variables, and what is absent is not timeliness but the second vintage, no repeat compilation of
598	the pilot sites having been found. The gap between the 2021 closing year and the reference year the
599	2025 cycle requires is three reference years (Figure 5). The second is a revision policy, for which the
600	standard supplies the substance (paras 4.17–4.18, p. 88) and Indonesia supplies no instrument. An
601	account silently improved between vintages would move a province's index value with no ecosystem change
602	behind it, and nothing in the published record would flag it. Twelve provinces already carry a 2045
603	target above the national 100,00 (Juknis, p. 69 [pdf 79], authors' own count), so a restatement rule
604	has to govern provincial and national figures that are inconsistent with each other before any account
605	enters.
606	
607	The institutional condition is unmet against an instrument whose own term has run. Kepmen KP 44/2023
608	constituted the Tim Penyusun Neraca Sumber Daya Laut in February 2023 with a term expiring that
609	December, and no renewal was located, which is a statement that none was found rather than that the
610	mandate lapsed, the July 2025 dashboard indicating activity under some instrument not obtained. Nor are
611	microdata released, so even a compliant supplier could not verify that its value survived into the
612	published score.
613	
614	One result bears on sequencing. Where a province has no account, an account-derived indicator would
615	itself be predicted from socio-economic proxies under the framework the Report publishes (pp. 64–66
616	[pdf 76–78]), so an environmental value would become partly a function of economic and social
617	covariates. The framework's first scenario also trains on the previous cycle's historical data, which a
618	newly introduced indicator does not have. Supplementary S3 carries the derivation for each condition.

636	# 5. Discussion
637	
638	## 5.1 What compilation changes
639	
640	The question was whether an account compiled to SEEA specifications can carry the reporting load of a
641	statutory index. The load is now quantified and it is small, and on that measure an account cannot
642	carry it. The measure is also the wrong one. What an account offers at the rows it touches is a
643	production process rather than a stock of substitutable values, which is a narrower claim than an
644	efficiency claim, and the evidence assembled here would not support the wider one. Of the register's
645	62 constituent data items, the three at indicators 19, 20 and 21 are cross-classifications of one
646	extent-and-condition compilation, so the consolidation is three items into one production run. The
647	argument is not that the count is large. It is that those three are among the items the index
648	presently has no way to show it measured rather than predicted.
649	
650	A cover-class percentage compiled from a gridded extent surface and a classified cover layer carries
651	its own spatial unit, its own opening and closing stocks, and a provenance chain back to the imagery
652	and field survey behind it. It is reproducible in the next cycle by repeating the compilation. A
653	value taken from a secondary publication, or predicted where no publication exists, carries none of
654	those properties, and the published record does not distinguish the two (Section 2.2).
655	
656	That the distinction is worth publishing is established outside Indonesia. The Ocean Health Index, a
657	composite of comparable construction in the same domain (Halpern et al., 2012), reports that 18.5% of
658	its global score rests on gapfilled rather than observed data, and its authors recommend that
659	gapfilling be tracked from the outset and presented in the same framework as the scores themselves
660	(Frazier et al., 2016). The IBEI publishes an imputation framework and no per-indicator record, so the
661	comparison identifies a reporting reform the index could adopt without any account being compiled.
662	
663	Everything downstream of the input is untouched. Account supply changes the value entering a slot, not
664	the slot, its weight or the aggregation rule. The three-stage principal component procedure, the
665	loading-factor weights, the pillar shares and the index's published limitations stand as they are.
666	
667	## 5.2 What survives the counter-instance
668	
669	The strongest evidence against this article's own claim is in its own account-side record. The one
670	compiled Indonesian account has produced two closing stocks and two deltas for the same site, the
671	same three ecosystems and the same two years, disagreeing not by a constant offset but by a ratio
672	that moves across ecosystems and across the accounting window (Section 4.1). The pilot's own
673	reporting compounds it, the narrative giving 16.99% live hard coral cover for 2021 against 18.36% in
674	the report's table on the same page. Change is the quantity a balancing identity exists to report,
675	and provenance is what this article claims accounts buy.
676	
677	This is a disconfirming instance and is retained as one. It is not disposed of by observing that the two
678	series are parallel releases of one mapping layer and that neither is an account of the other. That
679	observation is true, and it is also unfalsifiable in the direction this article needs, because the only
680	compiled Indonesian instance would be reclassified out of the evidence base at the point where it tests
681	the claim. The narrower property claimed
682	here is not that compilation prevents divergence, which nothing in the standard promises, but that a
683	balancing identity gives divergence somewhere to appear. Opening stock, additions, reductions and
684	closing stock are four entries that must reconcile, and a second compilation must restate rather than
685	replace. Gili Matra cannot confirm that property, because neither document was compiled under such an
686	identity. It cannot refute it either, for the same reason. What it does establish is the cost of the
687	absence: a 6.26 ha disagreement about coral change was published twice and reconciled nowhere. The
688	provenance property is therefore asserted from account form and is untested on the Indonesian record,
689	and it will remain untested until a compilation is revised under such a rule.
690	
691	A second objection bears on the same argument, and holds outright on two of the four properties. A
692	standing unit and classification definition is an act of codification, being a published definition of
693	the mapping unit, the ecosystem classes and the seaward limits, and nothing about compiling an account
694	is needed to write one down. Documented vintage and provenance is likewise a metadata
695	requirement any well-run register discharges. The spatial property is intermediate: a register can
696	mandate a grid and a minimum mapping unit but cannot produce the re-aggregable cell-level tabulation
697	the mandate presupposes. What does not reduce to codification is the residual: the balancing identity,
698	and the revision rule that alters the opening and closing entries themselves, both properties of
699	double-entry over a stock. The residual is thin, and saying so is more useful than defending a wider
700	claim. A reader who concludes that Indonesia should codify definitions before compiling anything has
701	read the evidence correctly, and the ordering follows from Section 4.4's own result. An indicator
702	inserted ahead of coverage would be imputed from socio-economic proxies in every province lacking an
703	account, so insertion should be phased behind compilation, which is a sequencing recommendation rather
704	than a change to the index's method.
705	
706	## 5.3 What account supply does not fix
707	
708	The index's custodian has printed its own methodological limits, and account supply reaches none of
709	them. Four are conceded in the Report's own methods statement (Section 3.1.1, p. 34 [pdf 46]): an
710	assumption of linear and monotonic relationships, sensitivity to outliers, weights set on 2024 data
711	that *mungkin saja menjadi tidak relevan untuk tahun-tahun mendatang*, and a single weight structure
712	applied across a heterogeneous archipelago. Every one is a property of the estimator, whilst an
713	account changes the input. The first is worth dwelling on. The threshold relationship BAPPENAS itself
714	describes, in which the benefit of fishing intensity holds below a threshold whilst damage rises
715	above it, is what an ecosystem account measures well, and better inputs cannot reach a relationship the
716	estimator represents linearly by design.
717	
718	Aggregation discards what the account was built to show. The constituent data items for indicators
719	19, 20 and 21 each end *per provinsi*, so an account compiled on a fine spatial grid loses its
720	within-province distribution at the moment it becomes an indicator. The account can tell a
721	provincial planner which reef complex is driving a change, whilst the index can report only that a
722	percentage moved. That is best read as a consequence of publishing the indicator without the grid, so
723	the remedy is an architecture in which both are released. The provincial boundary is administrative and
724	not ecological, and the standard leaves the accounting area to the compiler (para. 2.12), so a reef
725	complex straddling that line is divided by legal artefact, a division accounts make explicit and
726	auditable rather than ecologically meaningful.
727	
728	Net extent change cannot simply be inserted. The index states its own weighting rule: an indicator
729	with greater variability and strong correlation to others in its domain *akan secara alami
730	mendapatkan bobot yang lebih tinggi* (p. 29 [pdf 41]). A signed annual difference of a large, slowly
731	changing stock is low-variance by construction and can change sign between cycles, so it would attract
732	a small and unstable weight, and might meet the printed rule *Reversi nilai indikator (atau drop
733	indikator) jika ditemukan relasi negatif* (p. 30 [pdf 42]), though whether a sign-changing series
734	triggers it is not specified and is read here as an inference. Under principal components, weighting
735	corrects for overlapping information rather than measuring importance (Nardo et al., 2008, §6.1). Net
736	extent change therefore belongs beside the composite as a companion series rather than inside it as a
737	forty-fifth indicator.
738	
739	Half the environment pillar measures something no ocean account should be built to measure. The
740	renewable-energy sub-pillar carries 50.0% of the pillar and contains no marine variable, being solar
741	and hydropower throughout (S4.3). No existing account can supply what it asks for, and building one
742	to do so would be an instrument error. The coverage claim stops at that boundary, and stopping is
743	what makes it credible.
744	
745	## 5.4 Loose coupling, and four readings
746	
747	One objection has to be met before any recommendation is made, because it vitiates the headline
748	quantities on which a coverage argument would rest. The shares reported above are nominal published
749	weight and not effective importance, and the test that would settle the difference cannot be run
750	whilst the provincial variable matrix is unpublished (Section 5.5). What follows is therefore a
751	recommendation about production and not about coverage, and it does not rest on the shares.
752	
753	Where substitution is not warranted, and on the published maturity evidence it is not, the
754	arrangement that delivers the gain is loose coupling. It has three requirements: (1) shared indicator
755	definitions at the cover-class thresholds the Juknis already prints, with the unit and denominator
756	rules the register leaves open settled with its custodian, (2) a shared spatial unit that aggregates to
757	the province, and (3) parallel publication of the account alongside the index. It is defined as much by
758	what it does not require: no institutional transfer, no change to the index's data pipeline, and no
759	dependency of a statutory index on an experimental compilation. Parallel publication is a combined
760	presentation (paras 14.56–14.57) and is the nearer term of the two, whilst sub-national compilation
761	follows from the accounting area being definable at a subnational administrative boundary (para.
762	2.12) and has been demonstrated at that scale elsewhere (Gacutan et al., 2022). Loose coupling would have
763	the property that a compilation cycle missing the window fails without the index failing with it, a
764	property that follows from the arrangement's form and has not been observed, no coupling of this kind
765	having been instantiated in Indonesia or, in any document obtained, elsewhere.
766	
767	The institutional division holds at the point of recommendation, where it is most at risk. BAPPENAS is
768	*pengampu* of the calculation whilst the constituent data are provincial (Juknis, p. 68 [pdf 78]), so
769	nothing here asks either institution to take on the other's mandate. The arrangement would have to be
770	settled by whichever body now holds the coordinating function, the team constituted under Kepmen KP
771	44/2023 having had its term expire with no renewal instrument located.
772	
773	Four readings of the same record would lead elsewhere. The first is the position this article's whole
774	premise contests: that secondary compilation is adequate for a policy composite provided the
775	uncertainty it introduces is quantified and published. It is the cheaper answer and on three of this
776	article's four properties a good one, and Frazier et al. (2016) show the Ocean Health Index doing it.
777	What it cannot supply is the quantity to be uncertain about. The register asks for a share of
778	provincial area within a cover band, which no Indonesian source publishes at any uncertainty (Section
779	4.2), and an uncertainty statement over an imputed value describes the imputation rather than the reef.
780	The position is right about most of the index and silent about the rows at issue here, and adopting it
781	would cost nothing to try, needing only the reform recommended at Section 5.1. Second, Rinaldi et al. (2025) construct an alternative index on a human development index template,
782	treating the problem as one of index design rather than of the production system beneath it, on which
783	reading the effort recommended here is misdirected. Third, Wuwung et al. (2024) locate the constraint on Indonesian ocean policy in
784	institutional fragmentation across mandates. That concession is accepted here and it reorders Section
785	4.4: an inter-agency supply arrangement is the hardest of the conditions precedent, not the cheapest,
786	and the three acts of codification should be read as the tractable ones precisely because each sits
787	inside a single mandate. Fourth, Ravallion (2012) defines a mashup index as a
788	composite for which theory and practice provide little guidance on design, and doubts the value added
789	of such indices relative to monitoring the components separately. The answer this record supports is
790	statutory rather than methodological. The IBEI is scored for every province annually under Perpres
791	12/2025, so the dashboard alternative is not in BAPPENAS's gift, and what supplies the composite is a
792	live question whether or not an aggregate is the instrument a statistician would have chosen. No source in
793	the set searched argues that accounts cannot serve as statistical infrastructure for a composite index,
794	which is a statement about a bilingual search over a named document set rather than about the
795	literature (Section 3.3).
796	
797	## 5.5 Limitations
798	
799	Every share reported here is a nominal share of printed weight, not a measure of effective
800	influence. Nardo et al. (2008, §6.1) hold that under principal components, weighting is not a
801	measure of the theoretical importance of the associated indicator. Paruolo et al. (2013) measure
802	what each variable actually contributes to five composites, the human development index among them,
803	and find that because socio-economic variables are heteroscedastic and correlated, relative nominal
804	weights hardly ever match relative effective importance. The IBEI is a composite over correlated
805	provincial socio-economic variables with weights taken from first-component loadings, which is the
806	configuration in which that divergence was found. Becker et al. (2017) supply the test that would
807	settle it, a correlation-ratio measure of each variable's importance with confidence intervals. It
808	cannot be run here, because it requires the provincial variable matrix beneath the index, which is
809	unpublished. What does not survive should be named as plainly as what does.
810	What does not survive is the reading of 7.64%, 2.33%, 12.79% and 13.70% as measures of consequence:
811	they are shares of nominal published weight, and nominal weight is not importance under this estimator.
812	They remain exact statements of what the handbook prints and of what an account bears on, which is the
813	use made of them here. What survives is the production-process case, which never rested on the
814	shares.
815	
816	Six further limitations bear on specific figures. Whether Tabel 4 to Tabel 6's percentages are raw
817	loadings or normalised contributions is not stated anywhere. The reading adopted here, that they are
818	normalised because all fifteen printed groups sum to 100.0 within rounding, is an inference from the
819	tables rather than a statement in the source (Section 3.5). Were it wrong, the
820	derived shares would lose their denominator rather than move. The printed aggregation formulae are
821	illegible in the PDF text layer at Juknis pp. 64–65 and Report pp. 31–32, so what is reconstructed
822	is the notation, at medium-high confidence, rather than the method. The per-indicator imputation
823	record is unpublished, which is the largest single unknown, because it determines how much of any
824	published provincial value is measurement and how much is prediction. The mapping is a single-coder assessment against
825	stated criteria, graded by the authors and not blind, with no second coder and no adjudication
826	protocol. Deposit secures auditability, not reliability, and the two are not substitutes: the same
827	team's earlier mapping moved from 61% to 39% to 2.8% as the assumed indicator set changed, which is
828	direct evidence that a grading of this kind is unstable under a change of premise. An
829	author-independent re-grade of all five Tier A rows and a random fifteen of the thirty-nine, reporting
830	raw disagreements, is the check this study has not run. It is why every row's
831	score and exclusion ground is deposited. Compilation cost is absent from the public record at every
832	site. And this study engaged no Indonesian agency, by project constraint, so the absence claims
833	above are conditions to live with rather than future work.
834	
835	# 6. Conclusion
836	
837	Both findings point the same way, and it is not the way the question is usually put. Asking how much of
838	a composite an ocean account could fill invites an answer in shares, which is what this evidence
839	supports least, shares being nominal weight that need not track effective importance under this
840	estimator. Asking how the indicators it touches would be produced gives an answer the evidence does
841	support, that question being settled by documents rather than weights. On it the record is unambiguous.
842	Every route runs through content the register specifies and no compiled account produces, so
843	compilation maturity turns out not to be what separates them.
844	
845	The binding constraint on acting on this is not methodological. No compilation cost appears anywhere in
846	the record obtained for this study: no staff-day figure, no budget line, no imagery cost and no
847	field-survey cost, at any site. A province-by-province annual obligation is a scope
848	decision before it is a method decision. This study declines to estimate a cost, since an
849	order-of-magnitude range would be read as a figure, and that declining is itself a limitation: the
850	scope decision identified here as binding is one this study cannot size.
851	
852	Two follow-on studies suggest themselves. The claim that indicators 19, 20 and 21 are definitionally
853	matched to account output is empirically testable today at exactly one site. Re-tabulating the Gili
854	Matra compilation to the index's own 50% to 100% cut would settle whether an account's extent and cover
855	layers yield the register's class-share form at all, and would generate the first real compilation-cost
856	observation. Its limits are set by the record itself: the coral and seagrass cover behind that
857	compilation was surveyed at stations rather than mapped per cell, the mangrove row is blocked on an
858	undocumented unit conversion, and a footprint of 2,980.84 ha supports no comparison against a published
859	provincial value. Extending the tabulation to NTB as a whole would test provincial resolution, and
860	requires the province-wide compilation that does not exist. The second output is practitioner-facing
861	rather than research, and is a document rather than a call for guidance. It is a compilation note,
862	addressed to MMAF as compiler, fixing the four conventions this study found open: the grid and minimum
863	mapping unit, the clipping and allocation rule for a provincial figure, the cover-class thresholds and
864	denominator indicators 19 to 21 require, and a revision entry disclosing what changed between
865	vintages. Both assume continued external
866	technical assistance to the Indonesian programme, which is an assumption rather than an established
867	condition.
868	
869	What would falsify the argument is worth stating, because a paper of this shape is otherwise
870	structurally unfalsifiable. The first arm is testable now: if a single-province compilation reproduces
871	the published provincial value no better than the current secondary-publication route, the
872	production-process case fails on its own terms. The second arm is cost, which cannot be a threshold whilst no
873	compilation cost has ever been published, and it becomes testable only once the first observation
874	exists. The test is then whether cost per province exceeds what the index's existing
875	secondary-compilation cycle spends per province, a quantity BAPPENAS holds and has not released.
876	
877	Within the statutory boundary held throughout, what this study describes is a capacity gap under a
878	statutory obligation, in a country that has demonstrated the production process the obligation would
879	require at exactly one experimental site of 2,980.84 ha, on one vintage now three reference years
880	stale, and with the method limits recorded above. Whether that process scales to 38 provinces annually
881	is not shown here and cannot be shown from one site.

1410	
1411	Long form of Table 4. Each condition states what the index requires, what the accounting standard
1412	supplies, and what this study observed. Absence claims are unfound in the searched set stated at
1413	Section 3.3, not claims of non-existence.
1414	
1415	## S3.1 A national spatial standard
1416	
1417	Provincial account output is not comparable across provinces or across vintages until one minimum
1418	mapping unit and one basic spatial unit are adopted nationally. This is an act of codification,
1419	discharged once, requiring no new compilation.
1420	
1421	The standard requires an account to be consistent over time and with respect to concepts and
1422	classifications (United Nations et al., 2024, para. 1.60), and requires previous estimates to be
1423	revised for continuity of the time series wherever updated data, methods or classifications permit a
1424	reassessment of area (para. 4.17). Grid-based spatial units are the mechanism by which an account
1425	aggregates to any administrative boundary, conditional on the grid existing and being national.
1426	
1427	No adopted national marine grid or minimum mapping unit was found in any obtained Indonesian document. Rahayu et al. (2024), the only peer-reviewed Indonesian account source, state that a
1428	standardised grid has yet to be established, and the pilot literature records different resolutions at
1429	different sites — the case the rule exists to prevent. Grid choice changes the extent tabulation
1430	itself: mangrove error of 44.06% under a 25 m hierarchy scheme against 1.59% under 25 m dominance. Until
1431	a grid rule is adopted, the account's figure is a family of figures, and the index would be importing a
1432	methodological choice.
1433	
1434	## S3.2 A codified clipping rule
1435	
1436	Provincial marine authority extends to 12 nautical miles under Law 23/2014, cited here at second hand
1437	and not read at source, so an account reaching further must be clipped before it can
1438	yield a provincial number.
1439	
1440	The standard leaves the accounting area to the compiler (para. 2.12) and supplies no sub-national
1441	allocation rule for an asset spanning a boundary. The convention this study takes as necessary —
1442	allocation by physical extent within each side, with condition compiled per spatial unit rather than
1443	inherited whole — is a specification rather than a citation. Neither it nor the
1444	statutory line is codified in any account obtained.
1445	
1446	One consequence follows from the standard directly. Condition is not additive across areas, its
1447	variable account being an area-weighted arithmetic mean over the assets of an ecosystem type (para.
1448	5.54, p. 105). Because indicators 19, 20 and 21 ask for a ratio, a provincial value cannot be recovered
1449	by averaging district values, and a supplier would need to publish the denominator alongside the
1450	percentage for a provincial value to be recoverable.
1451	
1452	Clean nesting of cell boundaries across administrative, fisheries-management and marine-spatial-planning
1453	frames is asserted rather than evidenced, and forms part of this condition rather than a
1454	property that may be assumed.
1455	
1456	## S3.3 A repeated compilation inside the reporting window
1457	
1458	SIRUSA specifies reference period T-1 for all five mapped variables (19, 20, 21, 24, 27). The single
1459	verified Indonesian compilation closes at 2021 and was published the following year, a lag T-1 can
1460	accommodate. What is absent is the second vintage. One compilation, however timely, cannot meet a T-1
1461	requirement in any subsequent cycle, and no repeat compilation of the pilot sites was found.
1462	
1463	This condition is recurring rather than one-off. It is discharged by running the cycle again, not by a
1464	decision, and it is taken to survive the index's own inconsistent rendering of its reference
1465	periods. Sustaining a cycle is a resourcing question, and no compilation cost was located in the
1466	public record, so no condition in this register can be priced.
1467	
1468	## S3.4 A revision policy
1469	
1470	The standard already supplies the substance: revision of previous estimates for continuity of the
1471	series, recording no distinct revision entry but altering the opening, closing, addition and reduction
1472	entries themselves, with the size of a revision shown as the difference between the historical and the
1473	revised account for the same period (paras 4.17–4.18, p. 88).
1474	
1475	What is unwritten is the Indonesian instrument that would designate a baseline year and disclose where
1476	the latest extent data refer to an earlier period. The index's own principle of controlled adaptability
1477	presupposes that discipline on the supply side. No revision policy was found for any Indonesian ocean
1478	account output in the searched set.
1479	
1480	The consequence is specific. An account silently improved between vintages would move a province's
1481	index value with no ecosystem change behind it, and nothing in the published record would flag it. The
1482	instrument requires no new compilation: it is a statement of when a series is restated and how a
1483	provisional estimate is marked.
1484	
1485	## S3.5 A statistical work-programme entry
1486	
1487	Account output cannot become a durable input to an official index whilst it remains an unregistered
1488	ministry product feeding an activity itself registered only as sectoral statistics. The IBEI is
1489	recorded in SIRUSA as *Kompilasi Produk Administrasi* under *Statistik Sektoral* with an empty
1490	*Identitas Rekomendasi* (kegiatan 94936), whilst the Juknis names BAPPENAS as *pengampu* of the
1491	calculation (p. 68 [pdf 78]).
1492	
1493	Indonesian ocean account output is an MMAF product constituted under Kepmen KP 44/2023, and no BPS
1494	statistical work-programme entry appears in any document obtained, no dedicated search of the BPS
1495	statistical catalogue having been undertaken. That Kepmen set the team's expiry at
1496	31 December 2023, and no renewal instrument was located, so a supply arrangement would be settled by
1497	whichever body now holds the coordinating function.
1498	
1499	The condition is a documented supply arrangement between MMAF as producer, BPS as statistical authority
1500	and BAPPENAS as compiler. Its minimum content is stated here as this study's own specification rather
1501	than taken from any standard: scope, frequency, quality standards, confidentiality, format and dispute
1502	resolution. Written that way it is a short document rather than a programme. That
1503	accreditation would be the route is inferred from the index's own registration rather than stated
1504	anywhere.
