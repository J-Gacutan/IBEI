1	# Ocean accounts as compilation infrastructure for a blue economy index
2	
3	## Abstract
4	
5	Indonesia is statutorily obliged to score every province annually on a 44-indicator blue economy
6	composite that, on its own registration, conducts no primary collection and compiles entirely from
7	published sectoral statistics. This study asks whether ocean accounts can serve that index as
8	compilation infrastructure rather than as an alternative indicator source, scoring each register row
9	against the verbatim constituent data items that define it. Reading data items rather than indicator
10	names changes the answer, and corrects a mapping the present authors published. Three condition
11	indicators ask for the share of provincial area within a stated cover band — a tabulation of extent by
12	class, not the index against reference condition that earlier work, including our own, judged missing.
13	Five rows are the whole of what an account touches, carrying 7.64% of published weight, of which about
14	2.33% is what an account would itself supply. What separates the account routes into and out of reach
15	is content rather than compilation maturity: routes compiled at pilot scale bear on 12.79%, which
16	wholly contains the 7.64%, and the one route compiled nowhere on a comparable 13.70%, yet no compiled
17	account produces the data item the register asks for at any of them. Every share is nominal published
18	weight, which under principal-component weighting need not track effective importance, and the test
19	that would settle this cannot be run because the provincial variable matrix is unpublished. The
20	finding is therefore not a quantity of indicators an account could fill. It is that what accounting
21	offers such an index is a production process — a standing classification, a balancing identity, a
22	re-aggregable spatial basis and a documented revision rule — and that the load a statistical
23	infrastructure can carry for a statutory composite is decided by that process rather than by coverage.
24	
25	
26	# 1. Introduction
27	
28	Composite indices are a standard instrument of ocean governance. They convert heterogeneous marine
29	statistics into a single number that ministries can target, budget against and report. Their
30	credibility rests less on indicator design than on the production system beneath them, because an
31	index can only be as traceable as the statistics it consumes. That dependency is rarely examined.
32	
33	The form is well established. The Ocean Health Index scores every coastal country against ten goals
34	for a coupled human–ocean system (Halpern et al., 2012). The methodological difficulties are
35	documented rather than novel: the standard reference treats imputation, weighting and aggregation as
36	the three choices that most move a published ranking (Nardo et al., 2008). The Indonesian case
37	therefore belongs to a recognised class of measurement problem.
38	
39	Ocean accounting has developed as the candidate infrastructure for that problem. The System of
40	Environmental-Economic Accounting Ecosystem Accounting framework was adopted by the UN Statistical
41	Commission in 2021 as an international statistical standard. It supplies the extent, condition,
42	service and asset structures on which such accounts are built (United Nations et al., 2024), and a
43	complete national sequence has been specified in detail (IDEEA Group, 2021). Ocean accounts have been
44	argued to belong in the national statistical apparatus rather than in analysis, on the grounds that
45	recurrent policy reporting requires a production system and not a study (Fenichel et al., 2020).
46	Sub-national compilation across extent, service flows and ocean-economy production has been
47	demonstrated operationally (Gacutan et al., 2022). In Indonesia, the spatial basis such accounts
48	require has been tested empirically: grid size and designation rule change the extent estimate
49	materially (Rahayu et al., 2024). None of that work establishes whether an account compiled to those
50	specifications can carry the reporting load of a statutory index. That is a question about production
51	capacity, not about accounting method.
52	
53	Indonesia is where the policy demand is most explicit. Its ocean policy lineage runs from the 2017
54	Indonesian Ocean Policy through a development framework (BAPPENAS, 2021) to a blue economy roadmap
55	(BAPPENAS, 2024). A recent review of that lineage names limited data support among the obstacles to
56	implementation (Wuwung et al., 2024). Quantitative work on Indonesian blue economy indicators remains
57	thin. Rinaldi et al. (2025) construct their own sub-national index for Sumatera on a human development
58	index template and regress it on regional product. That is the closest existing treatment, and it is
59	not an analysis of the official index. The production system beneath the official index has not been
60	examined.
61	
62	The Indonesia Blue Economy Index (IBEI) is an official national development indicator under
63	presidential regulation Perpres 12/2025. Provincial targets for 2045 are assigned by joint circular of
64	the Ministry of Home Affairs and the Ministry of National Development Planning (BAPPENAS) for carriage
65	in long-term development plans. A province cannot decline to be scored, and BAPPENAS cannot decline to
66	score it. Against that recurring obligation, the compilation activity is registered in Indonesia's
67	statistical activity system as sectoral statistics gathered by secondary collection from the
68	publications of the Ministry of Marine Affairs and Fisheries (MMAF, Indonesian KKP) and of Statistics
69	Indonesia (BPS) (SIRUSA kegiatan 94936). The index conducts no primary collection of its own. It
70	inherits whatever those agencies publish, at whatever resolution they publish it. Section 2 documents
71	the traceability defects that follow.
72	
73	This study assesses Indonesia's ocean accounts as compilation infrastructure for an index the state is
74	already obliged to produce, rather than as an alternative source of indicator values. The assessment
75	is built on the IBEI's own published documentation, on the primary reports of the Indonesian account
76	pilots, and on the international accounting standard, with every claim tagged for maturity. It
77	contributes four things: a documented account of what makes provincial compilation hard, a three-tier
78	indicator mapping scored against the register's verbatim constituent data items rather than its
79	indicator names, a quantification of where in the index the account-relevant weight sits, and a
80	register of the conditions precedent for continued supply.
81	
82	Two boundaries hold throughout. Account compilation has its own statutory and analytical purposes
83	under MMAF's mandate, and supplying an index is one downstream use among several. Nothing here
84	proposes replacing the IBEI or subordinating either framework to the other; the relationship examined
85	is statistical infrastructure feeding a policy instrument. The object of study is bounded to the
86	44-indicator edition of the index, and every Indonesian ocean account output discussed carries maturity
87	tag (b), experimental or pilot compilation, with no statistical work-programme entry and no revision
88	policy.
89	
90	Three findings follow. First, read against verbatim constituent data items rather than indicator
91	names, the three ecosystem condition indicators are definitionally matched to account output rather
92	than category-mismatched. That corrects a judgement in earlier work, including a provisional mapping by
93	the present authors (Gacutan and Vandenbergh, 2025) that scored account-derivable shares at figures
94	moving from 61% to 39% to 2.8% as the assumed indicator set changed, the last over a 36-indicator
95	denominator the register does not have (Section 4.2). Second, what separates the account routes into and out of reach is not compilation
96	maturity but content. The routes Indonesia has compiled at pilot scale bear on 12.79% of the
97	composite's published weight and the one route compiled nowhere on a comparable 13.70%, the first
98	wholly containing the Tier A share above it; yet no compiled account produces the constituent data
99	item the register asks for at any of these rows (Section 4.3). Third, the share an account would itself supply is about 2.33%, and
100	what the mature account types offer at those rows is a production process rather than a stock of
101	substitutable values (Section 5).

104	# 2. The compilation problem
105	
106	A recurring statutory obligation to score every province annually sits on top of a compilation
107	activity that conducts no primary collection of its own. The obligation is assigned rather than
108	volunteered. Perpres 12/2025 carries the IBEI as an official national development indicator, and
109	joint circular SEB Mendagri/Bappenas No. 2/2024 distributes a 2045 target to each province for
110	carriage in its long-term development plan (Juknis, Tabel 2, p. 69 [pdf 79]). That the compilation
111	beneath it collects nothing directly is a design decision recorded in Indonesia's own statistical
112	metadata system, and its consequences are legible as specific strains in the published output. This
113	section establishes both from the IBEI's own documentation, and closes by declaring the pillar
114	structure the rest of the article computes on, since the handbook prints two.
115	
116	## 2.1 Compilation without collection
117	
118	SIRUSA registers the IBEI compilation as *Kompilasi Produk Administrasi* under *Statistik Sektoral*,
119	implemented by BAPPENAS's Directorate of Marine Affairs and Fisheries, with an empty *Identitas
120	Rekomendasi* (kegiatan 94936, retrieved 2026-08-13). The design is secondary compilation throughout.
121	Collection mode is *Pengumpulan data sekunder*, the instrument is *Lainnya: Publikasi KKP dan BPS*,
122	and the unit is the province. The register records no pilot survey, no non-response adjustment, no
123	staff training, zero supervisors and zero enumerators. The index's supply is therefore fixed to
124	whatever KKP and BPS have already published, at the resolution and vintage they published it.
125	
126	The collection capability the index draws on is real, and it sits with those two producers. That is
127	the statutory division this article holds to: BAPPENAS plans, whilst MMAF and BPS produce
128	statistics. What the register establishes is not an absence of capability in the system. It is the
129	absence of any collection instrument inside the compilation itself.
130	
131	The reporting window is narrow and is published from two directions. SIRUSA schedules collection
132	from 1 April to 1 May 2025 and dissemination from 1 August to 1 September 2025. The Juknis puts the
133	first phase of its monitoring and evaluation cycle in October to December of year *n* (p. 71 [pdf
134	81]). Read together, these imply that an upstream figure not public by roughly April of year *n+1*
135	cannot enter that cycle's index. Neither document reconciles them, and only the first phase was
136	extracted into the corpus, so the implication is stated conditionally `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Figure 1
137	draws the two calendars against the thirteen-step production chain they feed, and marks the single
138	step at which account-derived data could enter.

140	## 2.2 What the published output records
141	
142	Two defects carry the argument, and both are failures of provenance rather than of arithmetic.
143	
144	Gaps are filled by prediction, and the prediction is not recorded. Annex 1 of the Report specifies a
145	machine-learning framework predicting missing values from socio-economic proxies under two scenarios,
146	with SUSENAS named as the proxy source (pp. 64–66 [pdf 76–78]). The framework itself is specified in
147	detail. What is missing sits downstream of it: the annex names no imputed indicator, no count of
148	imputed cells, no selected model, no achieved error, and no proxy beyond SUSENAS. Under the first
149	scenario an indicator absent in every province is predicted for every province. No reader of any
150	published table can therefore distinguish a measured provincial value from a predicted one
151	`[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Which indicators were imputed must not be inferred from which look hard to collect.
152	
153	Three provinces record no value across three sub-pillars. Papua Barat Daya, Papua Selatan and Papua
154	Tengah each record 0.00 for marine-based industry, technology and governance, and Papua Tengah's
155	headline value of 8.51 is the lowest in the annex (Report p. 70 [pdf 82]). All three were created in
156	the 2022 provincial split. Juknis Tabel 2 carries the same three provinces at 2023 baselines of 49.25,
157	33.50 and 21.48 against 2025 achievements of 13.16, 12.25 and 8.51 (p. 69 [pdf 79]), and the two
158	documents head those columns differently. Whether the zeros reflect unavailability or a measured zero
159	is not stated in either document. They are reported as printed, and read as consistent with a
160	compilation discontinuity rather than as proof of one `[ASSUMPTION]`. This is where a new
161	administrative unit needs a spatially re-aggregable source rather than a republished provincial total.
162	
163	Table 1 records four further symptoms without arguing them here. Each is uninterrogable from the
164	published record.
165	
166	The denominator of the obligation is itself unstable inside the guidance that creates it. Indonesia
167	has 38 provinces. The Report's Annex 2 tabulates 37, Juknis Tabel 2 lists 38 rows plus a national
168	row with Papua Pegunungan carrying a dash in all three columns, the monitoring chapter asserts
169	comparability over *37 provinsi* (p. 71 [pdf 81]), and the sensitivity chapter thirty pages later
170	recomputes the index *untuk 38 provinsi* across 59 scenarios (p. 101 [pdf 111]). Two of the four are
171	in the same handbook. The number used in any sentence here is the number that sentence is about.

208	# 3. Materials and methods
209	
210	## 3.1 Design
211	
212	The study is a documentary analysis of two published specifications read against each other: the
213	IBEI register as printed by its custodian, and the account structures of the SEEA Ecosystem
214	Accounting standard together with the specified sequence of national ocean accounts. No sampling,
215	statistical inference or hypothesis test is involved, and no result reported here is an estimate
216	with a sampling distribution. The equivalent burden is reproducibility of a scoring rule and of an
217	arithmetic chain, which the deposited materials discharge.
218	
219	## 3.2 What an account has to be, for this test
220	
221	The membership rule below tests register rows against account output, so the account side needs
222	specifying first. Compilation infrastructure, as used here, means a production system that turns
223	primary observation into a statistical output on a repeating cycle, carrying its own spatial unit,
224	provenance chain and revision rule. Four properties do that work. They are this study's synthesis
225	rather than a construct the standard names as such, assembled from provisions it states separately.
226	
227	*A standing unit and classification definition* is fixed by the account types themselves. *A
228	balancing identity* renders an unexplained change a visible residual: closing stock equals opening
229	stock plus additions less reductions, with additions and reductions each split into managed and
230	unmanaged, so that the nature of a change is recorded rather than inferred (United Nations et al.,
231	2024, paras 4.14–4.15, p. 86). *A re-aggregable spatial basis* follows from basic spatial units
232	being "a means of implementing the ecosystem accounting approach rather than … elements in a nested
233	hierarchy" (App. A1.1, para. A1.3), with the reporting geography a compiler-delineated accounting
234	area; provincial and district figures are then two aggregations of one production run rather than
235	two data requests. *Documented vintage and revision* follows from the requirement that an account be
236	consistent over time and across classifications (para. 1.60, p. 18), and that previous estimates be
237	revised where updated imagery or methods permit a reassessment, with the size of the revision
238	recoverable as a difference (para. 4.17, p. 88).
239	
240	Two of the four are properties of a compiled account rather than of a well-run register. Section 5
241	concedes the other two.
242	
243	## 3.3 Corpus
244	
245	A citation-anchored corpus of 199 chunks was built over the index's own published documentation and
246	is released with this article under the Data availability statement, so every quotation resolves to a
247	numbered record rather than a page
248	range. Extraction was purposive. The build, dated 2026-08-14, comprises 106
249	chunks from the Juknis (BAPPENAS 2025a), 49 from the IBEI Report 2024 in its December 2025 revision
250	(BAPPENAS 2025b), 15 from Rinaldi et al. (2025) and 29 from the SIRUSA metadata (kegiatan 94936
251	together with indikator 105371 and 105709–105718, all retrieved 2026-08-13), at the printed-page
252	ranges listed in the deposit. Three rules governed it. A passage was included where it stated a
253	register row, a weight, a method step, a published provincial value or a metadata field of the
254	compilation; excluded where it was narrative framing, policy justification, or repetition of a passage
255	already captured; and extraction of a slice stopped when a further page yielded nothing meeting the
256	inclusion rule. The boundary is the four documents named, so the corpus is a census of what they state
257	on those five topics rather than a sample of a literature. Each record carries its document and slice,
258	the verbatim Indonesian text, an English gloss used nowhere as a quotation source, the printed page
259	with the PDF page in brackets, a maturity tag, and a reconstruction flag. That flag, on 17 records,
260	marks content assembled from a table the PDF text layer renders incompletely: the values are read from
261	the document but their layout is reconstructed, and no flagged record is the sole support for any claim
262	made here. Chunks in conflict are
263	recorded as conflicts rather than reconciled, and the four-against-three pillar contradiction is
264	held as one such record.
265	
266	Account-side evidence sits outside that corpus and was read directly: the three MMAF 2022 reports,
267	of which Gili Matra is the compilation (MMAF, 2022a, 2022b, 2022c); BPS SEEA-workshop decks
268	(Tasriah et al., 2022; Wulandari and Jannah, 2023); Rahayu et al. (2024); Kepmen KP 44/2023; and the
269	GOAP country page (retrieved 2026-08-13). All carry clean text layers; no optical character recognition
270	was applied. Retrieval and
271	assembly ran from 11 to 16 August 2026. A join table reproduces all 44 register rows with each row's
272	*Data Pembentuk Indikator* constituent data items verbatim, transcribed from Juknis Tabel 1, pp.
273	11–13 [pdf 21–23], totalling 62 items. That join table, not the running text of the handbook, is the
274	object the membership rule was applied to.
275	
276	Searching was bilingual and was run over the document set named above rather than over a statistical
277	catalogue. The Indonesian terms used were *indeks ekonomi biru*, *neraca laut*, *akun laut*, *neraca
278	ekosistem*, *unit spasial dasar laut*, *peta jalan ekonomi biru* and *Satu Data Indonesia*, with
279	their English equivalents and no date restriction. No dedicated search of the BPS statistical
280	catalogue or of the Satu Data Indonesia portal was undertaken, and by project constraint no
281	Indonesian agency was approached. Every absence claim in this article is therefore stated as unfound
282	within the named set, and is not a claim about the Indonesian statistical system. Within that set,
283	no Indonesian statistical register supplying provenance, spatial unit, vintage and revision policy
284	for marine ecosystem extent and condition products, separately from an account structure, was
285	encountered `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. That negative bears on the register objection weighed in Section 5.2.

287	## 3.4 Unit of analysis and the membership rule
288	
289	The unit is the register row, of which there are 44. The object tested is the row's constituent data
290	items rather than its title, on the Indonesian wording. Each row was graded on one of three grounds.
291	A row is *matched* where a constituent data item names a quantity that a defined SEEA Ecosystem
292	Accounting output contains. It is *partial* where account output supplies a component of the quantity
293	but not the quantity itself. It is a *provenance question* where account output bears on the row
294	without supplying it. Tier A is the union of the three grades and has five members: indicators 19, 20
295	and 21 matched, 24 partial, and 27 a provenance question. The remaining 39 rows are graded none.
296	
297	The rule was applied item by item, so a row carrying several items takes the highest grade any one
298	attains, which makes the 62 items rather than the 44 rows the tested population. Two decisions were
299	fixed before scoring: that indicator titles are not evidence of what a row requires, titles having
300	produced the predecessor figures this analysis rejects; and that a definitional match carries no
301	claim about whether anything has been compiled. Grading was by the authors against those criteria,
302	not blind, with no second coder and no adjudication protocol. Every row's grade and ground is
303	deposited as Supplementary Table S1.
304	
305	Membership in Tier A is a definitional property. The separate operative test applied to the account
306	side is repeatability into a dated window rather than demonstrated capability, since the obligation
307	is annual and can receive only what a producer has already released at the required vintage.
308	Maturity is tagged on every claim that something exists, as (a) published official statistics, (b)
309	experimental or pilot compilation, (c) methodology under development, or (d) announced but not
310	started. The tag describes the datum rather than the document reporting it.
311	
312	## 3.5 Weight arithmetic and its two ambiguities
313	
314	Weights are taken from the Juknis as printed: pillar weights from Tabel 4, p. 94 [pdf 104];
315	sub-pillar splits from Tabel 5, p. 94 [pdf 104]; variable weights from Tabel 6, pp. 95–96 [pdf
316	105–106]; and the sub-pillar shares of Gambar 11, p. 93 [pdf 103], used as an independent integer
317	cross-check. Shares of the composite are products of those published percentages, carried to two
318	decimal places from one-decimal inputs and reported in prose to one decimal with 'about'. Every
319	weight used here is printed; none is reconstructed. Where a total is built from component shares it
320	is the sum of the rounded components rather than the product of the unrounded inputs, which moves
321	three totals by 0.01 and is stated so either route is reproducible. All weight arithmetic follows
322	the three-pillar structure of Tabel 4, Tabel 5 and Gambar 11, for the stated reason that weights are
323	published only there, whilst register rows keep their Tabel 1 labels (Section 2.3). The choice is
324	material in one place and immaterial in another. Every indicator in the Tier A mapping sits in the
325	environment pillar under both structures, so Section 4.2's arithmetic is unaffected `[ASSUMPTION]`,
326	whilst Section 4.3's figures are computed over precisely the pillars across which indicators 15 to 18
327	move and are therefore structure-dependent, with no four-pillar equivalent available from the published
328	weights.
329	
330	The handbook does not state whether the percentages in Tabel 4 to Tabel 6 are raw
331	first-principal-component loadings or normalised contributions, noting only that *besaran pembobot
332	dihasilkan secara otomatis melalui proses PCA* (p. 93 [pdf 103]). The distinction matters because
333	raw loadings need not sum to anything in particular, so shares built by multiplying them would have
334	no defined denominator. The test applied is to renormalise each printed group to sum to 100 and
335	recompute. It changes almost nothing, for a reason that is a property of the tables rather than a
336	stipulation of this study: all fifteen printed weight groups already sum to 100.0 within one-decimal
337	rounding, none departing by more than a tenth of a point. The largest effect anywhere is 0.03
338	percentage points, moving the four Tier B sub-pillars from 26.15% to 26.12%; 18.45%, 7.64%, 3.58%,
339	2.33% and governance at 5.15% are unchanged, and Gambar 11's printed integers of 26 and 5 agree with
340	both. Fifteen independent groups of raw loadings would not sum to 100 by accident, so the
341	percentages are read here as normalised contributions and the derived shares are carried without a
342	denominator caveat. That inference is this study's reading of the tables and is stated nowhere in
343	the source `[ASSUMPTION]`. A second ambiguity is local rather than global and the census does not
344	reach it: indicator 38 is built from a spatial domain in Tabel 1 and a sectoral one in Tabel 6, which
345	moves the social share between 13.7% and about 14.0% (S4.1). The census is deposited as Supplementary
346	Data S2.
347	
348	## 3.6 Reporting conventions
349	
350	Indonesian decimal commas are preserved inside quotation and rendered as points in prose. Quotations
351	are from the Indonesian source text. Three inline flags mark evidential status: `[EVIDENCE GAP]`
352	where a required source was not found, `[ASSUMPTION]` where the argument depends on something
353	unconfirmed, and `[CONTESTED DENOMINATOR]` on every share of the composite, carrying one meaning
354	throughout and defined at the head of Section 4.2. It is not a flag about the weights, which are
355	printed. Conflicting sources are reported as conflicts and are not averaged or
356	adjudicated.

358	# 4. Results
359	
360	## 4.1 The compiled record
361	
362	Indonesian ocean accounting has produced one pilot compilation, in one marine protected area, in one
363	province, on one vintage. Every output carries maturity tag (b).
364	
365	The compiled record comprises the Gili Matra Marine Recreational Park pilot off north Lombok, together
366	with one peer-reviewed grid-optimisation study at Saleh Bay, Sumbawa (Rahayu et al., 2024). Both sit in
367	West Nusa Tenggara, so NTB is the single province carrying a documented published compilation located
368	by this study. A national Ocean Accounts Dashboard launched in July 2025 is described by GOAP as publicly available
369	and interactive on a harmonised spatial frame (GOAP country page, retrieved 2026-08-13) — a programme
370	self-statement about a platform, evidencing no compilation beyond the pilot record.
371	
372	The pilot's footprint belongs in its own units, because the smallness is part of the finding: 2,980.84
373	ha on a 25 m by 25 m marine basic spatial unit (MBSU) grid of 48,211 cells, opening in 2015 and closing
374	in 2021, those years chosen on data availability (MMAF, 2022a, pp. 30, 39 [pdf 44, 53]). The two figures
375	do not reconcile, the cell count implying 32.35 ha more than the footprint printed; S5.1 gives the
376	arithmetic, and the discrepancy is recorded as observed rather than resolved `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Against
377	a statutory obligation to score every province annually, this is roughly 30 km², compiled once.
378	
379	Maturity is the programme's own self-description rather than an external verdict. GOAP labels the three
380	2022 MMAF outputs 'preliminary consultation drafts', and BPS lists scaling up to the total Indonesian
381	marine area among its outstanding challenges (Tasriah et al., 2022, slide 9). There is no BPS ocean
382	account release, no entry in the national statistical work programme, no published revision policy, and
383	no published compilation cost per site or per unit. The ecosystem scope is confined to mangrove,
384	seagrass and coral reef.
385	
386	The three account types differ in completeness of the account form, not in the standing of the
387	compilation producing them. Extent is the only type with a verified opening, change and closing series:
388	the pilot publishes a complete asset account, with additions and reductions itemised and an
389	ecosystem-conversion matrix alongside (MMAF, 2022a, tab. 5–6, pp. 52–53 [pdf 66–67]). Condition is
390	compiled in a limited form, as cover-based measurement at Tier 1 to Tier 2 read against documented
391	reference bands rather than as a normalised multi-indicator composite (pp. 31–33 [pdf 45–47]). Valuation
392	is the least developed and may not be described as an ecosystem services account in physical units.
393	What exists is benefit-transferred monetary valuation, rising from IDR 45.24 bn per year in 2015 to IDR
394	64.17 bn in 2021 (pp. 36, 60 [pdf 50, 74]). Benefit transfer is not the exchange-value basis the
395	standard requires, a limit BPS states itself (Tasriah et al., 2022, slide 9). No ecosystem services
396	account in physical units has been compiled in Indonesia. Supplementary S5 reproduces all three.
397	
398	Extent is also where two official series diverge, and the divergence is this article's strongest
399	internal counter-instance. A BPS presentation reports the same pilot, ecosystems and years at
400	substantially lower areas (Tasriah et al., 2022, slide 3), although both series trace to one layer, the
401	Geospatial Information Agency's 2020 mapping validated by 2021 field survey. Neither document states
402	its relationship to the other and no reconciliation is published `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. The divergence is
403	not a constant offset: the BPS-to-MMAF ratio moves across ecosystems and across the accounting window
404	(S5.2). The two series therefore disagree on the change and not merely on the level, MMAF recording a
405	coral loss of 12.00 ha against a BPS loss of 18.26 ha. Change is the quantity a balancing identity
406	exists to report, and Section 5.2 interrogates the consequence.

408	## 4.2 Indicators the existing accounts can serve
409	
410	Five of the register's 44 rows are the whole of what an ocean account touches. Indicators 19, 20 and 21
411	name quantities an account's output structure already contains; indicators 24 and 27 sit adjacent to
412	account output and are disposed of rather than claimed (Table 2). Figure 2 gives the assignment for all
413	44 rows with the constituent data items in full.
414	
415	Four qualifications govern every share in this section and the two that follow. Each carries `[CONTESTED DENOMINATOR]`: it measures what these account types bear on, never
416	what an account would supply and never an achievable score improvement. Each is a share of nominal
417	published weight, which under this estimator need not track effective importance (Section 5.5). Every economy and
418	social figure — 26.1%, 5.15%, 4.11%, 1.34%, 0.31%, 7.20%, 6.29%, 13.70% — is structure-dependent on the
419	three-pillar locus of Section 3.5, with no four-pillar equivalent in the published weights. And the social pillar's indicators remain BPS outputs throughout, the
420	account contribution being a classification standard rather than a substitute source, so the wider
421	30.5% reading may be used only where it is named as the wider reading.
422	
423	On the compilation axis the concession is complete. None of the five indicator values has been
424	published by any Indonesian account, for any province or any site `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Every capability
425	claim here is a claim about definitional correspondence and a demonstrated input layer, never about an
426	existing output. All five rows sit in the environment pillar under both pillar structures, so the
427	unresolved conflict does not reach this arithmetic.
428	
429	The three condition indicators share one grammatical form. Indicator 19 asks for the *Persentase
430	kawasan terumbu karang dengan tutupan karang hidup antara 50-100% per provinsi*, indicator 20 for the
431	seagrass equivalent, and indicator 21 for *kerapatan tutupan* in mangrove (Juknis, Tabel 1, p. 12 [pdf
432	22]). Each names a percentage of area falling within a stated cover or density band, per province. That
433	is a tabulation of extent by class, not a normalised index against a reference condition. Earlier
434	assessments judged that Indonesia lacks the condition account these indicators require. They do not
435	require one, so the judgement answers a question the index does not pose.
436	
437	What the pilot sets beside that specification is the input layer, not the tabulation. The MBSU extent
438	surface exists, and cover is measured at survey stations: live coral cover of 18.11% in 2016 and live
439	hard coral cover of 16.99% in 2021, which the report does not state to be the same measure and which
440	are therefore not read here as a series, and seagrass cover averaging 32.28% with a station maximum of
441	82.01% (MMAF, 2022a, pp. 40, 45 [pdf 54, 59]). The 2021 coral figure is unreconciled in the source,
442	where the narrative reports 16.99% whilst the report's own tab. 3 on the same page prints 18.36%. The
443	cross-tabulation of extent by cover class is not published. The step from the input layer to indicator
444	19 is one tabulation that has not been performed.
445	
446	The match frays at four edges. Two are definitional. The index's 50% threshold matches no Indonesian
447	condition class in use, the pilot classifying reef on the bands of Giyanto et al. (2017); reclassifying
448	at 50% is trivial on a continuous per-cell surface but impossible where national data exist only as
449	classes pre-binned on those boundaries, which makes the coral row a definition gap rather than a
450	coverage gap `[ASSUMPTION]`. No obtained document evidences a wall-to-wall condition surface
451	`[EVIDENCE GAP]`. The mangrove unit differs outright: indicator 21 asks for canopy density as a
452	percentage, whilst the pilot reports m² per ha and individuals per ha, with no documented conversion
453	(p. 47 [pdf 61]) `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. S5.3 gives the measurement protocols and the reference bands.
454	
455	The other two edges are unfixed choices. Neither the minimum mapping unit nor the denominator is fixed
456	by the register or by the SIRUSA definitions `[ASSUMPTION]`. And the extent estimate is itself a
457	function of the grid rule: Rahayu et al. (2024) report mangrove error of 44.06% under a 25 m hierarchy
458	scheme against 1.59% under 25 m dominance, so until a grid rule is adopted the account's figure is a
459	family of figures. A vintage gap sits alongside these, the only verified series closing in 2021 against
460	the index's T-1 requirement.
461	
462	Indicator 24 asks for the *Total luas kawasan mangrove, rawa, dan lahan gambut yang direstorasi per
463	provinsi*, glossed in SIRUSA as degraded area subsequently replanted. That is an activity input measured
464	in hectares planted. The pilot's asset account carries a managed-expansion line that is unpopulated at
465	Gili Matra, where every addition is natural expansion and the only managed entry is 11.19 ha of managed
466	mangrove regression. The correspondence is structural rather than demonstrated: an account of this form
467	records realised managed change, whilst the indicator counts hectares planted. A scope mismatch
468	compounds it, the indicator's three land covers being mangrove, swamp and peatland against the marine
469	account's mangrove, seagrass and coral reef.
470	
471	Indicator 27 asks for the *Total luas kawasan konservasi perairan yang ditetapkan oleh KKP, KLHK, dan
472	Pemerintah Daerah per provinsi*, and is in the tier for a different reason. At 22.0% it is the largest
473	single variable in the resource-quality sub-pillar, about 4.06% of the composite, and it needs no
474	account to be produced. Designated area is an administrative datum already held by its custodians. The
475	account's near-term contribution here is reconciliation of mapped extent to the designated polygon, not
476	supply.
477	
478	The weight chain is shown openly as a multiplication. The environment pillar carries 36.9% of the
479	composite and the resource-quality sub-pillar 50.0% of the pillar (Juknis, Tabel 4 and Tabel 5, p. 94
480	[pdf 104]), giving 18.45%, of which the five Tier A variables sum to about 7.64%. Figure 3 places that
481	band inside the published weight structure. Most of the 7.64% sits at rows the mapping has itself
482	disqualified, so the decomposition is reported in three parts: about 2.33% at

484	indicator 27, which the account does not supply. **The share at which an account is the prospective
485	supply route is about 2.33%**, rising to about 3.58% only if indicator 24's structural correspondence
486	is credited in full, which the activity-against-outcome mismatch and the scope gap indicate it should
487	not be. The lower figure is operative and the upper an outer bound.

490	`[EVIDENCE GAP]`.

620	## 4.4 Conditions precedent for supply
621	
622	A definitional match is not a supply arrangement. Five conditions must hold before pilot account output
623	could serve the IBEI as a repeating input, none is satisfied in the obtained record, and they are not
624	equally binding (Table 4). Three are one-off acts of codification that a single decision by MMAF, BPS or
625	BAPPENAS could discharge. Two are discharged only by sustaining a production cycle, and those two
626	decide whether the mapping in Section 4.2 is a recommendation or a wish.
627	
628	The register would be refuted by producing an adopted national marine spatial standard, a BPS
629	statistical work-programme entry for a marine ecosystem account, or an extent compilation covering more
630	than the pilot sites and closing after 2021.
631	
632	The two recurring conditions are the binding ones. SIRUSA specifies reference period T-1 for all five
633	mapped variables. The single verified compilation closes at 2021 and was published the following year,
634	a lag T-1 can accommodate; what is absent is the second vintage, and no repeat compilation of the pilot
635	sites was found `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Figure 5 sets the required cycle against the vintages the accounts
636	have produced: the gap between the 2021 closing year and the reference year the 2025 cycle requires is
637	three reference years. The second is a revision policy, for which the standard supplies the substance
638	(paras 4.17–4.18, p. 88) and Indonesia supplies no instrument `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. An account silently
639	improved between vintages would move a province's index value with no ecosystem change behind it, and
640	nothing in the published record would flag it.
641	
642	The institutional condition is unmet against an instrument whose own term has run. Kepmen KP 44/2023
643	constituted the Tim Penyusun Neraca Sumber Daya Laut in February 2023 with a term expiring that
644	December, and no renewal was located `[EVIDENCE GAP]` — the defensible statement being that none was
645	found rather than that the mandate lapsed, since the July 2025 dashboard indicates activity under some
646	instrument not obtained. Two sectoral products feeding one another do not by themselves constitute an
647	accredited statistical input, and no microdata are released, so even a compliant supplier could not
648	verify that its value survived into the published score.
649	
650	One result bears on sequencing. Where a province has no account, an account-derived indicator would
651	itself be predicted from socio-economic proxies under the framework the Report publishes (pp. 64–66
652	[pdf 76–78]), so an environmental value would become partly a function of economic and social
653	covariates; and the framework's first scenario trains on the previous cycle's historical data, which a
654	newly introduced indicator does not have. Supplementary S3 carries the derivation for each condition.

672	# 5. Discussion
673	
674	## 5.1 What compilation changes, and what it does not
675	
676	The question was whether an account compiled to SEEA specifications can carry the reporting load of a
677	statutory index. The load is now quantified and it is small: five of 44 rows at 7.64% of
678	published weight, of which about 2.33% is what an account would itself supply. On that measure an
679	account cannot carry the load — and the measure is the wrong one. What an account offers at the rows
680	it touches is a production process, not a stock of substitutable values — a narrower claim than an
681	efficiency claim, and the evidence assembled here would not support the wider one.
682	
683	The register decomposes into 62 constituent data items across its 44 rows. Three of them —
684	indicators 19, 20 and 21 — are cross-classifications of one extent-and-condition compilation, so the
685	consolidation is three items into one production run, out of 62. The argument is not that the count
686	is large. It is that those three are among the items the index presently has no way to show it
687	measured rather than predicted.
688	
689	A cover-class percentage compiled from a gridded extent surface and a classified cover layer carries
690	its own spatial unit, its own opening and closing stocks, and a provenance chain back to the imagery
691	and field survey behind it. It is reproducible in the next cycle by repeating the compilation. A
692	value taken from a secondary publication, or predicted where no publication exists, carries none of
693	those properties, and the published record does not distinguish the two (Section 2.2).
694	
695	That the distinction is worth publishing is established outside Indonesia. The Ocean Health Index, a
696	composite of comparable construction in the same domain (Halpern et al., 2012), reports that 18.5%
697	of its global score rests on gapfilled rather than observed data, ranging from 1% to 43% across its
698	ten goals and exceeding 60% for ten regions that are predominantly small island territories. Its
699	authors recommend that gapfilling be tracked from the outset and presented in the same framework as
700	the scores themselves (Frazier et al., 2016). The IBEI publishes an imputation framework and no
701	per-indicator record, so the comparison identifies a reporting reform rather than a data programme —
702	and one the index could adopt without any account being compiled.
703	
704	Everything downstream of the input is untouched. Account supply changes the value entering a slot,
705	not the slot, its weight or the aggregation rule. The three-stage principal component procedure, the
706	loading-factor weights, the pillar shares and every limitation the index publishes about itself
707	stand exactly as they are.
708	
709	## 5.2 The counter-instance, and what survives it
710	
711	The strongest evidence against this article's own claim is in its own account-side record. The one
712	compiled Indonesian account has produced two closing stocks and two deltas for the same site, the
713	same three ecosystems and the same two years, disagreeing not by a constant offset but by a ratio
714	that moves across ecosystems and across the accounting window (Section 4.1). The pilot's own
715	reporting compounds it, the narrative giving 16.99% live hard coral cover for 2021 against 18.36% in
716	the report's table on the same page. Change is the quantity a balancing identity exists to report,
717	and provenance is what this article claims accounts buy.
718	
719	This is a disconfirming instance and is retained as one. It is not disposed of by observing that the two
720	series are parallel releases of one mapping layer and that neither is an account of the other: that
721	observation is true, and it is also unfalsifiable in the direction this article needs, since the only
722	compiled Indonesian instance would be reclassified out of the evidence base at the point where it tests
723	the claim. The narrower property claimed
724	here is not that compilation prevents divergence, which nothing in the standard promises, but that a
725	balancing identity gives divergence somewhere to appear — opening stock, additions, reductions and
726	closing stock are four entries that must reconcile, and a second compilation must restate rather than
727	replace. Gili Matra cannot confirm that property, because neither document was compiled under such an
728	identity. It cannot refute it either, for the same reason. What it does establish is the cost of the
729	absence: a 6.26 ha disagreement about coral change was published twice and reconciled nowhere. The
730	provenance property is therefore asserted from account form and is untested on the Indonesian record,
731	and it will remain untested until a compilation is revised under such a rule `[ASSUMPTION]`.
732	
733	A second objection bears on the same argument, and holds outright on two of the four properties. A
734	standing unit and classification definition is an act of codification — a published definition of the
735	mapping unit, the ecosystem classes and the landward and seaward limits — and nothing about compiling
736	an account is needed to write one down. Documented vintage and provenance is likewise a metadata
737	requirement any well-run register discharges. The spatial property is intermediate: a register can
738	mandate a grid and a minimum mapping unit but cannot produce the re-aggregable cell-level tabulation
739	the mandate presupposes. What does not reduce to codification is the residual — the balancing identity,
740	and the revision rule that alters the opening and closing entries themselves — both properties of
741	double-entry over a stock. The residual is thin, and saying so is more useful than defending a wider
742	claim. A reader who concludes that Indonesia should codify definitions before compiling anything has
743	read the evidence correctly, and the ordering follows from Section 4.4's own result: an indicator
744	inserted ahead of coverage would be imputed from socio-economic proxies in every province lacking an
745	account, so insertion should be phased behind compilation. That is a sequencing recommendation, not a
746	change to the index's method.
747	
748	## 5.3 What account supply does not fix
749	
750	The index's custodian has printed its own methodological limits, and account supply reaches none of
751	them. Four are conceded in the Report's own methods statement (Section 3.1.1, p. 34 [pdf 46]): an
752	assumption of linear and monotonic relationships, sensitivity to outliers, weights set on 2024 data
753	that *mungkin saja menjadi tidak relevan untuk tahun-tahun mendatang*, and a single weight structure
754	applied across a heterogeneous archipelago. Every one is a property of the estimator, whilst an
755	account changes the input. The first is worth dwelling on. The threshold relationship BAPPENAS
756	describes — the benefit of fishing intensity holding below a threshold whilst damage rises
757	exponentially above it — is exactly what an ecosystem account measures well, and better inputs
758	cannot reach a relationship the estimator represents linearly by design.
759	
760	Aggregation discards what the account was built to show. The constituent data items for indicators
761	19, 20 and 21 each end *per provinsi*, so an account compiled on a fine spatial grid loses its
762	within-province distribution at the moment it becomes an indicator. The account can tell a
763	provincial planner which reef complex is driving a change; the index can report only that a
764	percentage moved. That is a consequence of publishing the indicator without the grid `[ASSUMPTION]`,
765	so the remedy is an architecture in which both are released. The provincial boundary is
766	administrative and not ecological, and the standard leaves the accounting area to the compiler
767	(para. 2.12), so a reef complex straddling that line is divided by legal artefact. Accounts make the
768	division explicit and auditable; they do not make it ecologically meaningful.
769	
770	Net extent change cannot simply be inserted. The index states its own weighting rule: an indicator
771	with greater variability and strong correlation to others in its domain *akan secara alami
772	mendapatkan bobot yang lebih tinggi* (p. 29 [pdf 41]). A signed annual difference of a large, slowly
773	changing stock is low-variance by construction and can change sign between cycles, so it would attract
774	a small and unstable weight, and might meet the printed rule *Reversi nilai indikator (atau drop
775	indikator) jika ditemukan relasi negatif* (p. 30 [pdf 42]) — whether a sign-changing series triggers it
776	is not specified and is read here as an inference. The mechanism is general: under principal
777	components, weighting corrects for overlapping information rather than measuring importance (Nardo et
778	al., 2008, §6.1). Net extent change belongs beside the composite as a companion series, not inside it
779	as a forty-fifth indicator.
780	
781	Half the environment pillar measures something no ocean account should be built to measure. The
782	renewable-energy sub-pillar carries 50.0% of the pillar and contains no marine variable, being solar
783	and hydropower throughout (S4.3). No existing account can supply what it asks for, and building one
784	to do so would be an instrument error. The coverage claim stops at that boundary, and stopping is
785	what makes it credible.
786	
787	## 5.4 Loose coupling, and three readings that would lead elsewhere
788	
789	Where substitution is not warranted, and on the published maturity evidence it is not, the
790	arrangement that delivers the gain is loose coupling. It has three requirements: shared indicator
791	definitions at the cover-class thresholds the Juknis already prints, with the unit and denominator
792	rules the register leaves open settled with its custodian; a shared spatial unit that aggregates to
793	the province; and parallel publication of the account alongside the index. It is defined as much by
794	what it does not require — no institutional transfer, no change to the index's data pipeline, and no
795	dependency of a statutory index on an experimental compilation. Parallel publication is a combined
796	presentation (paras 14.56–14.57) and is the nearer term of the two, whilst sub-national compilation
797	follows from the accounting area being definable at a subnational administrative boundary (para.
798	2.12) and has been demonstrated at that scale elsewhere (Gacutan et al., 2022). Loose coupling has
799	the property that a compilation cycle missing the window fails without the index failing with it.
800	
801	The institutional division holds at the point of recommendation, where it is most at risk. BAPPENAS is
802	*pengampu* of the calculation whilst the constituent data are provincial (Juknis, p. 68 [pdf 78]), so
803	nothing here asks either institution to take on the other's mandate. The arrangement would have to be
804	settled by whichever body now holds the coordinating function, the team constituted under Kepmen KP
805	44/2023 having had its term expire with no renewal instrument located.
806	
807	Four readings of the same record would lead elsewhere. The first is the position this article's whole
808	premise contests: that secondary compilation is adequate for a policy composite provided the
809	uncertainty it introduces is quantified and published. It is the cheaper answer and on three of this
810	article's four properties a good one, and Frazier et al. (2016) show the Ocean Health Index doing it,
811	publishing gapfilled shares goal by goal. What it cannot supply is the quantity to be uncertain about:
812	the register asks for a share of provincial area within a cover band, which no Indonesian source
813	publishes at any uncertainty (Section 4.2), and an uncertainty statement over an imputed value
814	describes the imputation rather than the reef. The position is right about most of the index and silent
815	about the rows at issue here — and adopting it would cost nothing to try, since the per-indicator
816	imputation record it needs is the reform recommended at Section 5.1 and depends on no account being
817	compiled. Second, Rinaldi et al. (2025) construct an alternative index on a
818	human development index template, treating the problem as one of index design
819	rather than of the production system beneath it; on that reading the effort recommended here is
820	misdirected. Third, Wuwung et al. (2024) locate the constraint on Indonesian ocean policy in
821	institutional fragmentation across mandates. That concession is accepted here and it reorders Section
822	4.4: an inter-agency supply arrangement is the hardest of the conditions precedent, not the cheapest,
823	and the three acts of codification should be read as the tractable ones precisely because each sits
824	inside a single mandate. Fourth, Ravallion (2012) defines a mashup index as a
825	composite for which theory and practice provide little guidance on design, leaving the producer free
826	to set an unusually large number of moving parts, and doubts the value added of such indices
827	relative to monitoring the components separately. The answer this record supports is statutory
828	rather than methodological: the IBEI is scored for every province annually under Perpres 12/2025, so
829	the dashboard alternative is not in BAPPENAS's gift, and what supplies the composite is a live
830	question whether or not an aggregate is the instrument a statistician would have chosen. No source in
831	the set searched argues that accounts cannot serve as statistical infrastructure for a composite index,
832	which is a statement about a bilingual search over a named document set rather than about the
833	literature (Section 3.3).
834	
835	## 5.5 Limitations
836	
837	Every share reported here is a nominal share of printed weight, not a measure of effective
838	influence. Nardo et al. (2008, §6.1) hold that under principal components, weighting is not a
839	measure of the theoretical importance of the associated indicator. Paruolo et al. (2013) measure
840	what each variable actually contributes to five composites, the human development index among them,
841	and find that because socio-economic variables are heteroscedastic and correlated, relative nominal
842	weights hardly ever match relative effective importance. The IBEI is a composite over correlated
843	provincial socio-economic variables with weights taken from first-component loadings, which is the
844	configuration in which that divergence was found. Becker et al. (2017) supply the test that would
845	settle it, a correlation-ratio measure of each variable's importance with confidence intervals. It
846	cannot be run here, because it requires the provincial variable matrix beneath the index, which is
847	unpublished `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. What does not survive should be named as plainly as what does.
848	What does not survive is the reading of 7.64%, 2.33%, 12.79% and 13.70% as measures of consequence:
849	they are shares of nominal published weight, and nominal weight is not importance under this estimator.
850	They remain exact statements of what the handbook prints and of what an account bears on, which is the
851	use made of them here. What survives is the production-process case, which never rested on the
852	shares.
853	
854	Six further limitations bear on specific figures. Whether Tabel 4 to Tabel 6's percentages are raw
855	loadings or normalised contributions is not stated anywhere, and the reading adopted here — that
856	they are normalised, because all fifteen printed groups sum to 100.0 within rounding — is an
857	inference from the tables rather than a statement in the source (Section 3.5). Were it wrong, the
858	derived shares would lose their denominator rather than move. The printed aggregation formulae are
859	illegible in the PDF text layer at Juknis pp. 64–65 and Report pp. 31–32, so what is reconstructed
860	is the notation, at medium-high confidence, rather than the method. The per-indicator imputation
861	record is unpublished, which is the largest single unknown, because it determines how much of any
862	published provincial value is measurement and how much is prediction. The mapping is a single-coder assessment against
863	stated criteria, graded by the authors and not blind, with no second coder and no adjudication
864	protocol. Deposit secures auditability, not reliability, and the two are not substitutes: the same
865	team's earlier mapping moved from 61% to 39% to 2.8% as the assumed indicator set changed, which is
866	direct evidence that a grading of this kind is unstable under a change of premise. An
867	author-independent re-grade of all five Tier A rows and a random fifteen of the thirty-nine, reporting
868	raw disagreements, is the check this study has not run. It is why every row's
869	score and exclusion ground is deposited. Compilation cost is absent from the public record at every
870	site. And this study engaged no Indonesian agency, by project constraint, so the absence claims
871	above are conditions to live with rather than future work.
872	
873	# 6. Conclusion
874	
875	Both findings point the same way, and it is not the way the question is usually put. Asking how much of
876	a composite an ocean account could fill invites an answer in shares, and shares are what this evidence
877	supports least: they are nominal weight, which need not track effective importance under this
878	estimator, and the largest of them sit at rows an account does not supply. Asking how the indicators it
879	touches would be produced gives an answer the evidence does support, because that question is settled
880	by documents rather than weights. On it the record is unambiguous: every route runs through content the
881	register specifies and no compiled account produces, so compilation maturity turns out not to be what
882	separates them.
883	
884	The binding constraint on acting on this is not methodological. No compilation cost appears anywhere in
885	the record obtained for this study — no staff-day figure, no budget line, no imagery cost and no
886	field-survey cost, at any site `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. A province-by-province annual obligation is a scope
887	decision before it is a method decision. This study declines to estimate a cost, since an
888	order-of-magnitude range would be read as a figure.
889	
890	Two follow-on studies suggest themselves. The claim that indicators 19, 20 and 21 are definitionally
891	matched to account output is empirically testable today at exactly one site. Re-tabulating the Gili
892	Matra compilation to the index's own 50% to 100% cut would settle whether an account's extent and cover
893	layers yield the register's class-share form at all, and would generate the first real compilation-cost
894	observation. Its limits are set by the record itself: the coral and seagrass cover behind that
895	compilation was surveyed at stations rather than mapped per cell, the mangrove row is blocked on an
896	undocumented unit conversion, and a footprint of 2,980.84 ha supports no comparison against a published
897	provincial value. Extending the tabulation to NTB as a whole would test provincial resolution, and
898	requires the province-wide compilation that does not exist. The second output is practitioner-facing
899	rather than research: no published guidance sets out how a sub-national marine ecosystem account should
900	be compiled to serve a provincial indicator in an Indonesian setting. Both assume continued external
901	technical assistance to the Indonesian programme, which is an assumption rather than an established
902	condition `[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
903	
904	What would falsify the argument is worth stating, because a paper of this shape is otherwise
905	structurally unfalsifiable. The first arm is testable now: if a single-province compilation reproduces
906	the published provincial value no better than the current secondary-publication route, the
907	production-process case fails on its own terms. The second arm is cost, which cannot be a threshold whilst no
908	compilation cost has ever been published; naming one would be the invented figure this study declines
909	to supply. It becomes testable once the first observation exists, which is why the re-tabulation above
910	is worth running for its cost record as much as its result. The test is then whether cost per province
911	exceeds what the index's existing secondary-compilation cycle spends per province, a quantity BAPPENAS
912	holds and has not released `[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
913	
914	Within the statutory boundary held throughout, what this study describes is a capacity gap under a
915	statutory obligation, in a country that has demonstrated the production process the obligation would
916	require at exactly one experimental site of 2,980.84 ha, on one vintage now three reference years
917	stale, and with the method limits recorded above. Whether that process scales to 38 provinces annually
918	is not shown here and cannot be shown from one site.

1257	# Supplementary Table S1. The item-level scoring sheet
1258	
1259	Every constituent data item of the Indonesia Blue Economy Index register — 62 items across 44 rows —
1260	with the grade of the row it belongs to and the ground on which that grade was reached. The register,
1261	its indicator titles and its *Data Pembentuk Indikator* items are transcribed from Juknis
1262	(BAPPENAS 2025a) Tabel 1, pp. 11–13 [pdf 21–23]. Pillar labels are Tabel 1's, which prints four
1263	pillars including *Enabler*; all weight arithmetic in the article follows the three-pillar structure of
1264	Tabel 4, Tabel 5 and Gambar 11, and rows 15 to 18 move between pillars depending on which table is
1265	followed (Section 3.5).
1266	
1267	The item, not the row, is the unit the membership rule was applied to (Section 3.4). A row takes the
1268	highest grade any one of its items attains, so where a row carries several items the **deciding item**
1269	column marks the one that set the grade; rows graded *none* have no deciding item, the grade holding
1270	for every item.
1271	
1272	Grades: **matched** (an item names a quantity a defined SEEA-EA account output contains), **partial**
1273	(account output supplies a component but not the quantity), **provenance question** (account output
1274	bears on the item without supplying it), **none**. Tier A is the union of the first three and has five
1275	members. The tier column records the extension route discussed in Section 4.3 for rows graded none.
1276	
1277	Grading was by the authors against the stated criteria, not blind, with no second coder and no
1278	adjudication protocol, and is an assessment rather than an independently verifiable quantity
1279	(Section 5.5). The items are reproduced verbatim so that a reader may reject a grade and recompute.

1452	## S3.1 A national spatial standard
1453	
1454	Provincial account output is not comparable across provinces or across vintages until one minimum
1455	mapping unit and one basic spatial unit are adopted nationally. This is an act of codification,
1456	discharged once, requiring no new compilation.
1457	
1458	The standard requires an account to be consistent over time and with respect to concepts and
1459	classifications (United Nations et al., 2024, para. 1.60), and requires previous estimates to be
1460	revised for continuity of the time series wherever updated data, methods or classifications permit a
1461	reassessment of area (para. 4.17). Grid-based spatial units are the mechanism by which an account
1462	aggregates to any administrative boundary, conditional on the grid existing and being national.
1463	
1464	No adopted national marine grid or minimum mapping unit was found in any obtained Indonesian document
1465	`[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Rahayu et al. (2024), the only peer-reviewed Indonesian account source, state that a
1466	standardised grid has yet to be established, and the pilot literature records different resolutions at
1467	different sites — the case the rule exists to prevent. Grid choice changes the extent tabulation
1468	itself: mangrove error of 44.06% under a 25 m hierarchy scheme against 1.59% under 25 m dominance. Until
1469	a grid rule is adopted, the account's figure is a family of figures, and the index would be importing a
1470	methodological choice.
1471	
1472	## S3.2 A codified clipping rule
1473	
1474	Provincial marine authority extends to 12 nautical miles under Law 23/2014, cited here at second hand
1475	and not read at source `[EVIDENCE GAP]`, so an account reaching further must be clipped before it can
1476	yield a provincial number.
1477	
1478	The standard leaves the accounting area to the compiler (para. 2.12) and supplies no sub-national
1479	allocation rule for an asset spanning a boundary. The convention this study takes as necessary —
1480	allocation by physical extent within each side, with condition compiled per spatial unit rather than
1481	inherited whole — is a specification rather than a citation `[ASSUMPTION]`. Neither it nor the
1482	statutory line is codified in any account obtained.
1483	
1484	One consequence follows from the standard directly. Condition is not additive across areas, its
1485	variable account being an area-weighted arithmetic mean over the assets of an ecosystem type (para.
1486	5.54, p. 105). Because indicators 19, 20 and 21 ask for a ratio, a provincial value cannot be recovered
1487	by averaging district values, and a supplier would need to publish the denominator alongside the
1488	percentage for a provincial value to be recoverable.
1489	
1490	Clean nesting of cell boundaries across administrative, fisheries-management and marine-spatial-planning
1491	frames is asserted rather than evidenced `[ASSUMPTION]`, and forms part of this condition rather than a
1492	property that may be assumed.
1493	
1494	## S3.3 A repeated compilation inside the reporting window
1495	
1496	SIRUSA specifies reference period T-1 for all five mapped variables (19, 20, 21, 24, 27). The single
1497	verified Indonesian compilation closes at 2021 and was published the following year, a lag T-1 can
1498	accommodate. What is absent is the second vintage. One compilation, however timely, cannot meet a T-1
1499	requirement in any subsequent cycle, and no repeat compilation of the pilot sites was found
1500	`[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
1501	
1502	This condition is recurring rather than one-off. It is discharged by running the cycle again, not by a
1503	decision, and it survives the index's own inconsistent rendering of its reference periods
1504	`[ASSUMPTION]`. Sustaining a cycle is a resourcing question, and no compilation cost was located in the
1505	public record, so no condition in this register can be priced.
1506	
1507	## S3.4 A revision policy
1508	
1509	The standard already supplies the substance: revision of previous estimates for continuity of the
1510	series, recording no distinct revision entry but altering the opening, closing, addition and reduction
1511	entries themselves, with the size of a revision shown as the difference between the historical and the
1512	revised account for the same period (paras 4.17–4.18, p. 88).
1513	
1514	What is unwritten is the Indonesian instrument that would designate a baseline year and disclose where
1515	the latest extent data refer to an earlier period. The index's own principle of controlled adaptability
1516	presupposes that discipline on the supply side. No revision policy was found for any Indonesian ocean
1517	account output in the searched set `[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
1518	
1519	The consequence is specific. An account silently improved between vintages would move a province's
1520	index value with no ecosystem change behind it, and nothing in the published record would flag it. The
1521	instrument requires no new compilation: it is a statement of when a series is restated and how a
1522	provisional estimate is marked.
1523

1670	ha against a BPS loss of 18.26 ha, a 6.26 ha disagreement.

1672	## S5.3 Condition measurement
1673	
1674	Coral was measured by underwater photo transect with CPCe analysis following Muttaqin et al. (2020),
1675	seagrass by the Seagrass Watch protocol, which MMAF attributes to Yulianto et al. (2012),
1676	and mangrove by plot sampling (MMAF, 2022a, pp. 31–33 [pdf
1677	45–47]). Values are classified against published reference levels: 16.99% live hard coral cover in 2021
1678	against the bands of Giyanto et al. (2017), and mangrove classified as good at a density of at least
1679	1,500 individuals per hectare under Ministry of Environment Decree 201/2004 (pp. 40, 47 [pdf 54, 61]).
1680	
1681	Two facts are recorded without adjudication. The pilot reports mean cover by zone rather than share of
1682	area by class, so producing indicators 19, 20 and 21 from it would require re-tabulating the underlying
1683	transect and grid data to a different class boundary `[ASSUMPTION]`. And 16.99% cover is reported, then
1684	classified as moderate against a band running from 19% to 35%.

1686	## S5.4 Benefit-transfer sources
1687	
1688	Coral values are transferred from Bohol (Samonte-Tan et al., 2007) and the Mariana Islands (van
1689	Beukering et al., 2006), seagrass from Mexico, and mangrove from Gazi Bay, Kenya (Hoberg, 2011) and
1690	Bintuni Bay, Indonesia (Ruitenbeek, 1992). Pilot totals rise from IDR 45.24 bn per year in 2015 to IDR
1691	64.17 bn per year in 2021 (pp. 36, 60 [pdf 50, 74]).
1692	
1693	Benefit transfer is not the exchange-value basis the SEEA Ecosystem Accounting standard requires, so no
1694	exchange-value or depreciation claim can rest on it — a limit BPS states itself in listing the absence
1695	of implemented standard valuation methods among its challenges (Tasriah et al., 2022, slide 9). On this
1696	evidence, no ecosystem services account in physical units has been compiled in Indonesia.
