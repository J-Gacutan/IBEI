1	# Ocean accounts as compilation infrastructure for a blue economy index
2	
3	## Abstract
4	
5	Indonesia is statutorily obliged to score every province annually on a 44-indicator blue economy
6	composite that, on its own registration, conducts no primary collection and compiles entirely from
7	published sectoral statistics. This study assesses Indonesia's ocean accounts as compilation
8	infrastructure for that index rather than as an alternative indicator source. Two published
9	specifications are read against each other: the index register as printed in its 2025 technical
10	handbook, and the account structures of the SEEA Ecosystem Accounting standard. Each of the register's
11	44 rows is scored against the verbatim constituent data items that define it, with every claim
12	maturity-tagged. Reading data items rather than indicator names corrects prior assessments. Three
13	condition indicators ask for the share of provincial area within a stated cover band, a tabulation of
14	extent by class rather than the index against reference condition earlier work found missing. Five rows
15	are the whole of what an account touches, carrying 7.64% of the composite's published weight, of which
16	about 2.33% is the share an account would itself supply. What separates the account routes into and out
17	of reach is content rather than compilation maturity: routes compiled at pilot scale bear on 12.79% and
18	the one route compiled nowhere on 13.70%, yet no compiled account produces the data item the register
19	asks for at any of them. All shares are contingent on an undocumented ambiguity in how the published
20	weights are expressed. What accounting offers such an index is a production process — a standing
21	classification, a balancing identity, a re-aggregable spatial basis and a documented revision rule —
22	rather than a stock of substitutable values. What an accounting
23	system can carry for a statutory composite is now a general question.
24	
25	
26	# 1. Introduction
27	
28	Composite indices are a standard instrument of ocean governance. They convert heterogeneous marine
29	statistics into a single number that ministries can target, budget against and report. Their
30	credibility rests less on indicator design than on the production system beneath them, because an
31	index can only be as traceable as the statistics it consumes. That dependency is rarely examined.
32	
33	The form is well established. The Ocean Health Index scores every coastal country against ten goals
34	for a coupled human–ocean system (Halpern et al., 2012). The methodological difficulties are
35	documented rather than novel: the standard reference treats imputation, weighting and aggregation as
36	the three choices that most move a published ranking (Nardo et al., 2008). The Indonesian case
37	therefore belongs to a recognised class of measurement problem.
38	
39	Ocean accounting has developed as the candidate infrastructure for that problem. The System of
40	Environmental-Economic Accounting Ecosystem Accounting framework was adopted by the UN Statistical
41	Commission in 2021 as an international statistical standard. It supplies the extent, condition,
42	service and asset structures on which such accounts are built (United Nations et al., 2024), and a
43	complete national sequence has been specified in detail (IDEEA Group, 2021). Ocean accounts have been
44	argued to belong in the national statistical apparatus rather than in analysis, on the grounds that
45	recurrent policy reporting requires a production system and not a study (Fenichel et al., 2020).
46	Sub-national compilation across extent, service flows and ocean-economy production has been
47	demonstrated operationally (Gacutan et al., 2022). In Indonesia, the spatial basis such accounts
48	require has been tested empirically: grid size and designation rule change the extent estimate
49	materially (Rahayu et al., 2024). None of that work establishes whether an account compiled to those
50	specifications can carry the reporting load of a statutory index. That is a question about production
51	capacity, not about accounting method.
52	
53	Indonesia is where the policy demand is most explicit. Its ocean policy lineage runs from the 2017
54	Indonesian Ocean Policy through a development framework (BAPPENAS, 2021) to a blue economy roadmap
55	(BAPPENAS, 2024). A recent review of that lineage names limited data support among the obstacles to
56	implementation (Wuwung et al., 2024). Quantitative work on Indonesian blue economy indicators remains
57	thin. Rinaldi et al. (2025) construct their own sub-national index for Sumatera on a human development
58	index template and regress it on regional product. That is the closest existing treatment, and it is
59	not an analysis of the official index. The production system beneath the official index has not been
60	examined.
61	
62	The Indonesia Blue Economy Index (IBEI) is an official national development indicator under
63	presidential regulation Perpres 12/2025. Provincial targets for 2045 are assigned by joint circular of
64	the Ministry of Home Affairs and the Ministry of National Development Planning (BAPPENAS) for carriage
65	in long-term development plans. A province cannot decline to be scored, and BAPPENAS cannot decline to
66	score it. Against that recurring obligation, the compilation activity is registered in Indonesia's
67	statistical activity system as sectoral statistics gathered by secondary collection from the
68	publications of the Ministry of Marine Affairs and Fisheries (MMAF, Indonesian KKP) and of Statistics
69	Indonesia (BPS) (SIRUSA kegiatan 94936). The index conducts no primary collection of its own. It
70	inherits whatever those agencies publish, at whatever resolution they publish it. Section 2 documents
71	the traceability defects that follow.
72	
73	This study assesses Indonesia's ocean accounts as compilation infrastructure for an index the state is
74	already obliged to produce, rather than as an alternative source of indicator values. The assessment
75	is built on the IBEI's own published documentation, on the primary reports of the Indonesian account
76	pilots, and on the international accounting standard, with every claim tagged for maturity. It
77	contributes four things: a documented account of what makes provincial compilation hard, a three-tier
78	indicator mapping scored against the register's verbatim constituent data items rather than its
79	indicator names, a quantification of where in the index the account-relevant weight sits, and a
80	register of the conditions precedent for continued supply.
81	
82	Two boundaries hold throughout. Account compilation has its own statutory and analytical purposes
83	under MMAF's mandate, and supplying an index is one downstream use among several. Nothing here
84	proposes replacing the IBEI or subordinating either framework to the other; the relationship examined
85	is statistical infrastructure feeding a policy instrument. The object of study is bounded to the
86	44-indicator edition of the index, and every Indonesian ocean account output discussed carries maturity
87	tag (b), experimental or pilot compilation, with no statistical work-programme entry and no revision
88	policy.
89	
90	Three findings follow. First, read against verbatim constituent data items rather than indicator
91	names, the three ecosystem condition indicators are definitionally matched to account output rather
92	than category-mismatched. That corrects a judgement in earlier work, including a provisional mapping by
93	the present authors that scored account-derivable shares at figures moving from 61% to 39% to 2.8% as
94	the assumed indicator set changed, the last over a 36-indicator denominator the register does not have
95	(Section 4.2). Second, what separates the account routes into and out of reach is not compilation
96	maturity but content. The routes Indonesia has compiled at pilot scale bear on 12.79% of the
97	composite's published weight and the one route compiled nowhere bears on 13.70%, a difference of
98	roughly 1.1 times; yet no compiled account produces the constituent data item the register asks for at
99	any of these rows (Section 4.3). Third, the share an account would itself supply is about 2.33%, and
100	what the mature account types offer at those rows is a production process rather than a stock of
101	substitutable values (Section 5).

104	# 2. The compilation problem
105	
106	A recurring statutory obligation to score every province annually sits on top of a compilation
107	activity that conducts no primary collection of its own. The obligation is assigned rather than
108	volunteered. Perpres 12/2025 carries the IBEI as an official national development indicator, and joint
109	circular SEB Mendagri/Bappenas No. 2/2024 distributes a 2045 target to each province for carriage in
110	its long-term development plan (Juknis, Tabel 2, p. 69 [pdf 79]). That the compilation beneath it
111	collects nothing directly is a design decision recorded in Indonesia's own statistical metadata system,
112	and its consequences are legible as specific strains in the published output. This section establishes
113	both from the IBEI's own documentation, and closes by declaring the pillar structure the rest of the
114	article computes on, since the handbook prints two.
115	
116	## 2.1 Compilation without collection
117	
118	SIRUSA registers the IBEI compilation as *Kompilasi Produk Administrasi* under *Statistik Sektoral*,
119	implemented by BAPPENAS's Directorate of Marine Affairs and Fisheries, with an empty *Identitas
120	Rekomendasi* (kegiatan 94936, retrieved 2026-08-13). The design is secondary compilation throughout.
121	Collection mode is *Pengumpulan data sekunder*, the instrument is *Lainnya: Publikasi KKP dan BPS*, and
122	the unit is the province. The register records no pilot survey, no non-response adjustment, no staff
123	training, zero supervisors and zero enumerators. The index's supply is therefore fixed to whatever KKP
124	and BPS have already published, at the resolution and vintage they published it.
125	
126	The collection capability the index draws on is real, and it sits with those two producers. That is
127	the statutory division this article holds to: BAPPENAS plans, whilst MMAF and BPS produce statistics.
128	What the register establishes is not an absence of capability in the system. It is the absence of any
129	collection instrument inside the compilation itself.
130	
131	The reporting window is narrow and is published from two directions. SIRUSA schedules collection from
132	1 April to 1 May 2025 and dissemination from 1 August to 1 September 2025. The Juknis puts the first
133	phase of its monitoring and evaluation cycle in October to December of year *n* (p. 71 [pdf 81]). Read
134	together, these imply that an upstream figure not public by roughly April of year *n+1* cannot enter
135	that cycle's index. Neither document reconciles them, and only the first phase was extracted into the
136	corpus, so the implication is stated conditionally `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Figure 1 draws the two calendars
137	against the thirteen-step production chain they feed, and marks the single step at which account-derived
138	data could enter.

140	## 2.2 What the published output records
141	
142	Two defects carry the argument, and both are failures of provenance rather than of arithmetic.
143	
144	Gaps are filled by prediction, and the prediction is not recorded. Annex 1 of the Report specifies a
145	machine-learning framework predicting missing values from socio-economic proxies under two scenarios,
146	with SUSENAS named as the proxy source (pp. 64–66 [pdf 76–78]). The framework itself is specified in
147	detail. What is missing sits downstream of it: the annex names no imputed indicator, no count of
148	imputed cells, no selected model, no achieved error, and no proxy beyond SUSENAS. Under the first
149	scenario an indicator absent in every province is predicted for every province. No reader of any
150	published table can therefore distinguish a measured provincial value from a predicted one
151	`[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Which indicators were imputed must not be inferred from which look hard to collect.
152	
153	Three provinces record no value across three sub-pillars. Papua Barat Daya, Papua Selatan and Papua
154	Tengah each record 0.00 for marine-based industry, technology and governance, and Papua Tengah's
155	headline value of 8.51 is the lowest in the annex (Report p. 70 [pdf 82]). All three were created in
156	the 2022 provincial split. Juknis Tabel 2 carries the same three provinces at 2023 baselines of 49.25,
157	33.50 and 21.48 against 2025 achievements of 13.16, 12.25 and 8.51 (p. 69 [pdf 79]), and the two
158	documents head those columns differently. Whether the zeros reflect unavailability or a measured zero
159	is not stated in either document. They are reported as printed, and read as consistent with a
160	compilation discontinuity rather than as proof of one `[ASSUMPTION]`. This is where a new
161	administrative unit needs a spatially re-aggregable source rather than a republished provincial total.
162	
163	Table 1 records four further symptoms without arguing them here. Each is uninterrogable from the
164	published record.
165	
166	The denominator of the obligation is itself unstable inside the guidance that creates it. Indonesia has
167	38 provinces. The Report's Annex 2 tabulates 37, Juknis Tabel 2 lists 38 rows plus a national row with
168	Papua Pegunungan carrying a dash in all three columns, and the monitoring chapter asserts comparability
169	over *37 provinsi* (p. 71 [pdf 81]). The number used in any sentence here is the number that sentence
170	is about.

172	## 2.3 The pillar conflict, and the locus this article adopts
173	
174	The handbook prints two pillar structures. Tabel 1 prints four, with an *Enabler (teknologi & tata
175	kelola)* pillar holding indicators 15 to 18. Tabel 4, Tabel 5 and Gambar 11 print three, placing
176	tourism, technology and governance as economy sub-pillars. This article does not resolve the conflict.
177	All weight arithmetic here follows the three-pillar structure, for the stated reason that weights are
178	published only there, whilst register rows keep their Tabel 1 labels.
179	
180	The choice is material in one place and immaterial in another. Every indicator in the Tier A mapping
181	sits in the environment pillar under both structures, so Section 4.2's arithmetic is unaffected
182	`[ASSUMPTION]`. Section 4.3's figures are computed over precisely the pillars across which indicators
183	15 to 18 move: the economy pillar's account-relevant share of about 26.1% and the governance share of
184	about 5.15% are structure-dependent, and no four-pillar equivalent can be given from the published
185	weights. Both are identified as structure-dependent at each use.
186	
187	A second contradiction bears on how any published value should be read. One printed page asserts both

214	# 3. Materials and methods
215	
216	## 3.1 Design
217	
218	The study is a documentary analysis of two published specifications read against each other: the IBEI
219	register as printed by its custodian, and the account structures of the SEEA Ecosystem Accounting
220	standard together with the specified sequence of national ocean accounts. No sampling, statistical
221	inference or hypothesis test is involved, and no result reported here is an estimate with a sampling
222	distribution. The equivalent burden is reproducibility of a scoring rule and of an arithmetic chain,
223	which the deposited materials discharge.
224	
225	## 3.2 What an account has to be, for this test
226	
227	The membership rule below tests register rows against account output, so the account side needs
228	specifying first. Compilation infrastructure, as used here, means a production system that turns
229	primary observation into a statistical output on a repeating cycle, carrying its own spatial unit,
230	provenance chain and revision rule. Four properties do that work. They are this study's synthesis
231	rather than a construct the standard names as such, assembled from provisions it states separately.
232	
233	*A standing unit and classification definition* is fixed by the account types themselves. *A balancing
234	identity* renders an unexplained change a visible residual: closing stock equals opening stock plus
235	additions less reductions, with additions and reductions each split into managed and unmanaged, so that
236	the nature of a change is recorded rather than inferred (United Nations et al., 2024, paras 4.14–4.15,
237	p. 86). *A re-aggregable spatial basis* follows from basic spatial units being "a means of implementing
238	the ecosystem accounting approach rather than … elements in a nested hierarchy" (App. A1.1, para.
239	A1.3), with the reporting geography a compiler-delineated accounting area; provincial and district
240	figures are then two aggregations of one production run rather than two data requests. *Documented
241	vintage and revision* follows from the requirement that an account be consistent over time and across
242	classifications (para. 1.60, p. 18), and that previous estimates be revised where updated imagery or
243	methods permit a reassessment, with the size of the revision recoverable as a difference (para. 4.17,
244	p. 88).
245	
246	Two of the four are properties of a compiled account rather than of a well-run register. Section 5
247	concedes the other two.
248	
249	## 3.3 Corpus
250	
251	A citation-anchored corpus of 199 chunks was built over the index's own published documentation and is
252	released with this article, so that every quotation and figure resolves to a numbered record rather
253	than to a page range. Extraction was purposive. The build, dated 2026-08-14, comprises 106 chunks from
254	the Juknis (BAPPENAS 2025a), 49 from the IBEI Report 2024 in its December 2025 revision (BAPPENAS
255	2025b), 15 from Rinaldi et al. (2025) and 29 from the SIRUSA metadata (kegiatan 94936 and indikator
256	105371, both retrieved 2026-08-13), at the printed-page ranges listed in the deposit. Each record
257	carries its document and slice, the verbatim Indonesian text, an English gloss used nowhere as a
258	quotation source, the printed page with the PDF page in brackets, a maturity tag, and a reconstruction
259	flag, which 17 records carry. Chunks in conflict are recorded as conflicts rather than reconciled, and
260	the four-against-three pillar contradiction is held as one such record.
261	
262	Account-side evidence sits outside that corpus and was read directly: the three MMAF 2022 reports
263	including the Gili Matra pilot study, the BPS materials prepared for the SEEA workshops, Rahayu et al.
264	(2024), Kepmen KP 44/2023, and the GOAP country page (retrieved 2026-08-13). All source documents carry clean text layers and no optical character recognition was applied.
265	Retrieval and assembly ran from 11 to 16 August 2026. A join table reproduces all 44 register rows with
266	each row's *Data Pembentuk Indikator* constituent data items verbatim, transcribed from Juknis Tabel 1,
267	pp. 11–13 [pdf 21–23], totalling 62 items. That join table, not the running text of the handbook, is
268	the object the membership rule was applied to.
269	
270	Searching was bilingual and was run over the document set named above rather than over a statistical
271	catalogue. The Indonesian terms used were *indeks ekonomi biru*, *neraca laut*, *akun laut*, *neraca
272	ekosistem*, *unit spasial dasar laut*, *peta jalan ekonomi biru* and *Satu Data Indonesia*, with their
273	English equivalents and no date restriction. No dedicated search of the BPS statistical catalogue or of
274	the Satu Data Indonesia portal was undertaken, and by project constraint no Indonesian agency was
275	approached. Every absence claim in this article is therefore stated as unfound within the named set,
276	and is not a claim about the Indonesian statistical system. Within that set, no Indonesian statistical
277	register supplying provenance, spatial unit, vintage and revision policy for marine ecosystem extent and
278	condition products, separately from an account structure, was encountered `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. That
279	negative bears on the register objection weighed in Section 5.2.
280	
281	## 3.4 Unit of analysis and the membership rule
282	
283	The unit is the register row, of which there are 44. The object tested is the row's constituent data
284	items rather than its title, on the Indonesian wording. Each row was graded on one of three grounds.
285	A row is *matched* where a constituent data item names a quantity that a defined SEEA Ecosystem
286	Accounting output contains. It is *partial* where account output supplies a component of the quantity
287	but not the quantity itself. It is a *provenance question* where account output bears on the row
288	without supplying it. Tier A is the union of the three grades and has five members: indicators 19, 20
289	and 21 matched, 24 partial, and 27 a provenance question. The remaining 39 rows are graded none.
290	
291	The rule was applied item by item, so a row carrying several items takes the highest grade any one
292	attains, which makes the 62 items rather than the 44 rows the tested population. Two decisions were
293	fixed before scoring: that indicator titles are not evidence of what a row requires, titles having
294	produced the predecessor figures this analysis rejects; and that a definitional match carries no claim
295	about whether anything has been compiled. Grading was by the authors against those criteria, not blind,
296	with no second coder and no adjudication protocol. Every row's grade and ground is deposited as
297	Supplementary Table S1.
298	
299	Membership in Tier A is a definitional property. The separate operative test applied to the account
300	side is repeatability into a dated window rather than demonstrated capability, since the obligation is
301	annual and can receive only what a producer has already released at the required vintage. Maturity is
302	tagged on every claim that something exists, as (a) published official statistics, (b) experimental or
303	pilot compilation, (c) methodology under development, or (d) announced but not started. The tag
304	describes the datum rather than the document reporting it.
305	
306	## 3.5 Weight arithmetic and its one ambiguity
307	
308	Weights are taken from the Juknis as printed: pillar weights from Tabel 4, p. 94 [pdf 104]; sub-pillar
309	splits from Tabel 5, p. 94 [pdf 104]; variable weights from Tabel 6, pp. 95–96 [pdf 105–106]; and the
310	sub-pillar shares of Gambar 11, p. 93 [pdf 103], used as an independent integer cross-check. Shares of
311	the composite are products of those published percentages, carried to two decimal places from
312	one-decimal inputs and reported in prose to one decimal with 'about'. The technology sub-pillar's
313	weight is not printed and is recovered as the residual of the economy pillar, at 4.11%. All weight
314	arithmetic follows the three-pillar structure (Section 2.3).
315	
316	The handbook does not state whether the percentages in Tabel 4 to Tabel 6 are raw first-principal-component
317	loadings or normalised contributions. Every derived figure here inherits that ambiguity
318	`[CONTESTED DENOMINATOR]`. The test applied is to renormalise each sub-pillar's printed variable
319	weights to sum to 100 and recompute. In the resource-quality sub-pillar the nine printed weights
320	already sum to exactly 100.0, so 18.45%, 7.64%, 3.58% and 2.33% are unchanged to the precision
321	printed, with a residual half-unit rounding uncertainty moving 7.64% by under 0.1 percentage points. In
322	the economy pillar the five printed sub-pillar weights sum to 96.0, and renormalising over those five
323	alone would give about 27.2% for the four Tier B sub-pillars and about 5.37% for governance, against
324	the 26.1% and 5.15% adopted here. Gambar 11's printed integers of 26 and 5 do not support the
325	alternative, which is reported as an upper bound of roughly one percentage point rather than carried.
326	The test cannot be run on the pillar weights themselves, which already sum to 100.0 as printed.
327	Workings are deposited as Supplementary Data S2.
328	
329	## 3.6 Reporting conventions
330	
331	Indonesian decimal commas are preserved inside quotation and rendered as points in prose. Quotations
332	are from the Indonesian source text. Three inline flags mark evidential status: `[EVIDENCE GAP]` where
333	a required source was not found, `[ASSUMPTION]` where the argument depends on something unconfirmed,
334	and `[CONTESTED DENOMINATOR]` on the Tier A share of 7.64% and figures derived from it, recording that
335	the share measures what these account types bear on rather than what an account would supply.
336	Conflicting sources are reported as conflicts and are not averaged or adjudicated.

339	# 4. Results
340	
341	## 4.1 The compiled record
342	
343	Indonesian ocean accounting has produced one pilot compilation, in one marine protected area, in one
344	province, on one vintage. Every output carries maturity tag (b).
345	
346	The compiled record comprises the Gili Matra Marine Recreational Park pilot off north Lombok, together
347	with one peer-reviewed grid-optimisation study at Saleh Bay, Sumbawa (Rahayu et al., 2024). Both sit in
348	West Nusa Tenggara, so NTB is the single province carrying a documented published compilation located
349	by this study. A national Ocean Accounts Dashboard was launched in July 2025 and is described by GOAP
350	as publicly available and interactive on a harmonised spatial frame (GOAP country page, retrieved
351	2026-08-13). That is a programme self-statement about a platform, and it evidences no compilation
352	beyond the pilot record.
353	
354	The pilot's footprint belongs in its own units, because the smallness is part of the finding: 2,980.84
355	ha on a 25 m by 25 m marine basic spatial unit (MBSU) grid of 48,211 cells, opening in 2015 and
356	closing in 2021, those years chosen on data availability (MMAF, 2022, pp. 30, 39 [pdf 44, 53]). The two
357	figures do not reconcile. At 625 m² each, 48,211 cells give 3,013.19 ha against the 2,980.84 ha
358	printed, a shortfall of 32.35 ha or 1.1%. That would be consistent with cells clipped at the park
359	boundary, but is nowhere explained, and is recorded as observed rather than resolved `[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
360	Against a statutory obligation to score every province annually, this is an area smaller than many
361	single districts, compiled once.
362	
363	Maturity is the programme's own self-description rather than an external verdict. GOAP labels the three
364	2022 MMAF outputs 'preliminary consultation drafts', and BPS lists scaling up to the total Indonesian
365	marine area among its outstanding challenges (Tasriah et al., 2022, slide 9). There is no BPS ocean
366	account release, no entry in the national statistical work programme, no published revision policy, and
367	no published compilation cost per site or per unit. The ecosystem scope is confined to mangrove,
368	seagrass and coral reef.
369	
370	The three account types differ in completeness of the account form, not in the standing of the
371	compilation producing them. Extent is the only type with a verified opening, change and closing series:
372	the pilot publishes a complete asset account, with additions and reductions itemised and an
373	ecosystem-conversion matrix alongside (MMAF, 2022, tab. 5–6, pp. 52–53 [pdf 66–67]). Condition is
374	compiled in a limited form, as cover-based measurement at Tier 1 to Tier 2 read against documented
375	reference bands rather than as a normalised multi-indicator composite (pp. 31–33 [pdf 45–47]). Valuation
376	is the least developed and may not be described as an ecosystem services account in physical units.
377	What exists is benefit-transferred monetary valuation, rising from IDR 45.24 bn per year in 2015 to IDR
378	64.17 bn in 2021 (pp. 36, 60 [pdf 50, 74]). Benefit transfer is not the exchange-value basis the
379	standard requires, a limit BPS states itself (Tasriah et al., 2022, slide 9). No ecosystem services
380	account in physical units has been compiled in Indonesia. Supplementary S5 reproduces all three.
381	
382	Extent is also where two official series diverge, and the divergence is this article's strongest
383	internal counter-instance. A BPS presentation reports the same pilot, ecosystems and years at
384	substantially lower areas (Tasriah et al., 2022, slide 3), although both series trace to one layer, the
385	Geospatial Information Agency's 2020 mapping validated by 2021 field survey. Neither document states
386	its relationship to the other and no reconciliation is published `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. The divergence is
387	not a constant offset: the BPS-to-MMAF ratio moves across ecosystems and across the accounting window
388	(S5.2). The two series therefore disagree on the change and not merely on the level, MMAF recording a
389	coral loss of 12.00 ha against a BPS loss of 18.26 ha. Change is the quantity a balancing identity
390	exists to report, and Section 5.2 interrogates the consequence.
391	
392	## 4.2 Indicators the existing accounts can serve
393	
394	Five of the register's 44 rows are the whole of what an ocean account touches. Indicators 19, 20 and 21
395	name quantities an account's output structure already contains; indicators 24 and 27 sit adjacent to
396	account output and are disposed of rather than claimed (Table 2). Figure 2 gives the assignment for all
397	44 rows with the constituent data items in full.
398	
399	On the compilation axis the concession is complete. None of the five indicator values has been
400	published by any Indonesian account, for any province or any site `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Every capability
401	claim here is a claim about definitional correspondence and a demonstrated input layer, never about an
402	existing output. All five rows sit in the environment pillar under both pillar structures, so the
403	unresolved conflict does not reach this arithmetic.
404	
405	The three condition indicators share one grammatical form. Indicator 19 asks for the *Persentase
406	kawasan terumbu karang dengan tutupan karang hidup antara 50-100% per provinsi*, indicator 20 for the
407	seagrass equivalent, and indicator 21 for *kerapatan tutupan* in mangrove (Juknis, Tabel 1, p. 12 [pdf
408	22]). Each names a percentage of area falling within a stated cover or density band, per province. That
409	is a tabulation of extent by class, not a normalised index against a reference condition. Earlier
410	assessments judged that Indonesia lacks the condition account these indicators require. They do not
411	require one, so the judgement answers a question the index does not pose.
412	
413	What the pilot sets beside that specification is the input layer, not the tabulation. The MBSU extent
414	surface exists, and cover is measured at survey stations: live coral cover of 18.11% in 2016 and live
415	hard coral cover of 16.99% in 2021, which the report does not state to be the same measure and which
416	are therefore not read here as a series, and seagrass cover averaging 32.28% with a station maximum of
417	82.01% (MMAF, 2022, pp. 40, 45 [pdf 54, 59]). The 2021 coral figure is unreconciled in the source,
418	where the narrative reports 16.99% whilst the report's own tab. 3 on the same page prints 18.36%. The
419	cross-tabulation of extent by cover class is not published. The step from the input layer to indicator
420	19 is one tabulation that has not been performed.
421	
422	The match frays at four edges. Two are definitional. The 50% threshold is the index's own and matches
423	no Indonesian condition class boundary in use, the pilot classifying reef condition on the bands of
424	Giyanto et al. (2017), where moderate runs from 19% to below 35%. Reclassifying a continuous per-cell
425	cover surface at 50% would be trivial, but only where condition is mapped wall to wall rather than
426	surveyed at stations, and no obtained document evidences a wall-to-wall condition surface
427	`[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Where national data exist only as classes pre-binned on the Giyanto boundaries, the
428	50% cut cannot be recovered, and the coral row becomes a definition gap rather than a coverage gap
429	`[ASSUMPTION]`. The mangrove unit differs outright: indicator 21 asks for canopy density as a
430	percentage, whilst the pilot reports m² per ha and individuals per ha, with no documented conversion
431	(p. 47 [pdf 61]) `[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
432	
433	The other two edges are unfixed choices. Neither the minimum mapping unit nor the denominator is fixed
434	by the register or by the SIRUSA definitions `[ASSUMPTION]`. And the extent estimate is itself a
435	function of the grid rule: Rahayu et al. (2024) report mangrove error of 44.06% under a 25 m hierarchy
436	scheme against 1.59% under 25 m dominance, so until a grid rule is adopted the account's figure is a
437	family of figures. A vintage gap sits alongside these, the only verified series closing in 2021 against
438	the index's T-1 requirement.
439	
440	Indicator 24 asks for the *Total luas kawasan mangrove, rawa, dan lahan gambut yang direstorasi per
441	provinsi*, glossed in SIRUSA as degraded area subsequently replanted. That is an activity input measured
442	in hectares planted. The pilot's asset account carries a managed-expansion line that is unpopulated at
443	Gili Matra, where every addition is natural expansion and the only managed entry is 11.19 ha of managed
444	mangrove regression. The correspondence is structural rather than demonstrated: an account of this form
445	records realised managed change, whilst the indicator counts hectares planted. A scope mismatch
446	compounds it, the indicator's three land covers being mangrove, swamp and peatland against the marine
447	account's mangrove, seagrass and coral reef.
448	
449	Indicator 27 asks for the *Total luas kawasan konservasi perairan yang ditetapkan oleh KKP, KLHK, dan
450	Pemerintah Daerah per provinsi*, and is in the tier for a different reason. At 22.0% it is the largest
451	single variable in the resource-quality sub-pillar, about 4.06% of the composite, and it needs no
452	account to be produced. Designated area is an administrative datum already held by its custodians. The
453	account's near-term contribution here is reconciliation of mapped extent to the designated polygon, not
454	supply.
455	
456	The weight chain is shown openly as a multiplication. The environment pillar carries 36.9% of the
457	composite and the resource-quality sub-pillar 50.0% of the pillar (Juknis, Tabel 4 and Tabel 5, p. 94
458	[pdf 104]), giving 18.45%, of which the five Tier A variables sum to about 7.64%
459	`[CONTESTED DENOMINATOR]`. Figure 3 places that band inside the published weight structure. The tag
460	names one specific objection: 7.64% is a defensible measure of the share these account types bear on,
461	and an indefensible measure of the share an account would supply, because most of it sits at rows the
462	mapping has itself disqualified. The decomposition is therefore reported in three parts: about 2.33% at
463	the three matched condition rows, about 1.25% at indicator 24 as a partial, and about 4.06% at
464	indicator 27, which the account does not supply. **The share at which an account is the prospective
465	supply route is about 2.33%**, rising to about 3.58% only if indicator 24's structural correspondence
466	is credited in full, which the activity-against-outcome mismatch and the scope gap indicate it should
467	not be. The lower figure is operative and the upper an outer bound.
468	
469	The register is silent on a point worth recording: no producing agency is stated for any of the 44 rows
470	`[EVIDENCE GAP]`.

487	## 4.3 Extension routes, and what separates them
488	
489	What separates the account routes into and out of reach is content, not compilation maturity. The
490	routes with a pilot-scale Indonesian compilation bear on 12.79% of the composite. The one route with
491	no Indonesian compilation at any maturity, the classification-dependent social share, bears on 13.70%.
492	That is a difference of roughly 1.1 times, and it is not the finding. The finding is that no compiled
493	account produces the constituent data item the register asks for at any of these rows, and that the
494	outputs the pilot does produce — monetary supply and use, zone areas, protection expenditure — carry no
495	weight in the index at all (Figure 4). The arithmetic below follows the three-pillar locus declared at
496	Section 2.3, so every tourism, technology and governance figure carries that locus with it.
497	
498	**Tier B adds no quantity the register lacks.** Four economy sub-pillars carry about 26.1% of the
499	composite, which Gambar 11's integers confirm at 5 + 8 + 6 + 7 = 26 (Juknis, Tabel 5 and Gambar 11,
500	pp. 93–94 [pdf 103–104]). Every constituent data item in them is an administrative quantity KKP and BPS
501	routinely compile: production and export tonnages, passenger and vessel counts, site counts. No route
502	in Table 3 supplies a quantity of a kind the register does not already specify. What an account adds is
503	an industry boundary consistent with international and Indonesian industrial classification, under
504	which such quantities become comparable across provinces and cycles (IDEEA Group, 2021; Fenichel et
505	al., 2020). Whether recompiling on that boundary would change the values cannot be determined here, and
506	under weights re-estimated by principal components it cannot be assumed not to.
507	
508	One correction inverts the intuitive fisheries mapping. Sub-pillar A is 83.0% aquaculture by published
509	weight against 6.3% for capture-fisheries volume (Tabel 6, p. 95 [pdf 105]), so reading production
510	against a stock assessment bears on about 0.31% of the composite whilst a consistent aquaculture
511	boundary bears on about 4.11% (S4.2). The intellectually interesting route is the numerically negligible
512	one.
513	
514	**Tier C is what the register has no term for.** None of the 44 constituent data items names an
515	ecosystem service flow, an asset value or a depletion term. The sharpest instance is an identity rather
516	than an aspiration: indicators 2 to 5 are unsigned production volumes, so a province raises its score
517	by producing more, and no term records whether the stock producing it is being drawn down. The missing
518	term is depletion (United Nations et al., 2024, para. A1.25), and the obstacle is that sustainable
519	yield by province and stock is not published, which makes stock assessment a prerequisite rather than
520	an alternative. Ecosystem services in physical units are specified in full (ch. 7) and compiled nowhere
521	in Indonesia; the nearest candidate is coastal protection from mangrove and reef, measurable against
522	the layers the pilot already produces. Combined presentations (paras 14.56–14.57) are the one row
523	available immediately, a presentation choice rather than a data gap.
524	
525	**The social pillar carries the largest account-relevant share, and nothing above methodology under
526	development has been compiled anywhere.** Seven of the thirteen social indicators require a
527	reproducible definition of the population working in the fisheries sector as a survey domain. Five are
528	ratios of a fisheries-sector population to a provincial total: indicator 32 divides *Jumlah penduduk
529	bekerja di sektor perikanan yang menerima PKH per provinsi* by the provincial total receiving that
530	transfer, and indicators 37, 42, 43 and 44 repeat the construction for health insurance,
531	upper-secondary schooling, certification and the Program Indonesia Pintar (Juknis, Tabel 1, p. 13 [pdf
532	23]). Two more presuppose the same identification: indicator 34 is a headcount of *nelayan dan
533	pembudidaya ikan*, and indicator 33 counts *pekerja perempuan di sektor perikanan*, which requires that
534	population sex-disaggregated. None is computable without the domain joined to SUSENAS, SAKERNAS and
535	poverty statistics. That is a classification deliverable, not a survey deliverable.
536	
537	The share is about 13.7% of the composite, decomposed row by row in Supplementary S4 so that a reader
538	may reject a row rather than the total. Coastal poverty at about 0.33% is the one row where the
539	handbook disagrees with itself, Tabel 1 building indicator 38 from a spatial domain and Tabel 6 from a
540	sectoral one (p. 96 [pdf 106]); the figure is held outside the 13.7% on the Tabel 1 wording, and the
541	Tabel 6 wording would take the total to about 14.0%. The 13.7% is the classification-standard claim,
542	whilst the pillar's published 30.5% is the wider claim that an ocean social account's structure
543	corresponds to the production of the whole pillar. The two are not interchangeable. Which indicators
544	require an ocean-sector domain is this study's reading of the verbatim constituent data items and is
545	stated in no source `[ASSUMPTION]`, and no Indonesian ocean-sector employment classification, ISIC or
546	KBLI concordance, or ocean-economy satellite boundary has been located `[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
547	
548	The maturity qualification belongs in the same breath as the share. Ocean employment and human capital
549	appear in the specified sequence of national ocean accounts as a component to be built (IDEEA Group,
550	2021), and the accounting standard specifies no such account, its subject being ecosystems rather than
551	an ocean workforce. The step that matters most, the join of an industry concordance to household survey
552	microdata, is specified nowhere this study located.
553	
554	**Governance is measured by one count, and Indonesia has compiled a governance account.** Tabel 6 gives
555	the governance sub-pillar exactly one variable, *Jumlah pelabuhan perikanan dengan Tempat Pelelangan
556	Ikan (TPI) (unit)*, at 100.0% (p. 95 [pdf 105]). At 15.8% of the economy pillar the sub-pillar is about
557	5.15% of the composite, matching Gambar 11's F at 5%. The Gili Matra pilot does compile a governance
558	account, at tables 18–19 (p. 79), which places that share at maturity (b) rather than (c). What the
559	pilot's governance account produces — zone areas and protection expenditure — appears nowhere in the
560	register, so the compilation and the indicator do not meet. The alternatives to a single count of landing sites — an institutional inventory, an account of legal
561	and policy instruments, a spatial classification of designated marine uses, and ocean expenditure
562	classified consistently — are set out here as this study's own reading of what such an account would
563	contain `[ASSUMPTION]`, the institutional inventory being the realistic provincial entry point.
564	Technology has the identical one-variable shape, vessels over 30 GT at 100.0% of a sub-pillar carrying
565	about 1.34% of the composite. Both figures
566	are structure-dependent: under Tabel 1's four-pillar structure these variables sit in the *Enabler*

615	## 4.4 Conditions precedent for supply
616	
617	A definitional match is not a supply arrangement. Five conditions must hold before pilot account output
618	could serve the IBEI as a repeating input, none is satisfied in the obtained record, and they are not
619	equally binding (Table 4). Three are one-off acts of codification that a single decision by MMAF, BPS or
620	BAPPENAS could discharge. Two are discharged only by sustaining a production cycle, and those two
621	decide whether the mapping in Section 4.2 is a recommendation or a wish.
622	
623	The register would be refuted by producing an adopted national marine spatial standard, a BPS
624	statistical work-programme entry for a marine ecosystem account, or an extent compilation covering more
625	than the pilot sites and closing after 2021.
626	
627	The two recurring conditions are the binding ones. SIRUSA specifies reference period T-1 for all five
628	mapped variables. The single verified compilation closes at 2021 and was published the following year,
629	a lag T-1 can accommodate; what is absent is the second vintage, and no repeat compilation of the pilot
630	sites was found `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Figure 5 sets the required cycle against the vintages the accounts
631	have produced: the gap between the 2021 closing year and the reference year the 2025 cycle requires is
632	three reference years. The second is a revision policy, for which the standard supplies the substance
633	(paras 4.17–4.18, p. 88) and Indonesia supplies no instrument `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. An account silently
634	improved between vintages would move a province's index value with no ecosystem change behind it, and
635	nothing in the published record would flag it.
636	
637	One condition follows from the standard rather than from Indonesian practice. Condition is not additive
638	across areas, its variable account being an area-weighted arithmetic mean over the assets of an
639	ecosystem type (para. 5.54, p. 105), so a provincial ratio cannot be recovered by averaging district
640	values and a supplier would need to publish the denominator alongside the percentage.
641	
642	The institutional condition is unmet against an instrument whose own term has run. Kepmen KP 44/2023
643	constituted the Tim Penyusun Neraca Sumber Daya Laut in February 2023 and set its term to expire on
644	31 December 2023, and no renewal instrument was located `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. The July 2025 dashboard launch
645	indicates continued activity under some instrument not obtained, so the defensible statement is that no
646	renewal was located rather than that the mandate lapsed. Two sectoral products feeding one another does
647	not by itself constitute an accredited statistical input. The gap runs in both directions: no microdata
648	are released, so even a compliant supplier could not verify that its value survived into the published
649	provincial score.
650	
651	One design recommendation follows. Where a province has no account, an account-derived indicator would
652	itself be predicted from socio-economic proxies under the framework the Report publishes (pp. 64–66
653	[pdf 76–78]), so an environmental value would become partly a function of economic and social
654	covariates. The framework's first scenario trains on the previous cycle's historical data, which a newly
655	introduced indicator does not have. **Insertion should be phased behind coverage: compile first, insert
656	second.** That is a sequencing recommendation, not a change to the index's method. Supplementary S3
657	carries the derivation for each condition.

675	# 5. Discussion
676	
677	## 5.1 What compilation changes, and what it does not
678	
679	What an account offers this index at the rows it touches is a production process, not a stock of
680	substitutable values. That is a narrower claim than an efficiency claim, and the evidence assembled
681	here would not support the wider one.
682	
683	The register decomposes into 62 constituent data items across its 44 rows. Three of them — indicators
684	19, 20 and 21 — are cross-classifications of one extent-and-condition compilation, so the consolidation
685	is three items into one production run, out of 62. The argument is not that the count is large. It is
686	that those three are among the items the index presently has no way to show it measured rather than
687	predicted.
688	
689	A cover-class percentage compiled from a gridded extent surface and a classified cover layer carries its
690	own spatial unit, its own opening and closing stocks, and a provenance chain back to the imagery and
691	field survey behind it. It is reproducible in the next cycle by repeating the compilation. A value taken
692	from a secondary publication, or predicted where no publication exists, carries none of those
693	properties, and the published record does not distinguish the two (Section 2.2).
694	
695	That the distinction is worth publishing is established outside Indonesia. The Ocean Health Index, a
696	composite of comparable construction in the same domain (Halpern et al., 2012), reports that 18.5% of
697	its global score rests on gapfilled rather than observed data, ranging from 1% to 43% across its ten
698	goals and exceeding 60% for ten regions that are predominantly small island territories. Its authors
699	recommend that gapfilling be tracked from the outset and presented in the same framework as the scores
700	themselves (Frazier et al., 2016). The IBEI publishes an imputation framework and no per-indicator
701	record, so the comparison identifies a reporting reform rather than a data programme — and one the index
702	could adopt without any account being compiled.
703	
704	Everything downstream of the input is untouched. Account supply changes the value entering a slot, not
705	the slot, its weight or the aggregation rule. The three-stage principal component procedure, the
706	loading-factor weights, the pillar shares and every limitation the index publishes about itself stand
707	exactly as they are.
708	
709	## 5.2 The counter-instance, and what survives it
710	
711	The strongest evidence against this article's own claim is in its own account-side record. The one
712	compiled Indonesian account has produced two closing stocks and two deltas for the same site, the same
713	three ecosystems and the same two years, disagreeing not by a constant offset but by a ratio that moves
714	across ecosystems and across the accounting window (Section 4.1). The pilot's own reporting compounds
715	it, the narrative giving 16.99% live hard coral cover for 2021 against 18.36% in the report's table on
716	the same page. Change is the quantity a balancing identity exists to report, and provenance is what
717	this article claims accounts buy.
718	
719	Read one way, this refutes the provenance claim outright. Read against what the two documents actually
720	are, it qualifies it in a specific place. The two series are parallel releases of the same underlying
721	mapping layer, published in a presentation and a report, and neither is an account of the other.
722	Publishing account-shaped tables is not the same as running an account. The property claimed here is not
723	that compilation prevents divergence, which nothing in the standard promises. It is that a balancing
724	identity gives divergence somewhere to appear: opening stock, additions, reductions and closing stock
725	are four entries that must reconcile, and that a second compilation must restate rather than replace.
726	The Gili Matra case is evidence for that reading in negative form. Precisely because neither document
727	was compiled under such an identity, and because no revision rule required either to restate the other,
728	a 6.26 ha disagreement about coral change could be published twice and reconciled nowhere. That is an
729	argument from the account form rather than a demonstration, since no Indonesian compilation has yet been
730	revised under such a rule `[ASSUMPTION]`.
731	
732	A second objection bears on the same argument. A well-documented statistical register could supply
733	several of the four properties without any account being compiled, and on two of the four it holds
734	outright. A standing unit and classification definition is an act of codification, requiring a published
735	definition of the mapping unit, the ecosystem classes and the landward and seaward limits; nothing about
736	compiling an account is needed to write one down. Documented vintage and provenance is likewise a
737	metadata requirement any well-run register discharges. The spatial property is intermediate: a register
738	can mandate a grid and a minimum mapping unit but cannot itself produce the re-aggregable cell-level
739	tabulation the mandate presupposes. What does not reduce to codification is the residual — the balancing
740	identity, and the revision rule that alters the opening and closing entries themselves. Both are
741	properties of double-entry over a stock. The residual is thin, and saying so is more useful than
742	defending a wider claim. A reader who concludes that Indonesia should codify definitions before
743	compiling anything has read the evidence correctly, and that ordering is what Section 4.4 recommends.
744	
745	## 5.3 What account supply does not fix
746	
747	The index's custodian has printed its own methodological limits, and account supply reaches none of
748	them. Four are conceded in the Report's own methods statement (Section 3.1.1, p. 34 [pdf 46]): an
749	assumption of linear and monotonic relationships, sensitivity to outliers, weights set on 2024 data that
750	*mungkin saja menjadi tidak relevan untuk tahun-tahun mendatang*, and a single weight structure applied
751	across a heterogeneous archipelago. Every one is a property of the estimator, whilst an account changes
752	the input. The first is worth dwelling on. The threshold relationship BAPPENAS describes — the benefit
753	of fishing intensity holding below a threshold whilst damage rises exponentially above it — is exactly
754	what an ecosystem account measures well, and better inputs cannot reach a relationship the estimator
755	represents linearly by design.
756	
757	Aggregation discards what the account was built to show. The constituent data items for indicators 19,
758	20 and 21 each end *per provinsi*, so an account compiled on a fine spatial grid loses its
759	within-province distribution at the moment it becomes an indicator. The account can tell a provincial
760	planner which reef complex is driving a change; the index can report only that a percentage moved. That
761	is a consequence of publishing the indicator without the grid `[ASSUMPTION]`, so the remedy is an
762	architecture in which both are released. The provincial boundary is administrative and not ecological,
763	and the standard leaves the accounting area to the compiler (para. 2.12), so a reef complex straddling
764	that line is divided by legal artefact. Accounts make the division explicit and auditable; they do not
765	make it ecologically meaningful.
766	
767	Net extent change cannot simply be inserted. The index states its own weighting rule: an indicator with
768	greater variability and strong correlation to others in its domain *akan secara alami mendapatkan bobot
769	yang lebih tinggi* (p. 29 [pdf 41]). A signed annual difference of a large, slowly changing stock is
770	low-variance by construction and can change sign between cycles, so it would attract a small and
771	unstable weight, and might meet the rule printed in the sub-pillar loop as *Reversi nilai indikator
772	(atau drop indikator) jika ditemukan relasi negatif* (p. 30 [pdf 42]). Whether a sign-changing series
773	would trigger it is not specified and is read here as an inference. The mechanism is not peculiar to
774	this index: under principal components, weighting corrects for overlapping information rather than
775	measuring importance, and where indicators are not correlated weights cannot be estimated by the method
776	at all (Nardo et al., 2008, §6.1). Net extent change belongs beside the composite as a reported
777	companion series, not inside it as a forty-fifth indicator.
778	
779	Half the environment pillar measures something no ocean account should be built to measure. The
780	renewable-energy sub-pillar carries 50.0% of the pillar and contains no marine variable, being solar
781	and hydropower throughout (S4.3). No existing account can supply what it asks for, and building one to
782	do so would be an instrument error. The coverage claim stops at that boundary, and stopping is what
783	makes it credible.
784	
785	## 5.4 Loose coupling, and three readings that would lead elsewhere
786	
787	Where substitution is not warranted, and on the published maturity evidence it is not, the arrangement
788	that delivers the gain is loose coupling. It has three requirements: shared indicator definitions at the
789	cover-class thresholds the Juknis already prints, with the unit and denominator rules the register
790	leaves open settled with its custodian; a shared spatial unit that aggregates to the province; and
791	parallel publication of the account alongside the index. It is defined as much by what it does not
792	require — no institutional transfer, no change to the index's data pipeline, and no dependency of a
793	statutory index on an experimental compilation. Parallel publication is a combined presentation (paras
794	14.56–14.57) and is the nearer term of the two, whilst sub-national compilation follows from the
795	accounting area being definable at a subnational administrative boundary (para. 2.12) and has been
796	demonstrated at that scale elsewhere (Gacutan et al., 2022). Loose coupling has the property that a
797	compilation cycle missing the window fails without the index failing with it.
798	
799	The institutional division holds at the point of recommendation, where it is most at risk. BAPPENAS is
800	*pengampu* of the calculation whilst the constituent data are provincial (Juknis, p. 68 [pdf 78]), so
801	nothing here asks either institution to take on the other's mandate. The arrangement would have to be
802	settled by whichever body now holds the coordinating function, the team constituted under Kepmen KP
803	44/2023 having had its term expire with no renewal instrument located.
804	
805	Three readings of the same record would lead elsewhere. Rinaldi et al. (2025) construct an alternative
806	index on a human development index template, treating the problem as one of index design rather than of
807	the production system beneath it; on that reading the effort recommended here is misdirected. Wuwung et
808	al. (2024) locate the constraint on Indonesian ocean policy in institutional fragmentation across
809	mandates, which would make an inter-agency supply arrangement the hardest rather than the cheapest of
810	the conditions precedent. Ravallion (2012) defines a mashup index as a composite for which theory and
811	practice provide little guidance on design, leaving the producer free to set an unusually large number
812	of moving parts, and doubts the value added of such indices relative to monitoring the components
813	separately. The answer this record supports is statutory rather than methodological: the IBEI is scored
814	for every province annually under Perpres 12/2025, so the dashboard alternative is not in BAPPENAS's
815	gift, and what supplies the composite is a live question whether or not an aggregate is the instrument a
816	statistician would have chosen. No source located by this study argues that accounts cannot serve as
817	statistical infrastructure for a composite index.
818	
819	## 5.5 Limitations
820	
821	Every share reported here is a nominal share of printed weight, not a measure of effective influence.
822	Nardo et al. (2008, §6.1) hold that under principal components, weighting is not a measure of the
823	theoretical importance of the associated indicator. Paruolo et al. (2013) measure what each variable
824	actually contributes to five composites, the human development index among them, and find that because
825	socio-economic variables are heteroscedastic and correlated, relative nominal weights hardly ever match
826	relative effective importance. The IBEI is a composite over correlated provincial socio-economic
827	variables with weights taken from first-component loadings, which is the configuration in which that
828	divergence was found. Becker et al. (2017) supply the test that would settle it, a correlation-ratio
829	measure of each variable's importance with confidence intervals. It cannot be run here, because it
830	requires the provincial variable matrix beneath the index, which is unpublished `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. What
831	survives the objection is the production-process case, which never rested on the shares.
832	
833	Six further limitations bear on specific figures. Whether Tabel 4 to Tabel 6's percentages are raw
834	loadings or normalised contributions is not stated anywhere, and every derived figure inherits it
835	`[CONTESTED DENOMINATOR]`; the renormalisation test bounds the difference at under a percentage point
836	wherever it can be run (Section 3.5). The printed aggregation formulae are illegible in the PDF text
837	layer at Juknis pp. 64–65 and Report pp. 31–32, so what is reconstructed is the notation, at
838	medium-high confidence, rather than the method. The per-indicator imputation record is unpublished,
839	which is the largest single unknown, because it determines how much of any published provincial value is
840	measurement and how much is prediction. The mapping is an assessment against stated criteria rather than
841	an independently verifiable quantity, which is why every row's score and exclusion ground is deposited.
842	Compilation cost is absent from the public record at every site. And this study engaged no Indonesian
843	agency, by project constraint, so the absence claims above are conditions to live with rather than
844	future work.
845	
846	
847	# 6. Conclusion
848	
849	Ocean accounts are worth building for this index because of how the handful of indicators they touch
850	would be produced, and not because of how many indicators they could fill. The share an account would
851	itself supply is about 2.33% of the composite on the published weights, and it rests in part on a
852	denominator the source documents do not settle. What separates the account routes into and out of reach
853	is content rather than compilation maturity: the routes compiled at pilot scale and the route compiled
854	nowhere bear on comparable shares of the index, 12.79% against 13.70%, yet no compiled account produces
855	the constituent data item the register asks for at any of those rows.
856	
857	The binding constraint on acting on this is not methodological. No compilation cost appears anywhere in
858	the record obtained for this study — no staff-day figure, no budget line, no imagery cost and no
859	field-survey cost, at any site `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. A province-by-province annual obligation is a scope
860	decision before it is a method decision. This study declines to estimate a cost, since an
861	order-of-magnitude range would be read as a figure.
862	
863	Two follow-on studies suggest themselves. The claim that indicators 19, 20 and 21 are definitionally
864	matched to account output is empirically testable today at exactly one site. Re-tabulating the Gili
865	Matra compilation to the index's own 50% to 100% cut would settle whether an account's extent and cover
866	layers yield the register's class-share form at all, and would generate the first real compilation-cost
867	observation. Its limits are set by the record itself: the coral and seagrass cover behind that
868	compilation was surveyed at stations rather than mapped per cell, the mangrove row is blocked on an
869	undocumented unit conversion, and a footprint of 2,980.84 ha supports no comparison against a published
870	provincial value. Extending the tabulation to NTB as a whole would test provincial resolution, and
871	requires the province-wide compilation that does not exist. The second output is practitioner-facing
872	rather than research: no published guidance sets out how a sub-national marine ecosystem account should
873	be compiled to serve a provincial indicator in an Indonesian setting. Both assume continued external
874	technical assistance to the Indonesian programme, which is an assumption rather than an established
875	condition `[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
876	
877	What would falsify the argument is worth stating, because a paper of this shape is otherwise
878	structurally unfalsifiable. If a single-province compilation reproduces the published provincial value
879	no better than the current secondary-publication route, or does so at a cost per province that an annual
880	national cycle cannot bear, then the production-process case fails on its own terms.
881	
882	Within the statutory boundary held throughout, what this study describes is a capacity gap under a
883	statutory obligation, in a country that has demonstrated, at one experimental pilot site, one instance
884	of the production process the obligation would require at scale.

1025	# Supplementary S3. Conditions precedent — derivations
1026	
1027	Long form of Table 4. Each condition states what the index requires, what the accounting standard
1028	supplies, and what this study observed. Absence claims are unfound in the searched set stated at
1029	Section 3.3, not claims of non-existence.
1030	
1031	## S3.1 A national spatial standard
1032	
1033	Provincial account output is not comparable across provinces or across vintages until one minimum
1034	mapping unit and one basic spatial unit are adopted nationally. This is an act of codification,
1035	discharged once, requiring no new compilation.
1036	
1037	The standard requires an account to be consistent over time and with respect to concepts and
1038	classifications (United Nations et al., 2024, para. 1.60), and requires previous estimates to be
1039	revised for continuity of the time series wherever updated data, methods or classifications permit a
1040	reassessment of area (para. 4.17). Grid-based spatial units are the mechanism by which an account
1041	aggregates to any administrative boundary, conditional on the grid existing and being national.
1042	
1043	No adopted national marine grid or minimum mapping unit was found in any obtained Indonesian document
1044	`[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Rahayu et al. (2024), the only peer-reviewed Indonesian account source, state that a
1045	standardised grid has yet to be established, and the pilot literature records different resolutions at
1046	different sites — the case the rule exists to prevent. Grid choice changes the extent tabulation
1047	itself: mangrove error of 44.06% under a 25 m hierarchy scheme against 1.59% under 25 m dominance. Until
1048	a grid rule is adopted, the account's figure is a family of figures, and the index would be importing a
1049	methodological choice.
1050	
1051	## S3.2 A codified clipping rule
1052	
1053	Provincial marine authority extends to 12 nautical miles under Law 23/2014, cited here at second hand
1054	and not read at source `[EVIDENCE GAP]`, so an account reaching further must be clipped before it can
1055	yield a provincial number.
1056	
1057	The standard leaves the accounting area to the compiler (para. 2.12) and supplies no sub-national
1058	allocation rule for an asset spanning a boundary. The convention this study takes as necessary —
1059	allocation by physical extent within each side, with condition compiled per spatial unit rather than
1060	inherited whole — is a specification rather than a citation `[ASSUMPTION]`. Neither it nor the
1061	statutory line is codified in any account obtained.
1062	
1063	One consequence follows from the standard directly. Condition is not additive across areas, its
1064	variable account being an area-weighted arithmetic mean over the assets of an ecosystem type (para.
1065	5.54, p. 105). Because indicators 19, 20 and 21 ask for a ratio, a provincial value cannot be recovered
1066	by averaging district values, and a supplier would need to publish the denominator alongside the
1067	percentage for a provincial value to be recoverable.
1068	
1069	Clean nesting of cell boundaries across administrative, fisheries-management and marine-spatial-planning
1070	frames is asserted rather than evidenced `[ASSUMPTION]`, and forms part of this condition rather than a
1071	property that may be assumed.
1072	
1073	## S3.3 A repeated compilation inside the reporting window
1074	
1075	SIRUSA specifies reference period T-1 for all five mapped variables (19, 20, 21, 24, 27). The single
1076	verified Indonesian compilation closes at 2021 and was published the following year, a lag T-1 can
1077	accommodate. What is absent is the second vintage. One compilation, however timely, cannot meet a T-1
1078	requirement in any subsequent cycle, and no repeat compilation of the pilot sites was found
1079	`[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
1080	
1081	This condition is recurring rather than one-off. It is discharged by running the cycle again, not by a
1082	decision, and it survives the index's own inconsistent rendering of its reference periods
1083	`[ASSUMPTION]`. Sustaining a cycle is a resourcing question, and no compilation cost was located in the
1084	public record, so no condition in this register can be priced.
1085	
1086	## S3.4 A revision policy
1087	
1088	The standard already supplies the substance: revision of previous estimates for continuity of the
1089	series, recording no distinct revision entry but altering the opening, closing, addition and reduction
1090	entries themselves, with the size of a revision shown as the difference between the historical and the
1091	revised account for the same period (paras 4.17–4.18, p. 88).
1092	
1093	What is unwritten is the Indonesian instrument that would designate a baseline year and disclose where
1094	the latest extent data refer to an earlier period. The index's own principle of controlled adaptability
1095	presupposes that discipline on the supply side. No revision policy was found for any Indonesian ocean
1096	account output in the searched set `[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
1097	
1098	The consequence is specific. An account silently improved between vintages would move a province's
1099	index value with no ecosystem change behind it, and nothing in the published record would flag it. The
1100	instrument requires no new compilation: it is a statement of when a series is restated and how a
1101	provisional estimate is marked.
1102	
1103	## S3.5 A statistical work-programme entry
1104	
1105	Account output cannot become a durable input to an official index whilst it remains an unregistered
1106	ministry product feeding an activity itself registered only as sectoral statistics. The IBEI is
1107	recorded in SIRUSA as *Kompilasi Produk Administrasi* under *Statistik Sektoral* with an empty
1108	*Identitas Rekomendasi* (kegiatan 94936), whilst the Juknis names BAPPENAS as *pengampu* of the
1109	calculation (p. 68 [pdf 78]).
1110	
1111	Indonesian ocean account output is an MMAF product constituted under Kepmen KP 44/2023, and no BPS
1112	statistical work-programme entry appears in any document obtained, no dedicated search of the BPS
1113	statistical catalogue having been undertaken `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. That Kepmen set the team's expiry at
1114	31 December 2023, and no renewal instrument was located, so a supply arrangement would be settled by
1115	whichever body now holds the coordinating function.
1116	
1117	The condition is a documented supply arrangement between MMAF as producer, BPS as statistical authority
1118	and BAPPENAS as compiler. Its minimum content is stated here as this study's own specification rather
1119	than taken from any standard: scope, frequency, quality standards, confidentiality, format and dispute
1120	resolution `[ASSUMPTION]`. Written that way it is a short document rather than a programme. That
1121	accreditation would be the route is inferred from the index's own registration rather than stated
