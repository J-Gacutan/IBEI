1	# Ocean accounts as compilation infrastructure for a blue economy index
2	
3	## Abstract
4	
5	Indonesia is statutorily obliged to score every province annually on a 44-indicator blue economy
6	composite that, on its own registration, conducts no primary collection and compiles entirely from
7	published sectoral statistics. This study assesses Indonesia's ocean accounts as compilation
8	infrastructure for that index rather than as an alternative indicator source. Two published
9	specifications are read against each other: the index register as printed in its 2025 technical
10	handbook, and the account structures of the SEEA Ecosystem Accounting standard. Each of the register's
11	44 rows is scored against the verbatim constituent data items that define it, with every claim
12	maturity-tagged. Reading data items rather than indicator names corrects prior assessments. Three
13	condition indicators ask for the share of provincial area within a stated cover band, a tabulation of
14	extent by class rather than the index against reference condition earlier work found missing. Five rows
15	are the whole of what an account touches, carrying 7.64% of the composite's published weight, of which
16	about 2.33% is the share an account would itself supply. What separates the account routes into and out
17	of reach is content rather than compilation maturity: routes compiled at pilot scale bear on 12.79% and
18	the one route compiled nowhere on 13.70%, yet no compiled account produces the data item the register
19	asks for at any of them. Every share measures what these account types bear on rather than what an
20	account would supply, a distinction the article keeps open at each use. What accounting offers such an index is a production process — a standing
21	classification, a balancing identity, a re-aggregable spatial basis and a documented revision rule —
22	rather than a stock of substitutable values. What an accounting
23	system can carry for a statutory composite is now a general question.
24	
25	
26	# 1. Introduction
27	
28	Composite indices are a standard instrument of ocean governance. They convert heterogeneous marine
29	statistics into a single number that ministries can target, budget against and report. Their
30	credibility rests less on indicator design than on the production system beneath them, because an
31	index can only be as traceable as the statistics it consumes. That dependency is rarely examined.
32	
33	The form is well established. The Ocean Health Index scores every coastal country against ten goals
34	for a coupled human–ocean system (Halpern et al., 2012). The methodological difficulties are
35	documented rather than novel: the standard reference treats imputation, weighting and aggregation as
36	the three choices that most move a published ranking (Nardo et al., 2008). The Indonesian case
37	therefore belongs to a recognised class of measurement problem.
38	
39	Ocean accounting has developed as the candidate infrastructure for that problem. The System of
40	Environmental-Economic Accounting Ecosystem Accounting framework was adopted by the UN Statistical
41	Commission in 2021 as an international statistical standard. It supplies the extent, condition,
42	service and asset structures on which such accounts are built (United Nations et al., 2024), and a
43	complete national sequence has been specified in detail (IDEEA Group, 2021). Ocean accounts have been
44	argued to belong in the national statistical apparatus rather than in analysis, on the grounds that
45	recurrent policy reporting requires a production system and not a study (Fenichel et al., 2020).
46	Sub-national compilation across extent, service flows and ocean-economy production has been
47	demonstrated operationally (Gacutan et al., 2022). In Indonesia, the spatial basis such accounts
48	require has been tested empirically: grid size and designation rule change the extent estimate
49	materially (Rahayu et al., 2024). None of that work establishes whether an account compiled to those
50	specifications can carry the reporting load of a statutory index. That is a question about production
51	capacity, not about accounting method.
52	
53	Indonesia is where the policy demand is most explicit. Its ocean policy lineage runs from the 2017
54	Indonesian Ocean Policy through a development framework (BAPPENAS, 2021) to a blue economy roadmap
55	(BAPPENAS, 2024). A recent review of that lineage names limited data support among the obstacles to
56	implementation (Wuwung et al., 2024). Quantitative work on Indonesian blue economy indicators remains
57	thin. Rinaldi et al. (2025) construct their own sub-national index for Sumatera on a human development
58	index template and regress it on regional product. That is the closest existing treatment, and it is
59	not an analysis of the official index. The production system beneath the official index has not been
60	examined.
61	
62	The Indonesia Blue Economy Index (IBEI) is an official national development indicator under
63	presidential regulation Perpres 12/2025. Provincial targets for 2045 are assigned by joint circular of
64	the Ministry of Home Affairs and the Ministry of National Development Planning (BAPPENAS) for carriage
65	in long-term development plans. A province cannot decline to be scored, and BAPPENAS cannot decline to
66	score it. Against that recurring obligation, the compilation activity is registered in Indonesia's
67	statistical activity system as sectoral statistics gathered by secondary collection from the
68	publications of the Ministry of Marine Affairs and Fisheries (MMAF, Indonesian KKP) and of Statistics
69	Indonesia (BPS) (SIRUSA kegiatan 94936). The index conducts no primary collection of its own. It
70	inherits whatever those agencies publish, at whatever resolution they publish it. Section 2 documents
71	the traceability defects that follow.
72	
73	This study assesses Indonesia's ocean accounts as compilation infrastructure for an index the state is
74	already obliged to produce, rather than as an alternative source of indicator values. The assessment
75	is built on the IBEI's own published documentation, on the primary reports of the Indonesian account
76	pilots, and on the international accounting standard, with every claim tagged for maturity. It
77	contributes four things: a documented account of what makes provincial compilation hard, a three-tier
78	indicator mapping scored against the register's verbatim constituent data items rather than its
79	indicator names, a quantification of where in the index the account-relevant weight sits, and a
80	register of the conditions precedent for continued supply.
81	
82	Two boundaries hold throughout. Account compilation has its own statutory and analytical purposes
83	under MMAF's mandate, and supplying an index is one downstream use among several. Nothing here
84	proposes replacing the IBEI or subordinating either framework to the other; the relationship examined
85	is statistical infrastructure feeding a policy instrument. The object of study is bounded to the
86	44-indicator edition of the index, and every Indonesian ocean account output discussed carries maturity
87	tag (b), experimental or pilot compilation, with no statistical work-programme entry and no revision
88	policy.
89	
90	Three findings follow. First, read against verbatim constituent data items rather than indicator
91	names, the three ecosystem condition indicators are definitionally matched to account output rather
92	than category-mismatched. That corrects a judgement in earlier work, including a provisional mapping by
93	the present authors that scored account-derivable shares at figures moving from 61% to 39% to 2.8% as
94	the assumed indicator set changed, the last over a 36-indicator denominator the register does not have
95	(Section 4.2). Second, what separates the account routes into and out of reach is not compilation
96	maturity but content. The routes Indonesia has compiled at pilot scale bear on 12.79% of the
97	composite's published weight and the one route compiled nowhere bears on 13.70%, a difference of
98	1.07 times; yet no compiled account produces the constituent data item the register asks for at
99	any of these rows (Section 4.3). Third, the share an account would itself supply is about 2.33%, and
100	what the mature account types offer at those rows is a production process rather than a stock of
101	substitutable values (Section 5).

104	# 2. The compilation problem
105	
106	A recurring statutory obligation to score every province annually sits on top of a compilation
107	activity that conducts no primary collection of its own. The obligation is assigned rather than
108	volunteered. Perpres 12/2025 carries the IBEI as an official national development indicator, and
109	joint circular SEB Mendagri/Bappenas No. 2/2024 distributes a 2045 target to each province for
110	carriage in its long-term development plan (Juknis, Tabel 2, p. 69 [pdf 79]). That the compilation
111	beneath it collects nothing directly is a design decision recorded in Indonesia's own statistical
112	metadata system, and its consequences are legible as specific strains in the published output. This
113	section establishes both from the IBEI's own documentation, and closes by declaring the pillar
114	structure the rest of the article computes on, since the handbook prints two.
115	
116	## 2.1 Compilation without collection
117	
118	SIRUSA registers the IBEI compilation as *Kompilasi Produk Administrasi* under *Statistik Sektoral*,
119	implemented by BAPPENAS's Directorate of Marine Affairs and Fisheries, with an empty *Identitas
120	Rekomendasi* (kegiatan 94936, retrieved 2026-08-13). The design is secondary compilation throughout.
121	Collection mode is *Pengumpulan data sekunder*, the instrument is *Lainnya: Publikasi KKP dan BPS*,
122	and the unit is the province. The register records no pilot survey, no non-response adjustment, no
123	staff training, zero supervisors and zero enumerators. The index's supply is therefore fixed to
124	whatever KKP and BPS have already published, at the resolution and vintage they published it.
125	
126	The collection capability the index draws on is real, and it sits with those two producers. That is
127	the statutory division this article holds to: BAPPENAS plans, whilst MMAF and BPS produce
128	statistics. What the register establishes is not an absence of capability in the system. It is the
129	absence of any collection instrument inside the compilation itself.
130	
131	The reporting window is narrow and is published from two directions. SIRUSA schedules collection
132	from 1 April to 1 May 2025 and dissemination from 1 August to 1 September 2025. The Juknis puts the
133	first phase of its monitoring and evaluation cycle in October to December of year *n* (p. 71 [pdf
134	81]). Read together, these imply that an upstream figure not public by roughly April of year *n+1*
135	cannot enter that cycle's index. Neither document reconciles them, and only the first phase was
136	extracted into the corpus, so the implication is stated conditionally `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Figure 1
137	draws the two calendars against the thirteen-step production chain they feed, and marks the single
138	step at which account-derived data could enter.

140	## 2.2 What the published output records
141	
142	Two defects carry the argument, and both are failures of provenance rather than of arithmetic.
143	
144	Gaps are filled by prediction, and the prediction is not recorded. Annex 1 of the Report specifies a
145	machine-learning framework predicting missing values from socio-economic proxies under two scenarios,
146	with SUSENAS named as the proxy source (pp. 64–66 [pdf 76–78]). The framework itself is specified in
147	detail. What is missing sits downstream of it: the annex names no imputed indicator, no count of
148	imputed cells, no selected model, no achieved error, and no proxy beyond SUSENAS. Under the first
149	scenario an indicator absent in every province is predicted for every province. No reader of any
150	published table can therefore distinguish a measured provincial value from a predicted one
151	`[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Which indicators were imputed must not be inferred from which look hard to collect.
152	
153	Three provinces record no value across three sub-pillars. Papua Barat Daya, Papua Selatan and Papua
154	Tengah each record 0.00 for marine-based industry, technology and governance, and Papua Tengah's
155	headline value of 8.51 is the lowest in the annex (Report p. 70 [pdf 82]). All three were created in
156	the 2022 provincial split. Juknis Tabel 2 carries the same three provinces at 2023 baselines of 49.25,
157	33.50 and 21.48 against 2025 achievements of 13.16, 12.25 and 8.51 (p. 69 [pdf 79]), and the two
158	documents head those columns differently. Whether the zeros reflect unavailability or a measured zero
159	is not stated in either document. They are reported as printed, and read as consistent with a
160	compilation discontinuity rather than as proof of one `[ASSUMPTION]`. This is where a new
161	administrative unit needs a spatially re-aggregable source rather than a republished provincial total.
162	
163	Table 1 records four further symptoms without arguing them here. Each is uninterrogable from the
164	published record.
165	
166	The denominator of the obligation is itself unstable inside the guidance that creates it. Indonesia
167	has 38 provinces. The Report's Annex 2 tabulates 37, Juknis Tabel 2 lists 38 rows plus a national
168	row with Papua Pegunungan carrying a dash in all three columns, the monitoring chapter asserts
169	comparability over *37 provinsi* (p. 71 [pdf 81]), and the sensitivity chapter thirty pages later
170	recomputes the index *untuk 38 provinsi* across 59 scenarios (p. 101 [pdf 111]). Two of the four are
171	in the same handbook. The number used in any sentence here is the number that sentence is about.

173	## 2.3 The pillar conflict, and the locus this article adopts
174	
175	The handbook prints two pillar structures. Tabel 1 prints four, with an *Enabler (teknologi & tata
176	kelola)* pillar holding indicators 15 to 18. Tabel 4, Tabel 5 and Gambar 11 print three, placing
177	tourism, technology and governance as economy sub-pillars. This article does not resolve the conflict.
178	All weight arithmetic here follows the three-pillar structure, for the stated reason that weights are
179	published only there, whilst register rows keep their Tabel 1 labels.
180	
181	The choice is material in one place and immaterial in another. Every indicator in the Tier A mapping
182	sits in the environment pillar under both structures, so Section 4.2's arithmetic is unaffected
183	`[ASSUMPTION]`. Section 4.3's figures are computed over precisely the pillars across which indicators
184	15 to 18 move: the economy pillar's account-relevant share of about 26.1% and the governance share of
185	about 5.15% are structure-dependent, and no four-pillar equivalent can be given from the published
186	weights. Both are identified as structure-dependent at each use.

215	# 3. Materials and methods
216	
217	## 3.1 Design
218	
219	The study is a documentary analysis of two published specifications read against each other: the
220	IBEI register as printed by its custodian, and the account structures of the SEEA Ecosystem
221	Accounting standard together with the specified sequence of national ocean accounts. No sampling,
222	statistical inference or hypothesis test is involved, and no result reported here is an estimate
223	with a sampling distribution. The equivalent burden is reproducibility of a scoring rule and of an
224	arithmetic chain, which the deposited materials discharge.
225	
226	## 3.2 What an account has to be, for this test
227	
228	The membership rule below tests register rows against account output, so the account side needs
229	specifying first. Compilation infrastructure, as used here, means a production system that turns
230	primary observation into a statistical output on a repeating cycle, carrying its own spatial unit,
231	provenance chain and revision rule. Four properties do that work. They are this study's synthesis
232	rather than a construct the standard names as such, assembled from provisions it states separately.
233	
234	*A standing unit and classification definition* is fixed by the account types themselves. *A
235	balancing identity* renders an unexplained change a visible residual: closing stock equals opening
236	stock plus additions less reductions, with additions and reductions each split into managed and
237	unmanaged, so that the nature of a change is recorded rather than inferred (United Nations et al.,
238	2024, paras 4.14–4.15, p. 86). *A re-aggregable spatial basis* follows from basic spatial units
239	being "a means of implementing the ecosystem accounting approach rather than … elements in a nested
240	hierarchy" (App. A1.1, para. A1.3), with the reporting geography a compiler-delineated accounting
241	area; provincial and district figures are then two aggregations of one production run rather than
242	two data requests. *Documented vintage and revision* follows from the requirement that an account be
243	consistent over time and across classifications (para. 1.60, p. 18), and that previous estimates be
244	revised where updated imagery or methods permit a reassessment, with the size of the revision
245	recoverable as a difference (para. 4.17, p. 88).
246	
247	Two of the four are properties of a compiled account rather than of a well-run register. Section 5
248	concedes the other two.
249	
250	## 3.3 Corpus
251	
252	A citation-anchored corpus of 199 chunks was built over the index's own published documentation and
253	is released with this article, so every quotation resolves to a numbered record rather than a page
254	range. Extraction was purposive. The build, dated 2026-08-14, comprises 106
255	chunks from the Juknis (BAPPENAS 2025a), 49 from the IBEI Report 2024 in its December 2025 revision
256	(BAPPENAS 2025b), 15 from Rinaldi et al. (2025) and 29 from the SIRUSA metadata (kegiatan 94936
257	together with indikator 105371 and 105709–105718, all retrieved 2026-08-13), at the printed-page
258	ranges listed in the deposit. Each record carries its document and slice, the verbatim Indonesian
259	text, an English gloss used nowhere as a quotation source, the printed page with the PDF page in
260	brackets, a maturity tag, and a reconstruction flag, which 17 records carry. Chunks in conflict are
261	recorded as conflicts rather than reconciled, and the four-against-three pillar contradiction is
262	held as one such record.
263	
264	Account-side evidence sits outside that corpus and was read directly: the three MMAF 2022 reports,
265	of which Gili Matra is the compilation (MMAF, 2022a, 2022b, 2022c); BPS SEEA-workshop decks
266	(Tasriah et al., 2022; Wulandari and Jannah, 2023); Rahayu et al. (2024); Kepmen KP 44/2023; and the
267	GOAP country page (retrieved 2026-08-13). All carry clean text layers; no optical character recognition
268	was applied. Retrieval and
269	assembly ran from 11 to 16 August 2026. A join table reproduces all 44 register rows with each row's
270	*Data Pembentuk Indikator* constituent data items verbatim, transcribed from Juknis Tabel 1, pp.
271	11–13 [pdf 21–23], totalling 62 items. That join table, not the running text of the handbook, is the
272	object the membership rule was applied to.
273	
274	Searching was bilingual and was run over the document set named above rather than over a statistical
275	catalogue. The Indonesian terms used were *indeks ekonomi biru*, *neraca laut*, *akun laut*, *neraca
276	ekosistem*, *unit spasial dasar laut*, *peta jalan ekonomi biru* and *Satu Data Indonesia*, with
277	their English equivalents and no date restriction. No dedicated search of the BPS statistical
278	catalogue or of the Satu Data Indonesia portal was undertaken, and by project constraint no
279	Indonesian agency was approached. Every absence claim in this article is therefore stated as unfound
280	within the named set, and is not a claim about the Indonesian statistical system. Within that set,
281	no Indonesian statistical register supplying provenance, spatial unit, vintage and revision policy
282	for marine ecosystem extent and condition products, separately from an account structure, was
283	encountered `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. That negative bears on the register objection weighed in Section 5.2.

285	## 3.4 Unit of analysis and the membership rule
286	
287	The unit is the register row, of which there are 44. The object tested is the row's constituent data
288	items rather than its title, on the Indonesian wording. Each row was graded on one of three grounds.
289	A row is *matched* where a constituent data item names a quantity that a defined SEEA Ecosystem
290	Accounting output contains. It is *partial* where account output supplies a component of the quantity
291	but not the quantity itself. It is a *provenance question* where account output bears on the row
292	without supplying it. Tier A is the union of the three grades and has five members: indicators 19, 20
293	and 21 matched, 24 partial, and 27 a provenance question. The remaining 39 rows are graded none.
294	
295	The rule was applied item by item, so a row carrying several items takes the highest grade any one
296	attains, which makes the 62 items rather than the 44 rows the tested population. Two decisions were
297	fixed before scoring: that indicator titles are not evidence of what a row requires, titles having
298	produced the predecessor figures this analysis rejects; and that a definitional match carries no
299	claim about whether anything has been compiled. Grading was by the authors against those criteria,
300	not blind, with no second coder and no adjudication protocol. Every row's grade and ground is
301	deposited as Supplementary Table S1.
302	
303	Membership in Tier A is a definitional property. The separate operative test applied to the account
304	side is repeatability into a dated window rather than demonstrated capability, since the obligation
305	is annual and can receive only what a producer has already released at the required vintage.
306	Maturity is tagged on every claim that something exists, as (a) published official statistics, (b)
307	experimental or pilot compilation, (c) methodology under development, or (d) announced but not
308	started. The tag describes the datum rather than the document reporting it.
309	
310	## 3.5 Weight arithmetic and its one ambiguity
311	
312	Weights are taken from the Juknis as printed: pillar weights from Tabel 4, p. 94 [pdf 104];
313	sub-pillar splits from Tabel 5, p. 94 [pdf 104]; variable weights from Tabel 6, pp. 95–96 [pdf
314	105–106]; and the sub-pillar shares of Gambar 11, p. 93 [pdf 103], used as an independent integer
315	cross-check. Shares of the composite are products of those published percentages, carried to two
316	decimal places from one-decimal inputs and reported in prose to one decimal with 'about'. Every
317	weight used here is printed; none is reconstructed. Where a total is built from component shares it
318	is the sum of the rounded components rather than the product of the unrounded inputs, which moves
319	three totals by 0.01 and is stated so either route is reproducible. All weight arithmetic follows
320	the three-pillar structure (Section 2.3).
321	
322	The handbook does not state whether the percentages in Tabel 4 to Tabel 6 are raw
323	first-principal-component loadings or normalised contributions, noting only that *besaran pembobot
324	dihasilkan secara otomatis melalui proses PCA* (p. 93 [pdf 103]). The distinction matters because
325	raw loadings need not sum to anything in particular, so shares built by multiplying them would have
326	no defined denominator. The test applied is to renormalise each printed group to sum to 100 and
327	recompute. It changes almost nothing, for a reason that is a property of the tables rather than a
328	stipulation of this study: all fifteen printed weight groups already sum to 100.0 within one-decimal
329	rounding, none departing by more than a tenth of a point. The largest effect anywhere is 0.03
330	percentage points, moving the four Tier B sub-pillars from 26.15% to 26.12%; 18.45%, 7.64%, 3.58%,
331	2.33% and governance at 5.15% are unchanged, and Gambar 11's printed integers of 26 and 5 agree with
332	both. Fifteen independent groups of raw loadings would not sum to 100 by accident, so the
333	percentages are read here as normalised contributions and the derived shares are carried without a
334	denominator caveat. That inference is this study's reading of the tables and is stated nowhere in
335	the source `[ASSUMPTION]`. The census is deposited as Supplementary Data S2.
336	
337	## 3.6 Reporting conventions
338	
339	Indonesian decimal commas are preserved inside quotation and rendered as points in prose. Quotations
340	are from the Indonesian source text. Three inline flags mark evidential status: `[EVIDENCE GAP]`
341	where a required source was not found, `[ASSUMPTION]` where the argument depends on something
342	unconfirmed, and `[CONTESTED DENOMINATOR]` on the Tier A share of 7.64% and figures derived from it.
343	That third flag carries one meaning throughout and not two: it records that the share measures what
344	these account types bear on rather than what an account would supply. It is not a flag about the
345	weights, which are printed. Conflicting sources are reported as conflicts and are not averaged or
346	adjudicated.

348	# 4. Results
349	
350	## 4.1 The compiled record
351	
352	Indonesian ocean accounting has produced one pilot compilation, in one marine protected area, in one
353	province, on one vintage. Every output carries maturity tag (b).
354	
355	The compiled record comprises the Gili Matra Marine Recreational Park pilot off north Lombok, together
356	with one peer-reviewed grid-optimisation study at Saleh Bay, Sumbawa (Rahayu et al., 2024). Both sit in
357	West Nusa Tenggara, so NTB is the single province carrying a documented published compilation located
358	by this study. A national Ocean Accounts Dashboard was launched in July 2025 and is described by GOAP
359	as publicly available and interactive on a harmonised spatial frame (GOAP country page, retrieved
360	2026-08-13). That is a programme self-statement about a platform, and it evidences no compilation
361	beyond the pilot record.
362	
363	The pilot's footprint belongs in its own units, because the smallness is part of the finding: 2,980.84
364	ha on a 25 m by 25 m marine basic spatial unit (MBSU) grid of 48,211 cells, opening in 2015 and
365	closing in 2021, those years chosen on data availability (MMAF, 2022a, pp. 30, 39 [pdf 44, 53]). The two
366	figures do not reconcile. At 625 m² each, 48,211 cells give 3,013.19 ha against the 2,980.84 ha
367	printed, a shortfall of 32.35 ha or 1.1%. That would be consistent with cells clipped at the park
368	boundary, but is nowhere explained, and is recorded as observed rather than resolved `[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
369	Against a statutory obligation to score every province annually, this is an area smaller than many
370	single districts, compiled once.
371	
372	Maturity is the programme's own self-description rather than an external verdict. GOAP labels the three
373	2022 MMAF outputs 'preliminary consultation drafts', and BPS lists scaling up to the total Indonesian
374	marine area among its outstanding challenges (Tasriah et al., 2022, slide 9). There is no BPS ocean
375	account release, no entry in the national statistical work programme, no published revision policy, and
376	no published compilation cost per site or per unit. The ecosystem scope is confined to mangrove,
377	seagrass and coral reef.
378	
379	The three account types differ in completeness of the account form, not in the standing of the
380	compilation producing them. Extent is the only type with a verified opening, change and closing series:
381	the pilot publishes a complete asset account, with additions and reductions itemised and an
382	ecosystem-conversion matrix alongside (MMAF, 2022a, tab. 5–6, pp. 52–53 [pdf 66–67]). Condition is
383	compiled in a limited form, as cover-based measurement at Tier 1 to Tier 2 read against documented
384	reference bands rather than as a normalised multi-indicator composite (pp. 31–33 [pdf 45–47]). Valuation
385	is the least developed and may not be described as an ecosystem services account in physical units.
386	What exists is benefit-transferred monetary valuation, rising from IDR 45.24 bn per year in 2015 to IDR
387	64.17 bn in 2021 (pp. 36, 60 [pdf 50, 74]). Benefit transfer is not the exchange-value basis the
388	standard requires, a limit BPS states itself (Tasriah et al., 2022, slide 9). No ecosystem services
389	account in physical units has been compiled in Indonesia. Supplementary S5 reproduces all three.
390	
391	Extent is also where two official series diverge, and the divergence is this article's strongest

401	## 4.2 Indicators the existing accounts can serve
402	
403	Five of the register's 44 rows are the whole of what an ocean account touches. Indicators 19, 20 and 21
404	name quantities an account's output structure already contains; indicators 24 and 27 sit adjacent to
405	account output and are disposed of rather than claimed (Table 2). Figure 2 gives the assignment for all
406	44 rows with the constituent data items in full.
407	
408	On the compilation axis the concession is complete. None of the five indicator values has been
409	published by any Indonesian account, for any province or any site `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Every capability
410	claim here is a claim about definitional correspondence and a demonstrated input layer, never about an
411	existing output. All five rows sit in the environment pillar under both pillar structures, so the
412	unresolved conflict does not reach this arithmetic.
413	
414	The three condition indicators share one grammatical form. Indicator 19 asks for the *Persentase
415	kawasan terumbu karang dengan tutupan karang hidup antara 50-100% per provinsi*, indicator 20 for the
416	seagrass equivalent, and indicator 21 for *kerapatan tutupan* in mangrove (Juknis, Tabel 1, p. 12 [pdf
417	22]). Each names a percentage of area falling within a stated cover or density band, per province. That
418	is a tabulation of extent by class, not a normalised index against a reference condition. Earlier
419	assessments judged that Indonesia lacks the condition account these indicators require. They do not
420	require one, so the judgement answers a question the index does not pose.
421	
422	What the pilot sets beside that specification is the input layer, not the tabulation. The MBSU extent
423	surface exists, and cover is measured at survey stations: live coral cover of 18.11% in 2016 and live
424	hard coral cover of 16.99% in 2021, which the report does not state to be the same measure and which
425	are therefore not read here as a series, and seagrass cover averaging 32.28% with a station maximum of
426	82.01% (MMAF, 2022a, pp. 40, 45 [pdf 54, 59]). The 2021 coral figure is unreconciled in the source,
427	where the narrative reports 16.99% whilst the report's own tab. 3 on the same page prints 18.36%. The
428	cross-tabulation of extent by cover class is not published. The step from the input layer to indicator
429	19 is one tabulation that has not been performed.
430	
431	The match frays at four edges. Two are definitional. The 50% threshold is the index's own and matches
432	no Indonesian condition class boundary in use, the pilot classifying reef condition on the bands of
433	Giyanto et al. (2017), where moderate runs from 19% to below 35%. Reclassifying a continuous per-cell
434	cover surface at 50% would be trivial, but only where condition is mapped wall to wall rather than
435	surveyed at stations, and no obtained document evidences a wall-to-wall condition surface
436	`[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Where national data exist only as classes pre-binned on the Giyanto boundaries, the
437	50% cut cannot be recovered, and the coral row becomes a definition gap rather than a coverage gap
438	`[ASSUMPTION]`. The mangrove unit differs outright: indicator 21 asks for canopy density as a
439	percentage, whilst the pilot reports m² per ha and individuals per ha, with no documented conversion
440	(p. 47 [pdf 61]) `[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
441	
442	The other two edges are unfixed choices. Neither the minimum mapping unit nor the denominator is fixed
443	by the register or by the SIRUSA definitions `[ASSUMPTION]`. And the extent estimate is itself a
444	function of the grid rule: Rahayu et al. (2024) report mangrove error of 44.06% under a 25 m hierarchy
445	scheme against 1.59% under 25 m dominance, so until a grid rule is adopted the account's figure is a
446	family of figures. A vintage gap sits alongside these, the only verified series closing in 2021 against
447	the index's T-1 requirement.
448	
449	Indicator 24 asks for the *Total luas kawasan mangrove, rawa, dan lahan gambut yang direstorasi per
450	provinsi*, glossed in SIRUSA as degraded area subsequently replanted. That is an activity input measured
451	in hectares planted. The pilot's asset account carries a managed-expansion line that is unpopulated at
452	Gili Matra, where every addition is natural expansion and the only managed entry is 11.19 ha of managed
453	mangrove regression. The correspondence is structural rather than demonstrated: an account of this form
454	records realised managed change, whilst the indicator counts hectares planted. A scope mismatch
455	compounds it, the indicator's three land covers being mangrove, swamp and peatland against the marine
456	account's mangrove, seagrass and coral reef.
457	
458	Indicator 27 asks for the *Total luas kawasan konservasi perairan yang ditetapkan oleh KKP, KLHK, dan
459	Pemerintah Daerah per provinsi*, and is in the tier for a different reason. At 22.0% it is the largest
460	single variable in the resource-quality sub-pillar, about 4.06% of the composite, and it needs no
461	account to be produced. Designated area is an administrative datum already held by its custodians. The
462	account's near-term contribution here is reconciliation of mapped extent to the designated polygon, not
463	supply.
464	
465	The weight chain is shown openly as a multiplication. The environment pillar carries 36.9% of the
466	composite and the resource-quality sub-pillar 50.0% of the pillar (Juknis, Tabel 4 and Tabel 5, p. 94
467	[pdf 104]), giving 18.45%, of which the five Tier A variables sum to about 7.64%
468	`[CONTESTED DENOMINATOR]`. Figure 3 places that band inside the published weight structure. The tag
469	names one specific objection: 7.64% is a defensible measure of the share these account types bear on,
470	and an indefensible measure of the share an account would supply, because most of it sits at rows the
471	mapping has itself disqualified. The decomposition is therefore reported in three parts: about 2.33% at
472	the three matched condition rows, about 1.25% at indicator 24 as a partial, and about 4.06% at
473	indicator 27, which the account does not supply. **The share at which an account is the prospective
474	supply route is about 2.33%**, rising to about 3.58% only if indicator 24's structural correspondence
475	is credited in full, which the activity-against-outcome mismatch and the scope gap indicate it should
476	not be. The lower figure is operative and the upper an outer bound.
477	
478	The register is silent on a point worth recording: no producing agency is stated for any of the 44 rows
479	`[EVIDENCE GAP]`.

496	## 4.3 Extension routes, and what separates them
497	
498	What separates the account routes into and out of reach is content, not compilation maturity. The
499	routes with a pilot-scale Indonesian compilation bear on 12.79% of the composite. The one route with
500	no Indonesian compilation at any maturity, the classification-dependent social share, bears on 13.70%.
501	That is a difference of 1.07 times, and it is not the finding. The finding is that no compiled
502	account produces the constituent data item the register asks for at any of these rows, and that the
503	outputs the pilot does produce — monetary supply and use, zone areas, protection expenditure — carry no
504	weight in the index at all (Figure 4). The arithmetic below follows the three-pillar locus declared at
505	Section 2.3, so every tourism, technology and governance figure carries that locus with it.
506	
507	**Tier B adds no quantity the register lacks.** Four economy sub-pillars carry about 26.1% of the
508	composite, which Gambar 11's integers confirm at 5 + 8 + 6 + 7 = 26 (Juknis, Tabel 5 and Gambar 11,
509	pp. 93–94 [pdf 103–104]). Every constituent data item in them is an administrative quantity KKP and BPS
510	routinely compile: production and export tonnages, passenger and vessel counts, site counts. No route
511	in Table 3 supplies a quantity of a kind the register does not already specify. What an account adds is
512	an industry boundary consistent with international and Indonesian industrial classification, under
513	which such quantities become comparable across provinces and cycles (IDEEA Group, 2021; Fenichel et
514	al., 2020). Whether recompiling on that boundary would change the values cannot be determined here, and
515	under weights re-estimated by principal components it cannot be assumed not to.
516	
517	One correction inverts the intuitive fisheries mapping. Sub-pillar A is 83.0% aquaculture by published
518	weight against 6.3% for capture-fisheries volume (Tabel 6, p. 95 [pdf 105]), so reading production
519	against a stock assessment bears on about 0.31% of the composite whilst a consistent aquaculture
520	boundary bears on about 4.11% (S4.2). The intellectually interesting route is the numerically negligible
521	one.
522	
523	**Tier C is what the register has no term for.** None of the 44 constituent data items names an
524	ecosystem service flow, an asset value or a depletion term. The sharpest instance is an identity rather
525	than an aspiration: indicators 2 to 5 are unsigned production volumes, so a province raises its score
526	by producing more, and no term records whether the stock producing it is being drawn down. The missing
527	term is depletion (United Nations et al., 2024, para. A1.25), and the obstacle is that sustainable
528	yield by province and stock is not published, which makes stock assessment a prerequisite rather than
529	an alternative. Ecosystem services in physical units are specified in full (ch. 7) and compiled nowhere
530	in Indonesia; the nearest candidate is coastal protection from mangrove and reef, measurable against
531	the layers the pilot already produces. Combined presentations (paras 14.56–14.57) are the one row
532	available immediately, a presentation choice rather than a data gap.
533	
534	**The social pillar carries the largest account-relevant share, and nothing above methodology under
535	development has been compiled anywhere.** Seven of the thirteen social indicators require a
536	reproducible definition of the population working in the fisheries sector as a survey domain. Five are
537	ratios of a fisheries-sector population to a provincial total: indicator 32 divides *Jumlah penduduk
538	bekerja di sektor perikanan yang menerima PKH per provinsi* by the provincial total receiving that
539	transfer, and indicators 37, 42, 43 and 44 repeat the construction for health insurance,

541	23]). Two more presuppose the same identification: indicator 34 is a headcount of *nelayan dan
542	pembudidaya ikan*, and indicator 33 counts *pekerja perempuan di sektor perikanan*, which requires that
543	population sex-disaggregated. None is computable without the domain joined to SUSENAS, SAKERNAS and

545	
546	The share is about 13.7% of the composite, decomposed row by row in Supplementary S4 so that a reader
547	may reject a row rather than the total. Coastal poverty at about 0.33% is the one row where the
548	handbook disagrees with itself, Tabel 1 building indicator 38 from a spatial domain and Tabel 6 from a
549	sectoral one (p. 96 [pdf 106]); the figure is held outside the 13.7% on the Tabel 1 wording, and the
550	Tabel 6 wording would take the total to about 14.0%. The 13.7% is the classification-standard claim,
551	whilst the pillar's published 30.5% is the wider claim that an ocean social account's structure

553	require an ocean-sector domain is this study's reading of the verbatim constituent data items and is

624	## 4.4 Conditions precedent for supply
625	
626	A definitional match is not a supply arrangement. Five conditions must hold before pilot account output
627	could serve the IBEI as a repeating input, none is satisfied in the obtained record, and they are not
628	equally binding (Table 4). Three are one-off acts of codification that a single decision by MMAF, BPS or
629	BAPPENAS could discharge. Two are discharged only by sustaining a production cycle, and those two
630	decide whether the mapping in Section 4.2 is a recommendation or a wish.
631	
632	The register would be refuted by producing an adopted national marine spatial standard, a BPS
633	statistical work-programme entry for a marine ecosystem account, or an extent compilation covering more
634	than the pilot sites and closing after 2021.
635	
636	The two recurring conditions are the binding ones. SIRUSA specifies reference period T-1 for all five
637	mapped variables. The single verified compilation closes at 2021 and was published the following year,
638	a lag T-1 can accommodate; what is absent is the second vintage, and no repeat compilation of the pilot
639	sites was found `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Figure 5 sets the required cycle against the vintages the accounts
640	have produced: the gap between the 2021 closing year and the reference year the 2025 cycle requires is
641	three reference years. The second is a revision policy, for which the standard supplies the substance
642	(paras 4.17–4.18, p. 88) and Indonesia supplies no instrument `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. An account silently
643	improved between vintages would move a province's index value with no ecosystem change behind it, and
644	nothing in the published record would flag it.
645	
646	One condition follows from the standard rather than from Indonesian practice. Condition is not additive
647	across areas, its variable account being an area-weighted arithmetic mean over the assets of an
648	ecosystem type (para. 5.54, p. 105), so a provincial ratio cannot be recovered by averaging district
649	values and a supplier would need to publish the denominator alongside the percentage.
650	
651	The institutional condition is unmet against an instrument whose own term has run. Kepmen KP 44/2023
652	constituted the Tim Penyusun Neraca Sumber Daya Laut in February 2023 and set its term to expire on
653	31 December 2023, and no renewal instrument was located `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. The July 2025 dashboard launch
654	indicates continued activity under some instrument not obtained, so the defensible statement is that no
655	renewal was located rather than that the mandate lapsed. Two sectoral products feeding one another does
656	not by itself constitute an accredited statistical input. The gap runs in both directions: no microdata
657	are released, so even a compliant supplier could not verify that its value survived into the published
658	provincial score.
659	
660	One design recommendation follows. Where a province has no account, an account-derived indicator would
661	itself be predicted from socio-economic proxies under the framework the Report publishes (pp. 64–66
662	[pdf 76–78]), so an environmental value would become partly a function of economic and social
663	covariates. The framework's first scenario trains on the previous cycle's historical data, which a newly
664	introduced indicator does not have. **Insertion should be phased behind coverage: compile first, insert
665	second.** That is a sequencing recommendation, not a change to the index's method. Supplementary S3
666	carries the derivation for each condition.

684	# 5. Discussion
685	
686	## 5.1 What compilation changes, and what it does not
687	
688	What an account offers this index at the rows it touches is a production process, not a stock of
689	substitutable values. That is a narrower claim than an efficiency claim, and the evidence assembled
690	here would not support the wider one.
691	
692	The register decomposes into 62 constituent data items across its 44 rows. Three of them —
693	indicators 19, 20 and 21 — are cross-classifications of one extent-and-condition compilation, so the
694	consolidation is three items into one production run, out of 62. The argument is not that the count
695	is large. It is that those three are among the items the index presently has no way to show it
696	measured rather than predicted.
697	
698	A cover-class percentage compiled from a gridded extent surface and a classified cover layer carries
699	its own spatial unit, its own opening and closing stocks, and a provenance chain back to the imagery
700	and field survey behind it. It is reproducible in the next cycle by repeating the compilation. A
701	value taken from a secondary publication, or predicted where no publication exists, carries none of
702	those properties, and the published record does not distinguish the two (Section 2.2).
703	
704	That the distinction is worth publishing is established outside Indonesia. The Ocean Health Index, a
705	composite of comparable construction in the same domain (Halpern et al., 2012), reports that 18.5%
706	of its global score rests on gapfilled rather than observed data, ranging from 1% to 43% across its
707	ten goals and exceeding 60% for ten regions that are predominantly small island territories. Its
708	authors recommend that gapfilling be tracked from the outset and presented in the same framework as
709	the scores themselves (Frazier et al., 2016). The IBEI publishes an imputation framework and no
710	per-indicator record, so the comparison identifies a reporting reform rather than a data programme —
711	and one the index could adopt without any account being compiled.
712	
713	Everything downstream of the input is untouched. Account supply changes the value entering a slot,
714	not the slot, its weight or the aggregation rule. The three-stage principal component procedure, the
715	loading-factor weights, the pillar shares and every limitation the index publishes about itself
716	stand exactly as they are.
717	
718	## 5.2 The counter-instance, and what survives it
719	
720	The strongest evidence against this article's own claim is in its own account-side record. The one
721	compiled Indonesian account has produced two closing stocks and two deltas for the same site, the
722	same three ecosystems and the same two years, disagreeing not by a constant offset but by a ratio
723	that moves across ecosystems and across the accounting window (Section 4.1). The pilot's own
724	reporting compounds it, the narrative giving 16.99% live hard coral cover for 2021 against 18.36% in
725	the report's table on the same page. Change is the quantity a balancing identity exists to report,
726	and provenance is what this article claims accounts buy.
727	
728	Read one way, this refutes the provenance claim outright. Read against what the two documents
729	actually are, it qualifies it in a specific place. The two series are parallel releases of the same
730	underlying mapping layer, published in a presentation and a report, and neither is an account of the
731	other. Publishing account-shaped tables is not the same as running an account. The property claimed
732	here is not that compilation prevents divergence, which nothing in the standard promises. It is that
733	a balancing identity gives divergence somewhere to appear: opening stock, additions, reductions and
734	closing stock are four entries that must reconcile, and that a second compilation must restate
735	rather than replace. The Gili Matra case is evidence for that reading in negative form. Precisely
736	because neither document was compiled under such an identity, and because no revision rule required
737	either to restate the other, a 6.26 ha disagreement about coral change could be published twice and
738	reconciled nowhere. That is an argument from the account form rather than a demonstration, since no
739	Indonesian compilation has yet been revised under such a rule `[ASSUMPTION]`.
740	
741	A second objection bears on the same argument. A well-documented statistical register could supply
742	several of the four properties without any account being compiled, and on two of the four it holds
743	outright. A standing unit and classification definition is an act of codification, requiring a
744	published definition of the mapping unit, the ecosystem classes and the landward and seaward limits;
745	nothing about compiling an account is needed to write one down. Documented vintage and provenance is
746	likewise a metadata requirement any well-run register discharges. The spatial property is
747	intermediate: a register can mandate a grid and a minimum mapping unit but cannot itself produce the
748	re-aggregable cell-level tabulation the mandate presupposes. What does not reduce to codification is
749	the residual — the balancing identity, and the revision rule that alters the opening and closing
750	entries themselves. Both are properties of double-entry over a stock. The residual is thin, and
751	saying so is more useful than defending a wider claim. A reader who concludes that Indonesia should
752	codify definitions before compiling anything has read the evidence correctly, and that ordering is
753	what Section 4.4 recommends.
754	
755	## 5.3 What account supply does not fix
756	
757	The index's custodian has printed its own methodological limits, and account supply reaches none of
758	them. Four are conceded in the Report's own methods statement (Section 3.1.1, p. 34 [pdf 46]): an
759	assumption of linear and monotonic relationships, sensitivity to outliers, weights set on 2024 data
760	that *mungkin saja menjadi tidak relevan untuk tahun-tahun mendatang*, and a single weight structure
761	applied across a heterogeneous archipelago. Every one is a property of the estimator, whilst an
762	account changes the input. The first is worth dwelling on. The threshold relationship BAPPENAS
763	describes — the benefit of fishing intensity holding below a threshold whilst damage rises
764	exponentially above it — is exactly what an ecosystem account measures well, and better inputs
765	cannot reach a relationship the estimator represents linearly by design.
766	
767	Aggregation discards what the account was built to show. The constituent data items for indicators
768	19, 20 and 21 each end *per provinsi*, so an account compiled on a fine spatial grid loses its
769	within-province distribution at the moment it becomes an indicator. The account can tell a
770	provincial planner which reef complex is driving a change; the index can report only that a
771	percentage moved. That is a consequence of publishing the indicator without the grid `[ASSUMPTION]`,
772	so the remedy is an architecture in which both are released. The provincial boundary is
773	administrative and not ecological, and the standard leaves the accounting area to the compiler
774	(para. 2.12), so a reef complex straddling that line is divided by legal artefact. Accounts make the
775	division explicit and auditable; they do not make it ecologically meaningful.
776	
777	Net extent change cannot simply be inserted. The index states its own weighting rule: an indicator
778	with greater variability and strong correlation to others in its domain *akan secara alami
779	mendapatkan bobot yang lebih tinggi* (p. 29 [pdf 41]). A signed annual difference of a large, slowly
780	changing stock is low-variance by construction and can change sign between cycles, so it would
781	attract a small and unstable weight, and might meet the rule printed in the sub-pillar loop as
782	*Reversi nilai indikator (atau drop indikator) jika ditemukan relasi negatif* (p. 30 [pdf 42]).
783	Whether a sign-changing series would trigger it is not specified and is read here as an inference.
784	The mechanism is not peculiar to this index: under principal components, weighting corrects for
785	overlapping information rather than measuring importance, and where indicators are not correlated
786	weights cannot be estimated by the method at all (Nardo et al., 2008, §6.1). Net extent change
787	belongs beside the composite as a reported companion series, not inside it as a forty-fifth
788	indicator.
789	
790	Half the environment pillar measures something no ocean account should be built to measure. The
791	renewable-energy sub-pillar carries 50.0% of the pillar and contains no marine variable, being solar
792	and hydropower throughout (S4.3). No existing account can supply what it asks for, and building one
793	to do so would be an instrument error. The coverage claim stops at that boundary, and stopping is
794	what makes it credible.
795	
796	## 5.4 Loose coupling, and three readings that would lead elsewhere
797	
798	Where substitution is not warranted, and on the published maturity evidence it is not, the
799	arrangement that delivers the gain is loose coupling. It has three requirements: shared indicator
800	definitions at the cover-class thresholds the Juknis already prints, with the unit and denominator
801	rules the register leaves open settled with its custodian; a shared spatial unit that aggregates to
802	the province; and parallel publication of the account alongside the index. It is defined as much by
803	what it does not require — no institutional transfer, no change to the index's data pipeline, and no
804	dependency of a statutory index on an experimental compilation. Parallel publication is a combined
805	presentation (paras 14.56–14.57) and is the nearer term of the two, whilst sub-national compilation
806	follows from the accounting area being definable at a subnational administrative boundary (para.
807	2.12) and has been demonstrated at that scale elsewhere (Gacutan et al., 2022). Loose coupling has
808	the property that a compilation cycle missing the window fails without the index failing with it.
809	
810	The institutional division holds at the point of recommendation, where it is most at risk. BAPPENAS is
811	*pengampu* of the calculation whilst the constituent data are provincial (Juknis, p. 68 [pdf 78]), so
812	nothing here asks either institution to take on the other's mandate. The arrangement would have to be
813	settled by whichever body now holds the coordinating function, the team constituted under Kepmen KP
814	44/2023 having had its term expire with no renewal instrument located.
815	
816	Three readings of the same record would lead elsewhere. Rinaldi et al. (2025) construct an
817	alternative index on a human development index template, treating the problem as one of index design
818	rather than of the production system beneath it; on that reading the effort recommended here is
819	misdirected. Wuwung et al. (2024) locate the constraint on Indonesian ocean policy in institutional
820	fragmentation across mandates, which would make an inter-agency supply arrangement the hardest
821	rather than the cheapest of the conditions precedent. Ravallion (2012) defines a mashup index as a
822	composite for which theory and practice provide little guidance on design, leaving the producer free
823	to set an unusually large number of moving parts, and doubts the value added of such indices
824	relative to monitoring the components separately. The answer this record supports is statutory
825	rather than methodological: the IBEI is scored for every province annually under Perpres 12/2025, so
826	the dashboard alternative is not in BAPPENAS's gift, and what supplies the composite is a live
827	question whether or not an aggregate is the instrument a statistician would have chosen. No source
828	located by this study argues that accounts cannot serve as statistical infrastructure for a
829	composite index.
830	
831	## 5.5 Limitations
832	
833	Every share reported here is a nominal share of printed weight, not a measure of effective
834	influence. Nardo et al. (2008, §6.1) hold that under principal components, weighting is not a
835	measure of the theoretical importance of the associated indicator. Paruolo et al. (2013) measure
836	what each variable actually contributes to five composites, the human development index among them,
837	and find that because socio-economic variables are heteroscedastic and correlated, relative nominal
838	weights hardly ever match relative effective importance. The IBEI is a composite over correlated
839	provincial socio-economic variables with weights taken from first-component loadings, which is the
840	configuration in which that divergence was found. Becker et al. (2017) supply the test that would
841	settle it, a correlation-ratio measure of each variable's importance with confidence intervals. It
842	cannot be run here, because it requires the provincial variable matrix beneath the index, which is
843	unpublished `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. What survives the objection is the production-process case, which
844	never rested on the shares.
845	
846	Six further limitations bear on specific figures. Whether Tabel 4 to Tabel 6's percentages are raw
847	loadings or normalised contributions is not stated anywhere, and the reading adopted here — that
848	they are normalised, because all fifteen printed groups sum to 100.0 within rounding — is an
849	inference from the tables rather than a statement in the source (Section 3.5). Were it wrong, the
850	derived shares would lose their denominator rather than move. The printed aggregation formulae are
851	illegible in the PDF text layer at Juknis pp. 64–65 and Report pp. 31–32, so what is reconstructed
852	is the notation, at medium-high confidence, rather than the method. The per-indicator imputation
853	record is unpublished, which is the largest single unknown, because it determines how much of any
854	published provincial value is measurement and how much is prediction. The mapping is an assessment
855	against stated criteria rather than an independently verifiable quantity, which is why every row's
856	score and exclusion ground is deposited. Compilation cost is absent from the public record at every
857	site. And this study engaged no Indonesian agency, by project constraint, so the absence claims
858	above are conditions to live with rather than future work.
859	
860	# 6. Conclusion
861	
862	Ocean accounts are worth building for this index because of how the handful of indicators they touch
863	would be produced, and not because of how many indicators they could fill. The share an account would
864	itself supply is about 2.33% of the composite on the published weights, which the handbook prints at
865	every level. What separates the account routes into and out of reach
866	is content rather than compilation maturity: the routes compiled at pilot scale and the route compiled
867	nowhere bear on comparable shares of the index, 12.79% against 13.70%, yet no compiled account produces
868	the constituent data item the register asks for at any of those rows.
869	
870	The binding constraint on acting on this is not methodological. No compilation cost appears anywhere in
871	the record obtained for this study — no staff-day figure, no budget line, no imagery cost and no
872	field-survey cost, at any site `[EVIDENCE GAP]`. A province-by-province annual obligation is a scope
873	decision before it is a method decision. This study declines to estimate a cost, since an
874	order-of-magnitude range would be read as a figure.
875	
876	Two follow-on studies suggest themselves. The claim that indicators 19, 20 and 21 are definitionally
877	matched to account output is empirically testable today at exactly one site. Re-tabulating the Gili
878	Matra compilation to the index's own 50% to 100% cut would settle whether an account's extent and cover
879	layers yield the register's class-share form at all, and would generate the first real compilation-cost
880	observation. Its limits are set by the record itself: the coral and seagrass cover behind that
881	compilation was surveyed at stations rather than mapped per cell, the mangrove row is blocked on an
882	undocumented unit conversion, and a footprint of 2,980.84 ha supports no comparison against a published
883	provincial value. Extending the tabulation to NTB as a whole would test provincial resolution, and
884	requires the province-wide compilation that does not exist. The second output is practitioner-facing
885	rather than research: no published guidance sets out how a sub-national marine ecosystem account should
886	be compiled to serve a provincial indicator in an Indonesian setting. Both assume continued external
887	technical assistance to the Indonesian programme, which is an assumption rather than an established
888	condition `[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
889	
890	What would falsify the argument is worth stating, because a paper of this shape is otherwise
891	structurally unfalsifiable. If a single-province compilation reproduces the published provincial value
892	no better than the current secondary-publication route, or does so at a cost per province that an annual
893	national cycle cannot bear, then the production-process case fails on its own terms.
894	
895	Within the statutory boundary held throughout, what this study describes is a capacity gap under a
896	statutory obligation, in a country that has demonstrated, at one experimental pilot site, one instance
897	of the production process the obligation would require at scale.

1262	## S3.1 A national spatial standard
1263	
1264	Provincial account output is not comparable across provinces or across vintages until one minimum
1265	mapping unit and one basic spatial unit are adopted nationally. This is an act of codification,
1266	discharged once, requiring no new compilation.
1267	
1268	The standard requires an account to be consistent over time and with respect to concepts and
1269	classifications (United Nations et al., 2024, para. 1.60), and requires previous estimates to be
1270	revised for continuity of the time series wherever updated data, methods or classifications permit a
1271	reassessment of area (para. 4.17). Grid-based spatial units are the mechanism by which an account
1272	aggregates to any administrative boundary, conditional on the grid existing and being national.
1273	
1274	No adopted national marine grid or minimum mapping unit was found in any obtained Indonesian document
1275	`[EVIDENCE GAP]`. Rahayu et al. (2024), the only peer-reviewed Indonesian account source, state that a
1276	standardised grid has yet to be established, and the pilot literature records different resolutions at
1277	different sites — the case the rule exists to prevent. Grid choice changes the extent tabulation
1278	itself: mangrove error of 44.06% under a 25 m hierarchy scheme against 1.59% under 25 m dominance. Until
1279	a grid rule is adopted, the account's figure is a family of figures, and the index would be importing a
1280	methodological choice.
1281	
1282	## S3.2 A codified clipping rule
1283	
1284	Provincial marine authority extends to 12 nautical miles under Law 23/2014, cited here at second hand
1285	and not read at source `[EVIDENCE GAP]`, so an account reaching further must be clipped before it can
1286	yield a provincial number.
1287	
1288	The standard leaves the accounting area to the compiler (para. 2.12) and supplies no sub-national
1289	allocation rule for an asset spanning a boundary. The convention this study takes as necessary —
1290	allocation by physical extent within each side, with condition compiled per spatial unit rather than
1291	inherited whole — is a specification rather than a citation `[ASSUMPTION]`. Neither it nor the
1292	statutory line is codified in any account obtained.
1293	
1294	One consequence follows from the standard directly. Condition is not additive across areas, its
1295	variable account being an area-weighted arithmetic mean over the assets of an ecosystem type (para.
1296	5.54, p. 105). Because indicators 19, 20 and 21 ask for a ratio, a provincial value cannot be recovered
1297	by averaging district values, and a supplier would need to publish the denominator alongside the
1298	percentage for a provincial value to be recoverable.
1299	
1300	Clean nesting of cell boundaries across administrative, fisheries-management and marine-spatial-planning
1301	frames is asserted rather than evidenced `[ASSUMPTION]`, and forms part of this condition rather than a
1302	property that may be assumed.

1304	## S3.3 A repeated compilation inside the reporting window
1305	
1306	SIRUSA specifies reference period T-1 for all five mapped variables (19, 20, 21, 24, 27). The single
1307	verified Indonesian compilation closes at 2021 and was published the following year, a lag T-1 can
1308	accommodate. What is absent is the second vintage. One compilation, however timely, cannot meet a T-1
1309	requirement in any subsequent cycle, and no repeat compilation of the pilot sites was found
1310	`[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
1311	
1312	This condition is recurring rather than one-off. It is discharged by running the cycle again, not by a
1313	decision, and it survives the index's own inconsistent rendering of its reference periods
1314	`[ASSUMPTION]`. Sustaining a cycle is a resourcing question, and no compilation cost was located in the
1315	public record, so no condition in this register can be priced.

1317	## S3.4 A revision policy
1318	
1319	The standard already supplies the substance: revision of previous estimates for continuity of the
1320	series, recording no distinct revision entry but altering the opening, closing, addition and reduction
1321	entries themselves, with the size of a revision shown as the difference between the historical and the
1322	revised account for the same period (paras 4.17–4.18, p. 88).
1323	
1324	What is unwritten is the Indonesian instrument that would designate a baseline year and disclose where
1325	the latest extent data refer to an earlier period. The index's own principle of controlled adaptability
1326	presupposes that discipline on the supply side. No revision policy was found for any Indonesian ocean
1327	account output in the searched set `[EVIDENCE GAP]`.
1328	
1329	The consequence is specific. An account silently improved between vintages would move a province's
1330	index value with no ecosystem change behind it, and nothing in the published record would flag it. The
1331	instrument requires no new compilation: it is a statement of when a series is restated and how a
1332	provisional estimate is marked.

1336	Account output cannot become a durable input to an official index whilst it remains an unregistered
