## Filename years that are wrong

- `Rinaldi-2024-IBEI-indicators.pdf` is **2025** (received Nov 2024, accepted Jun 2025).
- `OceanPanel-2022-National-Accounting-for-the-Ocean.pdf` is **2020**, per its own suggested-citation block.
- `IDEEA-Defining-Complete-Sequence-National-Ocean-Accounts.pdf` cites itself as **2021**; the file was created 2023.
- `SEEA-EA-2021-final-2024ed.pdf` carries a **2024** imprint of the standard **adopted in 2021**. Cite the imprint, name the adoption year in text.

## Not citable, and why

- `ADB-Indonesia-blue-economy-key-policies.pdf` is a Danareksa Research Institute slide deck by R. P. Artha with no date, venue or publisher, and no ADB attribution anywhere in it. Its key slide is sourced to BAPPENAS (2021), which should be cited instead.
- `ASEAN-Blue-Wealth-Indonesia-snapshot.md` is an undated web snapshot with no named author; every figure in it is second-hand.
- `MFAT-2024-Indonesia-Green-Economy.pdf` has no personal author and two conflicting cover dates.
- `PermenKP-18-2025` is citable by its number and enactment date of 3 November 2025 only: the Berita Negara number and promulgation date render as unmapped glyphs in this copy and must not be supplied.
- `Bogor_Strategic_Action_Plan_2026` prints as published on 1 April 2026 but its PDF metadata still reads DRAFT. Verify status before relying on it.
- The Juknis and the IBEI Report are the objects of study, not prior literature. The three MMAF 2022 reports are labelled preliminary consultation drafts by GOAP and the two BPS 2023 decks are conference slides.

## The remaining hole

**No composite-indicator methodology literature exists in this project's document set.** There is no
OECD/JRC *Handbook on Constructing Composite Indicators*, no Ocean Health Index or comparable index
paper, and nothing on weighting-scheme sensitivity, PCA-derived weights, or imputation inside composite
indices. This matters at one specific point: the article's sharpest primary finding is the index's own
disclosure that an indicator observed in no province can still be predicted for every province, and
there is nothing in the set to situate that against the established literature on how imputation and
weighting choices propagate through composite indices. Until that is sourced from outside the folder,
the introduction cannot establish that the IBEI's construction is a recognised class of problem rather
than an idiosyncratic Indonesian one, which is the move that licenses the novelty claim. Section 8.3's
variance-weighting argument has the same exposure, resting on the index's own statement of the
mechanism with no methodological literature behind it.
