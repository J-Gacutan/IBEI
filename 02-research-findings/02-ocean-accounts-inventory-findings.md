# Indonesia Ocean Accounts — Inventory and Maturity

**Research executed:** 13 August 2026. All sources accessed 13 August 2026 unless noted.
**Maturity tags:** (a) published official statistics · (b) experimental / pilot compilation · (c) methodology under development · (d) announced, not started

---

## 1. Headline finding

Indonesia has compiled real ocean account tables, but none of them are official statistics. The entire evidentiary base sits at tag (b). The 2022 Gili Matra reports are described by GOAP itself as "preliminary consultation drafts"; the national output is a ministry dashboard (Neraca Sumber Daya Laut, NSDL) operated by MMAF, not a BPS statistical release. BPS-Statistics Indonesia has published no ocean or marine economy satellite account and no marine ecosystem account. There is therefore no ocean accounting entry in the official statistics stream at any spatial scale.

The distinction that matters downstream: **a dashboard field that renders a national number is not a published account with documented method, revision policy, and reference-year provenance.** Several NSDL modules populate at national and provincial scale, but the underlying compilation is not separately documented in any obtained publication.

## 3. Account-type maturity summary (one line each)

1. **Ecosystem extent** — (b). Only account type with a fully populated opening/addition–reduction/closing structure, proven at Gili Matra; national dashboard extent exists at 2018/2021 but its compilation is undocumented in any obtained publication.
2. **Ecosystem condition** — (b), and **weaker than extent**. Gili Matra condition was compiled from field methods (underwater photo transect for coral, Seagrass-Watch for seagrass, plot sampling for mangrove, plus satellite/laboratory biophysics), but the obtained material presents condition as maps and variable panels, **not as a normalised condition index against a stated reference condition**. No evidence was found of a SEEA-EA-compliant condition account with reference levels and indexed variables for any Indonesian site.
3. **Ecosystem service flow** — (b), physical quantification partial; service categories asserted (provisioning, regulating, cultural, supporting) but no supply–use table with named economic beneficiaries by sector was obtained.
4. **Monetary / valuation** — (b), and **not SNA-consistent**. Gili Matra values were derived by benefit transfer from Bohol (Philippines), the Mariana Islands (USA), Mexico, Gazi Bay (Kenya) and Bintuni Bay. Benefit transfer of total economic value is not the exchange-value basis SEEA-EA requires. BPS's own listed challenge is that standard valuation methods are not yet applied by relevant stakeholders; the April 2026 national NSDL workshop again recommended standardising ecosystem valuation.
5. **Ocean economy satellite account** — (c/d). Not compiled or published by BPS. Nearest existing outputs are the annual SDLP compendium, fisheries GDP inside the standard national accounts, and the dashboard's Neraca Ekonomi Kelautan module. BPS has demonstrated satellite-account capability elsewhere (Tourism Satellite Account 2016–2019), and a nine-cluster ocean sector classification has been discussed, but no correspondence table to KBLI and no provincial ocean-GDP disaggregation was found in any published BPS output.
6. **Social accounts** — (c). GOAP working group established 2024; methodology in the literature (Shellock et al. 2025, *Environmental Science & Policy* 173: 104221). No Indonesian social account compilation found.
7. **Governance accounts** — (b) but qualitative. The Gili Matra governance account is a structured statement of zoning rules (permitted gear by zone, permitted tourism activity by zone). It is a governance *register*, not a quantified account with stock–flow structure.

## 4. Provincial coverage matrix (RQ2.10)

The NSDL dashboard exposes a province selector listing **34 of Indonesia's 38 provinces**. Absent: **Papua Selatan, Papua Tengah, Papua Pegunungan, Papua Barat Daya** (the four provinces created in 2022; Papua Pegunungan is landlocked and would have no marine account in any case). Coverage is therefore 34/38 provinces as a *selectable spatial filter*, or 34/37 coastal provinces.

**This count must be read with care.** Selectability in the dashboard interface is not the same as a compiled, documented, published provincial account. The count of provinces for which a **documented compilation exists in an obtainable publication is 1** — Nusa Tenggara Barat, via Gili Matra (extent, condition, valuation, waste, governance) and Saleh Bay (extent, methodological).

## 5. MBSU methodology note (RQ2.7)

- **Definition and shape.** Indonesia uses rectangular grid cells, generated from the Ecosystem Accounting Area, joined to ecosystem extent layers.
- **Adopted resolution.** Gili Matra used **25 × 25 m**, producing 48,211 cells. No single national standard has been formally adopted in any obtained document.
- **Saleh Bay conclusion.** Rahayu et al. (2024) tested 10 × 10 m (36,346,207 cells) against 25 × 25 m (5,818,612 cells), and dominance (extent-based) against hierarchy (criteria-based) designation. **The dominance method was more accurate; the combination of larger grid with hierarchy designation was far cheaper computationally.** The paper's finding is that grid size and designation method materially change the resulting account tables, and that no standardised grid has been established internationally.
- **Nesting.** Underlying extent for seagrass and coral was derived from Sentinel-2 following BIG guidelines; mangrove from BIG 2021 data. GOAP describes the national dashboard's spatial frame as harmonised across Fisheries Management Areas, administrative areas, spatial zonations, and existing marine use permits (PKKPRL). No obtained document demonstrates cell-boundary nesting arithmetic, so clean nesting is **asserted, not evidenced**.

**Scaling target.** The stated ambition is extension to all Indonesian waters, with the accounts positioned to support the 30% marine conservation target by 2045. **No dated national coverage milestone was found in any obtained document.** Bogor's five strategic thrusts — data systems, blue economy finance, governance and political commitment, cross-country capacity, and extension to subnational level — are directional rather than scheduled.

**BPS statistical work programme.** No entry for ocean or marine ecosystem accounts was found. The BPS Deputy for Accounts and Statistical Analysis 2024 performance report lists an in-depth SEEA study among its activities, but the released account products are national, institutional, input–output and tourism satellite accounts. Ocean accounting is therefore institutionalised **at MMAF, not at the national statistical office** — a material constraint on any indicator requiring annual official reporting.

## 7. Lag and cost (RQ2.9)

- **Reference-year-to-publication lag, Gili Matra:** closing stock 2021 → draft report 2022 (~1 year to draft); to international presentation 2023; to national dashboard 2025.
- **National dashboard:** closing stock 2021 → public platform July 2025 = **approximately four years**.
- **Currency risk:** as of the access date the dashboard's ecosystem module still reports a 2021 closing stock. The most recent Indonesian ocean account closing year in the public domain is **2021, five years stale**. GOAP states the platform will be regularly updated from remote sensing and ground survey; no update has been observed in the extent module.
- **Cost:** **no documented per-site or per-spatial-unit resource requirement was found** — no staff-days, budget line, satellite data cost, or field survey cost. This is a genuine gap in the record, not an omission of search. The only cost signal obtained is computational: the Saleh Bay paper reports the 10 m grid at 36.3 million cells against 5.8 million at 25 m, a roughly six-fold processing burden for a single bay.
- **Institutional risk:** the 2025 independent evaluation of GOAP records a significant funding gap from March 2026 for the Secretariat that has provided Indonesia's technical support.

**Assessment:** on the observed record, these accounts cannot currently feed an annually-reported index. There is one closing year (2021) in the public domain, a four-year publication lag, no demonstrated revision cycle, and no compilation schedule in the national statistical work programme.

## 8. Figure verification note (RQ2.4 / method requirement 3)

**Confirmed against primary source** — BPS-Statistics Indonesia, *Ocean Accounts of Gili Meno, Ayer, Trawangan (Gili Matra) of Indonesia*, presented at the SEEA-EA Regional Training Workshop, Manila, 7 June 2023 (hosted on seea.un.org):

| Ecosystem | Opening 2015 (ha) | Change (ha) | Closing 2021 (ha) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coral reef | 183.34 | −18.16 | 165.08 |
| Seagrass | 56.50 | +8.43 | 64.93 |
| Mangrove | 13.53 | −7.38 | 6.15 |

All three prompt figures verified exactly. Source stated as BIG (2020), validated by 2021 field survey.

Also confirmed from the same source: 2021 ecosystem values of IDR 34,745 million (coral reef), IDR 5,578 million (seagrass), IDR 3,216 million (mangrove); solid waste generation 4.45 thousand tonnes in 2021, 56.38% organic, 25.86% recycled, 35.34% to landfill.

**Not verified.** The 2015 opening stock is attributed to a 2020 BIG dataset — the derivation of a 2015 extent from 2020 mapping is not explained in the obtained material. The Indonesia–Norway 2024–2028 period rests on journalistic reporting of a Statistics Norway interview; no primary agreement document was obtained. Provincial dashboard values could not be verified because the dashboard renders numbers client-side.

**Correction to the prompt's premise.** The national dashboard uses **2018 as opening and 2021 as closing**, not 2015/2021. Downstream work assuming a 2015 baseline outside Gili Matra would be assuming a series that does not exist.

## 11. Bottom line for downstream mapping

Any derivability rating that depends on Indonesian ocean accounts should be capped by three facts. Ecosystem **extent** is the only account type with a verified, fully structured compilation, and only for one MPA at one transition (2015–2021), with a national layer at 2018–2021 whose method is undocumented. Ecosystem **condition** has been observed and mapped but there is no evidence of a normalised, reference-condition-based condition account for any site — a "Full" derivability rating for habitat condition is not supported by the obtained record. And nothing in the system is **official statistics**: no BPS release, no work-programme entry, no revision policy, no demonstrated update since 2021.
